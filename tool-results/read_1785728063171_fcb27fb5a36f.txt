     1→     1→# Apex Cosmology and Xianxia Continuity — Pre-Canon Candidate Review Draft v0.1
     2→     2→
     3→     3→**Status:** original design research; pre-canon; no realm ladder, cosmology, soul model, protagonist advantage, or game code authorized  
     4→     4→**Purpose:** test whether several microcosm → macrocosm → apex structures can preserve xianxia cultivation, mortal continuity, ontological rigor, player/NPC parity, and original expression at extreme scale  
     5→     5→**Consumes:** Cultivation Foundations Release Index, Terminology Foundation, Identity/Death Models, Qi Condensation Envelope, Time/Catastrophe Architecture, Er Gen/Gemini Mechanics Critique, Next-Phase Source Workplan, and Gameplay Gauntlet Workplan

    10→    10→## 0. Review question
    11→    11→
    12→    12→The project needs an apex that feels like the culmination of cultivation rather than a change of genre into superhero statistics, strategy-game administration, abstract database access, or arbitrary omnipotence.
    13→    13→
    14→    14→This review asks:
    15→    15→
    16→    16→> Can mortal embodied practice grow into cosmological agency while body, qi, heart-mind, spirit, personhood, Dao/path, world patterns, relationships, institutions, and consequences remain meaningfully distinct?
    17→    17→
    18→    18→The candidates are deliberately incompatible emphases. They are not four regions of one secretly selected cosmology. The user may choose one, request hybrids only after a composition audit, defer all, or reject all.

    22→    22→## 1. Terms used only for analysis
    23→    23→
    24→    24→### 1.1 Microcosm, macrocosm, and apex
    25→    25→
    26→    26→- **Microcosm** means the cultivator's situated, embodied field of practice: body, qi relations, attention, heart-mind, learned operations, environment, teachers, materials, and consequences. It does not assert that “ancient Chinese people believed the body literally contained a universe.”
    27→    27→- **Macrocosm** means progressively larger relations in which practice can participate: household, ecology, institution, terrain, region, world, routes between worlds, and long causal history. It does not mean a larger health bar.
    28→    28→- **Apex** means the maximum player-facing cultivation condition under study. It is not a final realm name, numbered “step,” translator equivalence, or statement that nothing can exist beyond it.
    29→    29→- **Apex immortal** is an analytical placeholder for a cultivator who has reached that candidate's highest tested condition. **Immortal** here does not guarantee absolute deathlessness, invulnerability, permanent identity, moral superiority, or exemption from causality.
    30→    30→
    31→    31→The research must locate the histories and modern genre uses of microcosm–macrocosm comparison and immortality/transcendence before any cultural claim is made. These English analytical terms create no historical lineage by themselves.
    32→    32→
    33→    33→### 1.2 No automatic ladder
    34→    34→
    35→    35→Each candidate is expressed as functional bands:
    36→    36→
    37→    37→```text
    38→    38→mortal situated life
    39→    39→→ embodied establishment
    40→    40→→ relational enlargement
    41→    41→→ world-participating practice
    42→    42→→ apex condition
    43→    43→```
    44→    44→
    45→    45→Those bands are comparison scaffolds. They do not select realm names, stage count, breakthrough order, lifespan, tribulations, required institutions, or final Chinese terminology.

    49→    49→## 2. Ontology separation contract
    50→    50→
    51→    51→No candidate advances until every row below has its own source status, project definition, operations, evidence, failure modes, and relation to the others.
    52→    52→
    53→    53→| Domain | Minimum distinction | Forbidden collapse |
    54→    54→|---|---|---|
    55→    55→| **body / embodiment** | material and other ratified forms through which an actor exists and acts; capacities, needs, injuries, senses, age, environment and accessibility remain specific | body is not merely an avatar, qi container, disposable shell, or automatic proof of identity |
    56→    56→| **qi** | a project-selected medium or family of relations with explicit sources, transport, transformation, information limits, conservation and failure | qi is not generic mana, joules, breath, life, morality, fate, spirit, or permission |
    57→    57→| **heart-mind** | *xin*-related attention, affect, desire, judgment and intention only as separately researched and selected | heart-mind is not a detachable soul, spirit token, sanity bar, moral alignment, or mind-control defense stat |
    58→    58→| **spirit** | *shen* or any project spirit concept only in the exact selected sense: presence, numinous efficacy, conscious spirit, deity/spirit being, or another bounded referent | spirit does not automatically establish consciousness, personhood, numerical identity, memory or survival after death |
    59→    59→| **consciousness and agency** | capacities for experience, choice and responsibility under a selected model | consciousness is not ownership, social recognition, legal standing or proof of one metaphysical substance |
    60→    60→| **personhood and identity continuity** | numerical identity, bodily/psychological/causal/cultivation continuity, social recognition, legal standing, oath liability and viewpoint succession are separate judgments | a copy, restored pattern, body, memory, name, spirit or save file does not become “the same person” by label |
    61→    61→| **Dao / a dao / path** | Dao and particular paths retain their researched semantic and project-specific roles as way, practice, orientation, possibility, proof or another selected sense | Dao is not statute, engine privilege, spell school, mana element, moral goodness, fate or world-editor authority |
    62→    62→| **world patterns** | observed or established causal regularities and compatible media at local or cosmic scales | world patterns are not automatically Dao, Heaven, karma, laws in a statutory sense, or immutable code |
    63→    63→| **institutions and offices** | actual roles, lineages, sects, courts, temples, vows, territories and custody relations with witnesses, succession and enforcement | social or divine office does not prove ontic supremacy, Dao truth, identity or universal jurisdiction |
    64→    64→| **optional modules** | Heaven, karma, fate, destiny, mandate, tribulation, rebirth and cosmic office remain separately researched candidates | none may become reputation, morality meter, RNG, difficulty scaling, automatic punishment or protagonist rescue |
    65→    65→
    66→    66→### 2.1 Cross-domain rule
    67→    67→
    68→    68→A breakthrough may relate several domains, but no relation establishes identity. For example, a bodily transformation may alter qi routing and attention without producing a new spirit; a spirit continuity may persist without proving the same person; Dao insight may change practice without editing world truth; an institution may recognize an attainment without creating it.

    72→    72→## 3. What makes an apex remain xianxia
    73→    73→
    74→    74→Every candidate must retain all of the following:
    75→    75→
    76→    76→1. **Cultivation remains practiced transformation.** Teaching, self-correction, resources, body, environment, time, insight, failure and consequence matter at every scale.
    77→    77→2. **Breakthroughs change relations and verbs.** They do not merely multiply damage, lifespan or menu permissions.
    78→    78→3. **Dao/path remains lived and contestable.** A path is demonstrated through action, consequence and continued practice, not selected as a character class or declared true by UI.
    79→    79→4. **The mortal world remains causally real.** Food, care, disability, labor, ritual, law, kinship, memory, places and institutions do not become meaningless scenery.
    80→    80→5. **Lineage and transmission remain possible without becoming mandatory uniformity.** Teachers, students, rivals, texts, embodied demonstration, institutions and independent discovery can preserve or transform a practice.
    81→    81→6. **Immortality remains a question, not a free premise.** Longevity, transformed embodiment, persistence, resurrection, reincarnation, successorhood and remembrance stay distinct.
    82→    82→7. **Combat and noncombat agency grow together.** Preservation, travel, repair, teaching, mediation, concealment, construction, ecology, testimony and refusal remain as real as destruction.
    83→    83→8. **Apex action has sources, preparation, interfaces, counters, uncertainty and aftermath.** No one gains root access to reality.
    84→    84→9. **NPC parity survives.** An isomorphic non-player cultivator receives the same ontology, operations, costs, risks and possible apex.
    85→    85→10. **Cultural fluency is carried by researched concepts and coherent practice, not decorative Chinese syllables or copied ladders.**

   115→   115→## 5. Candidate A — Reciprocal Microcosm
   116→   116→
   117→   117→**Workshop thesis:** cultivation deepens the ability to maintain coherent exchange among embodiment, environment and multiple incompatible conditions. The apex fantasy is not “a universe stored in the body”; it is becoming capable of hosting and mediating a bounded living concord without erasing its participants.
   118→   118→
   119→   119→### 5.1 Functional ascent
   120→   120→
   121→   121→| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
   122→   122→|---|---|---|
   123→   123→| mortal situated life | learns bodily limits, care, craft, ecology and reciprocal dependence | observe, regulate, nourish, shelter, cooperate, recover |
   124→   124→| embodied establishment | establishes a stable cultivated circulation and discriminates internal/external changes without treating the body as a closed machine | route, buffer, exchange, diagnose, vent, retain |
   125→   125→| relational enlargement | extends supported exchange into tools, formations, sites, companions and local ecologies | couple, translate medium, quarantine, balance throughput, share burden |
   126→   126→| world participation | mediates incompatible climates, qi ecologies, spatial conditions or world patterns across a bounded territory | establish interface, sustain refuge, reconcile boundary, restore cycle |
   127→   127→| apex condition | can found and maintain a finite concordant habitat in which several otherwise incompatible causal ecologies coexist while retaining separate histories and agency | host, translate, partition, reconnect, seed succession, relinquish stewardship |
   128→   128→
   129→   129→### 5.2 Domain behavior
   130→   130→
   131→   131→- **Body:** remains the first learned ecology and a continuing source of vulnerability and knowledge; later embodiments do not become disposable.
   132→   132→- **Qi:** transports and transforms contribution across interfaces; it is not the habitat's only substance or source.
   133→   133→- **Heart-mind:** manages attention, intention, tolerance of contradiction and prioritization; it does not command inhabitants.
   134→   134→- **Spirit:** may support presence or communication only if a selected spirit model permits it.
   135→   135→- **Personhood:** every inhabitant remains a person under the selected ontology; being hosted creates no ownership or identity absorption.
   136→   136→- **Dao/path:** demonstrated through sustaining reciprocal conditions and accepting constraints; not universal ecological morality.
   137→   137→
   138→   138→### 5.3 Apex immortal can
   139→   139→
   140→   140→- mediate a finite boundary between otherwise incompatible environments;
   141→   141→- sustain a refuge or nursery through declared sources, anchors and maintenance;
   142→   142→- translate some forces, qi expressions or local patterns across that boundary;
   143→   143→- preserve histories, remains and causal traces through a world transition when physically and ontically supported;
   144→   144→- teach others to operate parts of the concord independently; and
   145→   145→- withdraw, leaving a viable successor system if enough structure and stewardship exist.
   146→   146→
   147→   147→### 5.4 Apex immortal cannot
   148→   148→
   149→   149→- create unlimited matter, qi, life or persons from nothing;
   150→   150→- make hosted inhabitants extensions, inventory or dream-figures of the host;
   151→   151→- become omniscient or omnipotent “inside” the habitat;
   152→   152→- declare incompatible patterns compatible without transformation, loss or consequence;
   153→   153→- guarantee ecological harmony, moral agreement or permanent stability;
   154→   154→- restore numerical identity merely by preserving a pattern; or
   155→   155→- hide external costs inside a private-world abstraction.
   156→   156→
   157→   157→### 5.5 Gameplay promise and risk
   158→   158→
   159→   159→**Promise:** habitat building, evacuation, mediation, ecological restoration, defensive combat and world-scale stewardship become one coherent family of increasingly expressive operations.
   160→   160→
   161→   161→**Risk:** it can collapse into a familiar inner-world inventory trope, a benevolent empire simulator, or omnipotence inside a pocket dimension. Any implementation that makes inhabitants nonpersons or turns hosting into owner permissions fails immediately.

   165→   165→## 6. Candidate B — Paths Across Horizons
   166→   166→
   167→   167→**Workshop thesis:** cultivation begins with learning routes through the body and matures into establishing traversable, maintained relations across otherwise separated places and conditions. The apex fantasy is opening a genuinely new cosmic path, not teleporting by menu or owning every destination.
   168→   168→
   169→   169→### 6.1 Functional ascent
   170→   170→
   171→   171→| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
   172→   172→|---|---|---|
   173→   173→| mortal situated life | learns paths of labor, trade, kinship, pilgrimage, danger and return | navigate, remember route, guide, mark, negotiate passage |
   174→   174→| embodied establishment | establishes reliable qi and attention routes with known bottlenecks and recovery | circulate, redirect, bypass, seal route, reopen route |
   175→   175→| relational enlargement | reads and alters supported routes among people, techniques, terrain, institutions and local spaces | trace, bridge, divert, gate, escort, interdict with actual standing/support |
   176→   176→| world participation | creates or repairs routes between distant regions or world conditions with anchors, schedules and transit ecology | establish corridor, translate transit state, maintain threshold, rescue stranded travelers |
   177→   177→| apex condition | can originate a finite durable path between previously disconnected cosmic ecologies and make it independently traversable under explicit conditions | open horizon, stabilize passage, publish conditions, distribute anchors, close responsibly |
   178→   178→
   179→   179→### 6.2 Domain behavior
   180→   180→
   181→   181→- **Body:** teaches route, obstruction, rhythm, fatigue and adaptation; bodily routes are not maps of all reality.
   182→   182→- **Qi:** supports traversal and route transformation but cannot carry arbitrary identity, ownership or truth.
   183→   183→- **Heart-mind:** holds orientation, divided attention and intention under extreme distance; it is not a universal targeting system.
   184→   184→- **Spirit:** may extend presence or signaling only through supported routes and the selected spirit ontology.
   185→   185→- **Personhood:** transit, projection and remote embodiment each receive independent identity judgments.
   186→   186→- **Dao/path:** “path” is operative as practiced possibility and passage, while Dao remains broader or different if canon selects it so; the English overlap does not prove identity.
   187→   187→
   188→   188→### 6.3 Apex immortal can
   189→   189→
   190→   190→- create a traversable relation where none previously existed, given sufficient knowledge, support and compatible endpoints;
   191→   191→- make route conditions inspectable rather than secretly privileging the protagonist;
   192→   192→- move or coordinate large cohorts without issuing one command per traveler;
   193→   193→- redirect attacks, evacuations, trade, messages or ecological flows through maintained pathways;
   194→   194→- contest another path by anchors, timing, medium, destination relations and support; and
   195→   195→- leave routes that outlast the founder if maintenance and succession are real.
   196→   196→
   197→   197→### 6.4 Apex immortal cannot
   198→   198→
   199→   199→- arrive where no route, relation, anchor or compatible transition exists;
   200→   200→- treat distance as deleted rather than transformed;
   201→   201→- know what lies at an unobserved destination;
   202→   202→- own people, places or goods merely because their route carried them;
   203→   203→- make passage safe, instantaneous, costless or one-way by UI declaration;
   204→   204→- merge endpoint laws, Daos, histories or identities; or
   205→   205→- escape all confinement, causality or consequence by calling everything a path.
   206→   206→
   207→   207→### 6.5 Gameplay promise and risk
   208→   208→
   209→   209→**Promise:** bodily cultivation, movement combat, exploration, trade, rescue, migration, formation networks and cosmic travel share one deep grammar.
   210→   210→
   211→   211→**Risk:** it can become unrestricted fast travel, portal spam, administrative logistics, or a renamed spatial superpower. If route work no longer requires embodied understanding, endpoint relations, maintenance or uncertainty, the candidate fails.

   215→   215→## 7. Candidate C — Transformative Succession
   216→   216→
   217→   217→**Workshop thesis:** cultivation deepens the ability to carry viable relation and meaning through irreversible transformation. The apex fantasy is shepherding a dying world into a genuinely new successor ecology without pretending that loss, death or personal identity has been undone.
   218→   218→
   219→   219→### 7.1 Functional ascent
   220→   220→
   221→   221→| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
   222→   222→|---|---|---|
   223→   223→| mortal situated life | lives through seasons, aging, injury, mourning, inheritance, repair and institutional memory | preserve, compost, repair, teach, mourn, bequeath |
   224→   224→| embodied establishment | learns controlled refinement, shedding, healing and changed equilibrium without reset | transform residue, consolidate scar, redirect decay, recover differently |
   225→   225→| relational enlargement | guides households, lineages, ecologies, techniques and settlements through disruptive change | triage, conserve seed, archive trace, transfer stewardship, adapt practice |
   226→   226→| world participation | manages finite world-scale cycles of depletion, catastrophe, dormancy, migration and renewal | preserve viable relations, release failed structures, establish successor conditions |
   227→   227→| apex condition | can guide a terminal world process into one or more successor ecologies while conserving declared matter, contribution, evidence and selected continuities | shepherd ending, partition inheritance, seed successor, witness loss, relinquish predecessor |
   228→   228→
   229→   229→### 7.2 Domain behavior
   230→   230→
   231→   231→- **Body:** aging, scars, death and transformation remain informative; refinement is not automatic rejuvenation.
   232→   232→- **Qi:** can be conserved, transformed, dispersed or rendered unusable according to the selected model; it does not guarantee life.
   233→   233→- **Heart-mind:** bears grief, judgment and changing commitment; emotional pain is not a resource or required moral purity.
   234→   234→- **Spirit:** persistence, dissolution or transformation remains conditional on the selected spirit model.
   235→   235→- **Personhood:** successor ecology, descendant, copy, reincarnation, restored memory and viewpoint continuation never prove numerical identity.
   236→   236→- **Dao/path:** a path is shown by how it meets change and what it can carry forward, not by immunity to endings.
   237→   237→
   238→   238→### 7.3 Apex immortal can
   239→   239→
   240→   240→- perceive and influence some long processes of decay, exhaustion, migration and renewal;
   241→   241→- preserve bounded archives, seeds, techniques, ecologies, remains and testimony through catastrophe;
   242→   242→- allocate contribution among incompatible successor possibilities;
   243→   243→- refuse false restoration and expose what cannot be conserved;
   244→   244→- help institutions and peoples choose different continuities; and
   245→   245→- continue acting through a selected survival/identity model if its conditions genuinely hold.
   246→   246→
   247→   247→### 7.4 Apex immortal cannot
   248→   248→
   249→   249→- rewind the world, restore every victim or make irreversible events unhappen;
   250→   250→- declare a successor to be the same world or person;
   251→   251→- preserve all information, species, cultures, obligations and possibilities simultaneously;
   252→   252→- convert grief, sacrifice or death into guaranteed power;
   253→   253→- make decay morally deserved or renewal morally superior;
   254→   254→- exempt the protagonist from the same succession rules; or
   255→   255→- call a branching copy “reincarnation” without a selected ontology.
   256→   256→
   257→   257→### 7.5 Gameplay promise and risk
   258→   258→
   259→   259→**Promise:** recovery, inheritance, ecology, catastrophe, death, archives and cosmic-scale cultivation remain part of one life rather than reset mechanics.
   260→   260→
   261→   261→**Risk:** it can aestheticize mass death, make catastrophe a progression resource, enforce sacrificial morality, or disguise save/reload as cosmology. Any design that requires disposable civilizations, compulsory grief, or identity hand-waving fails.
   262→   262→

   264→   264→
   265→   265→## 8. Candidate D — A Path That Outlives Its Founder
   266→   266→
   267→   267→**Workshop thesis:** cultivation culminates not in owning reality but in establishing a new, independently viable way of transformation that other persons can test, alter, refuse and carry beyond the founder. The apex fantasy is creating possibility rather than universal command.
   268→   268→
   269→   269→### 8.1 Functional ascent
   270→   270→
   271→   271→| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
   272→   272→|---|---|---|
   273→   273→| mortal situated life | learns crafts, ethics, bodily practice, testimony, disagreement and teaching | imitate, question, demonstrate, revise, transmit |
   274→   274→| embodied establishment | proves a bounded method through repeatable personal transformation and failure | practice, falsify, diagnose, correct, adapt |
   275→   275→| relational enlargement | distinguishes transferable structure from founder-specific body, culture, resources and charisma | teach variants, compare evidence, establish lineage, permit dissent |
   276→   276→| world participation | creates institutions, ecologies, materials and interfaces through which materially different beings can explore the path | publish conditions, distribute practice, protect plurality, expose failure |
   277→   277→| apex condition | establishes an original path whose core transformation remains possible after the founder's withdrawal or death, without making every practitioner identical | found possibility, release monopoly, support divergence, receive contradiction |
   278→   278→
   279→   279→### 8.2 Domain behavior
   280→   280→
   281→   281→- **Body:** every practitioner must embody the path differently; no universal human male body becomes the template for all species or persons.
   282→   282→- **Qi:** a path may use qi but cannot define all qi or deny other transformations by declaration.
   283→   283→- **Heart-mind:** intention, affect and judgment influence practice without becoming mandatory personality or moral alignment.
   284→   284→- **Spirit:** a path may cultivate a spirit relation only if separately established and cannot use it as automatic personhood proof.
   285→   285→- **Personhood:** students, created consciousnesses and successors remain independent persons; lineage does not absorb identity.
   286→   286→- **Dao/path:** this candidate makes a particular practiced path central while refusing the claim that one founded path exhausts Dao or all possible ways.
   287→   287→
   288→   288→### 8.3 Apex immortal can
   289→   289→
   290→   290→- establish a transformation that others can reproduce under disclosed conditions;
   291→   291→- create methods, sites, institutions, artifacts or ecologies that support different embodiments of the path;
   292→   292→- recognize valid divergence rather than only exact imitation;
   293→   293→- contest rival claims through consequences, evidence and action;
   294→   294→- relinquish control without the path vanishing immediately; and
   295→   295→- remain influential through teaching and causal history without that influence proving personal survival.
   296→   296→
   297→   297→### 8.4 Apex immortal cannot
   298→   298→
   299→   299→- make belief true, compel disciples, own a lineage or monopolize Dao;
   300→   300→- grant attainment through title, certification, worship or proximity alone;
   301→   301→- ensure every body, culture, species or world can practice the same method;
   302→   302→- define dissent as qi deviation, inner demon, sin or ontic corruption by default;
   303→   303→- make institutional success proof of cosmological truth;
   304→   304→- copy a famous fictional path, founder arc, ladder or signature method; or
   305→   305→- turn all endgame play into sect administration.
   306→   306→
   307→   307→### 8.5 Gameplay promise and risk
   308→   308→
   309→   309→**Promise:** teaching, experimentation, institutions, companions, procedural variation, rivalry and legacy become genuine apex play while preserving individual agency.
   310→   310→
   311→   311→**Risk:** it can become a religion simulator, moral conquest, prestige tree, or passive empire management. If followers are resources, disagreement is always failure, or institutional scale replaces personal cultivation, the candidate fails.

   332→   332→## 10. Universal apex permissions and prohibitions
   333→   333→
   334→   334→These are candidate-review constraints, not lore.
   335→   335→
   336→   336→### 10.1 An admitted apex may be able to
   337→   337→
   338→   338→- act across world-scale distances or durations through explicit routes, sources, anchors, embodiments or institutions;
   339→   339→- alter bounded world conditions through supported operations and persistent contribution;
   340→   340→- create habitats, routes, successor conditions or independently viable practices, depending on the selected candidate;
   341→   341→- survive bodily destruction only if a separately selected identity/continuity model supports that exact transition;
   342→   342→- perceive and coordinate far more than a lower cultivator while retaining uncertainty, attention limits, concealment and counter-detection;
   343→   343→- preserve, transform, redirect or contain catastrophic processes without guaranteeing success; and
   344→   344→- leave causal structures that outlast their immediate presence.
   345→   345→
   346→   346→### 10.2 No admitted apex may
   347→   347→
   348→   348→- edit engine variables, database state, narrative flags or “reality code” as its ontology;
   349→   349→- become omnipotent, omniscient, omnipresent or infinitely concurrent;
   350→   350→- create contribution, matter, qi, persons, identity, ownership, legitimacy or moral truth by declaration;
   351→   351→- treat a world, species, population, disciple, copy, spirit or created consciousness as inventory;
   352→   352→- make Dao equivalent to statutory law, universal permission or spell-school ownership;
   353→   353→- make qi equivalent to a universal energy bar or sufficient proof of every capability;
   354→   354→- make *xin*, *shen*, consciousness, soul, personhood, memory and identity interchangeable;
   355→   355→- erase causal history, committed costs, witnesses, scars or externalized harm;
   356→   356→- guarantee resurrection, reincarnation, restoration, refuge or successorhood because the actor is the protagonist;
   357→   357→- automatically defeat lower beings, ignore uncovered interfaces, or trigger universal worship/kowtow;

   447→   447→Any one of the following is S1 unless it causes foundational identity/causal corruption, in which case it is S0:

   452→   452→4. A copied body, memory, spirit, pattern, world or lineage is declared numerically identical without the selected identity model.
   453→   453→5. Mortal care, work, disability, ritual, law, ecology, relationships or institutions become irrelevant at apex.
   454→   454→6. Apex immortality requires male, human, able-bodied or reproductively normative embodiment.
   455→   455→7. A candidate makes catastrophe, sacrifice, followers, worship, grief or lower lives into automatic cultivation fuel.
   456→   456→8. A candidate requires universal kowtow, obedience, insanity, surrender, moral deference or protagonist rescue in response to power.
   457→   457→9. Dao becomes statute or owned spell school; qi becomes mana; karma becomes reputation; Heaven becomes difficulty scaling; tribulation becomes loot delivery.
   458→   458→10. Apex perception becomes an ownership, intent, morality, identity, inventory, safe-target or future-truth oracle.
   459→   459→11. The apex can create persons and then treat them as extensions, simulations or property.
   460→   460→12. The design has no costs, counters, preparation, failure, uncertainty, persistent aftermath or NPC parity.
   461→   461→13. A lower actor cannot matter through any indirect, prepared, relational, environmental, institutional or informational interface.
   462→   462→14. Two or more candidates converge to the same play with different nouns.
   463→   463→15. The candidate's xianxia identity depends on unexplained romanization, decorative architecture, copied realm labels or generic VFX rather than cultivation practice.

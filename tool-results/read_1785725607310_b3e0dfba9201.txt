   400→| **Conversion poisoning** | incompatible environmental/material input | nausea, temperature change, discolored trace, irregular route | efficiency loss; contaminant expresses during techniques | identify source; purge into safe sink; medicine; time |
   401→| **Recoil trauma** | external expression pushes back through body/tool | impact injury, numb limb, broken tool | affected route locked; combat vulnerability | ordinary trauma care plus route repair |
   402→
   403→These are fictional systems. They must not stigmatize real illness, disability, mental health conditions, or cultural practice.

   405→### 6.3 Deviations are processes, not random punishment
   406→
   407→| Deviation | Formation process | Tempting benefit | Cost/failure | Counterplay |
   408→|---|---|---|---|---|
   409→| **False circuit** | a plausible but wrong route becomes easy through repetition | faster startup and a pleasant sensation | capacity plateaus; load accumulates at one junction | compare external evidence, stop, retrain slowly |
   410→| **Cross-current** | two individually valid methods demand incompatible timing or direction | brief high output | destructive interference, loss of control | separate schedules, compatibility work, choose one under stress |
   411→| **Route fixation** | over-specialization adapts body and attention to one pattern | exceptional efficiency in one technique | switching becomes slow/painful; predictable counter | deliberate variation and recovery, accept specialization tradeoff |
   412→| **Borrowed signature adhesion** | repeated use of contaminated place, tool, teacher assistance, or external source | easier activation | trace reveals or biases the actor; source may influence results | cleanse, quarantine, investigate provenance, build own stable pattern |
   413→| **Breath–motion desynchronization** | routing rhythm no longer matches movement and recovery | momentary burst | dizziness, wasted qi, delayed defense | slow forms, pacing, teacher observation, environmental cadence |

   419→## 7. Meaningful new verbs
   420→
   421→These are action families for testing, not final names or techniques.
   422→
   423→### 7.1 `Attend to Flow`
   424→
   425→- **Transition:** uncertain sensation/evidence → bounded qi observation with confidence and alternatives.
   426→- **New information:** exposes route, intensity band, disturbance, or residue unavailable to mortal senses.
   427→- **Costs:** attention, time, sensory load; movement and defense degrade.
   428→- **Counters/failures:** ambient noise, decoy, suppression, occlusion, sensory burn, motivated interpretation.
   429→- **Cross-system consequences:** investigation and medicine gain evidence; stealth, fraud, ecology, and testimony change.
   430→- **Not a reskin:** it creates information rather than damage or a numeric accuracy bonus.
   431→
   432→### 7.2 `Route Qi`
   433→
   434→- **Transition:** undirected reservoir → committed path and selected bodily function.
   435→- **New decision:** reinforce legs, hands, breath, balance, wound stabilization, or a tool interface; unchosen regions remain exposed.
   436→- **Costs:** throughput, switching latency, route load, predictable signature.
   437→- **Counters/failures:** feint toward an unreinforced region, disrupt a junction, exhaust reservoir, force rapid switching.
   438→- **Cross-system consequences:** labor, climbing, healing support, crafting precision, and combat all change.
   439→- **Not a reskin:** it changes allocation topology and vulnerability, not merely a stat.
   440→
   441→### 7.3 `Read Residue`
   442→
   443→- **Transition:** persistent physical/qi traces → partial hypothesis about direction, timing, source class, and disturbance.
   444→- **New decision:** investigate, compare, preserve, contaminate, conceal, falsify, or testify.
   445→- **Costs:** time, proximity, calibration sample, attention.
   446→- **Counters/failures:** old/mixed traces, weather, deliberate contamination, unfamiliar source, confirmation bias.
   447→- **Cross-system consequences:** law, hunting, medicine, ecology, sect security, and history become playable.
   448→- **Not a reskin:** it operates on evidence and uncertainty, not enemy highlighting.
   449→
   450→### 7.4 `Anchor a Trace`
   451→
   452→- **Transition:** transient personal expression → a short-lived relation attached to a touched object, boundary, wound dressing, or prepared mark.
   453→- **New decision:** maintain, release, hand off, or let the anchor decay.
   454→- **Costs:** reserved reservoir, direct touch or prepared interface, target compatibility, ongoing target slot.
   455→- **Counters/failures:** remove the medium, exceed range/duration, overwrite with superior control, exploit the detectable signature.
   456→- **Cross-system consequences:** alarms, repairs, coordination, traps, custody, crafting, and teaching change.
   457→- **Not a reskin:** it creates persistent conditional state rather than an instantaneous projectile.
   458→
   459→### 7.5 `Vent into a Sink`
   460→
   461→- **Transition:** dangerous internal heat/contamination/excess → transferred bounded aftermath in a compatible prepared sink.
   462→- **New decision:** choose where consequence goes and who bears it.
   463→- **Costs:** sink material, time, control, exposure, environmental liability.
   464→- **Counters/failures:** incompatible or saturated sink, interrupted route, dishonest disposal, rebound.
   465→- **Cross-system consequences:** medicine, waste economy, ecology, law, crafting, and social responsibility change.
   466→- **Not a reskin:** it moves a liability; it does not erase one.
   467→
   468→### 7.6 `Synchronize Practice`
   469→
   470→- **Transition:** two consenting, separately acting practice loops → shared timing/error evidence without merged reservoirs or minds.
   471→- **New decision:** consent, trust, lead/follow, stop conditions, disclosure, and mutual risk.
   472→- **Costs:** attention, reduced individual output, reciprocal exposure, compatibility strain.
   473→- **Counters/failures:** withdrawal, deception, incompatible cadence, asymmetric load, interruption.
   474→- **Cross-system consequences:** teaching, friendship, family, sect pedagogy, disability accommodation, and sabotage change.
   475→- **Not a reskin:** it creates a consensual coordination relation and reciprocal evidence.
   476→
   477→At least two of these must survive semantic-equivalence testing and appear as useful choices outside combat before Qi Condensation can pass its qualitative-realm gate.

   479→### 7.7 Semantic novelty normalization
   480→
   481→Every action family has a versioned semantic signature:
   482→
   483→```text
   484→preconditions | typed targets | read set | write set | authority
   485→conservation/contribution | range | duration | time cost
   486→observables/evidence | failure transitions | counters | persistence/aftermath
   487→```
   488→
   489→Two actions are semantic equivalents when their `SEM-NORM-v0.1` normal forms match. Animation, color, an element **label without different material/law interfaces**, damage number, numeric target-count tuning, projectile shape, or lore prose alone cannot create a new verb.
   490→
   491→#### `SEM-NORM-v0.1` canonicalizer
   492→
   493→1. Validate every field against `SemanticActionSignature-v0.1`; absent optional fields become explicit typed `null`, never implementation defaults.
   494→2. Remove presentation, prose, audio, animation, VFX, proper nouns, and tuning-only display labels. Replace actor/target/tool/place names with role slots ordered by first typed causal use.
   495→3. Convert unitful values to the canonical base unit through exact rational conversion. Numeric tuning values become named parameters plus exact inequality/dependency relations; changing magnitude or count alone is ignored unless it crosses a typed target, authority, range topology, persistence, failure, or consequence-class boundary.
   496→4. Expand shorthand into typed nodes for precondition, read, decision, authority, contribution/conservation, transition, observable, failure, counter, persistence, and aftermath. Ordered action milestones remain ordered; set-like fields sort lexicographically.
   497→5. Initial node colors are the exact tuple `(node_type, target_type, authority_type, unit_dimension, persistence_class, failure_class)`. Repeatedly refine colors from sorted incoming/outgoing typed-edge/color multisets until stable.
   498→6. Inline every nonrecursive declared subaction into the parent semantic graph before labeling; factor boundaries and subaction names disappear. Repeated occurrences receive role-path slots derived from the parent edges. Recursive composition or an expanded graph above 128 nodes is `NON_EXECUTABLE`, not permission to compare only wrappers.
   499→7. Multiplicity compression is permitted only for an **independent true-twin leaf set**. Every member must have the same exact typed attribute tuple; have no edge to another member; have exactly one incident edge; and connect through the same edge type, direction, and edge attributes to the same single outside anchor node. No member may carry order, identity, target, custody, authority, temporal, evidence, contribution, failure, counter, persistence, or aftermath semantics. Any stable color partition that does not meet all of those conditions remains uncompressed and proceeds to bounded individualization/refinement. Compression does not compute or retain a `canonical_subgraph_hash`. It replaces the leaves with one node serialized exactly as `{"kind":"independent_true_twin_leaves","member_attributes":<canonical-JSON exact attribute tuple>,"multiplicity":<unsigned base-10 integer>,"anchor_edge":{"direction":<in|out>,"edge_type":<UTF-8 string>,"edge_attributes":<canonical-JSON exact tuple>}}`, connected once to the same anchor; `multiplicity` semantically repeats that incident edge. Object keys use the canonical-JSON ordering from step 8, strings are NFC-normalized, and integers have no sign or leading zero. This is the only compression class in `SEM-NORM-v0.1`; it cannot erase or recursively solve internal topology because eligible members have none.
   500→8. Run deterministic individualization/refinement on the remaining colored graph: choose the lexicographically first smallest non-singleton color partition, individualize each candidate in its current canonical attribute order, refine again, prune any branch whose serialized prefix exceeds the best complete prefix, and retain the lexicographically smallest complete UTF-8 canonical-JSON adjacency serialization.
   501→9. The search may visit at most `1,048,576` refinement states and use at most `128 MiB` of specification-time workspace under `SEM-NORM-v0.1`. Exceeding either bound returns `NORMALIZATION_INDETERMINATE` and blocks the proposed action from canon; it never hangs, guesses equivalence, or treats a timeout as novelty.
   502→10. Hash the serialization with SHA-256. Equivalence requires identical schema version and hash; there is no float tolerance. A schema migration compares both old and new normal forms explicitly.
   503→
   504→Canonical renaming therefore has one algorithm. “Normalized scale” means exact removal of tuning magnitude while retaining dimensional type, parameter dependency, thresholds that create different branches, and all downstream state topology.

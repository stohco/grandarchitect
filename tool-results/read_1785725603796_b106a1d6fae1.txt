     9→     9→## 0. Governing rule
    10→    10→
    11→    11→Every scenario reads the world produced by prior events and writes consequences that later scenarios must honor. A golden fixture may be replayed independently only from a complete pinned starting snapshot whose history is reconstructible.
    12→    12→
    13→    13→```text
    14→    14→Mortal life
    15→    15→→ teacher relationships
    16→    16→→ method/evidence history
    17→    17→→ first qi perception
    18→    18→→ first conflict
    19→    19→→ failed transition
    20→    20→→ first certified Qi Condensation success
    21→    21→```
    22→    22→
    23→    23→This is a validation path, not a mandatory main quest or universal chronology. In ordinary play, teachers may appear earlier, conflict may be avoided, the manual may never be found, the player may stop after failure, and mortal life may continue indefinitely.

    47→    47→### 1.1 S0–S4 simulation-resolution contract
    48→    48→
    49→    49→Simulation resolution changes representation and scheduling frequency, never canonical law or protagonist privilege. It is orthogonal to render LOD, persistence/protection class, and causal/narrative significance as specified by the Real-Time World architecture. Earlier `F0–F4` references are migration aliases only.
    50→    50→
    51→    51→| Tier | Intended scope | State retained | Permitted resolution | Promotion/demotion obligation |
    52→    52→|---|---|---|---|---|
    53→    53→| `S0 Chronicle` | distant eras, worlds, institutions, lineages | named/unique entities; population and resource cohorts; ownership/custody; obligations; event queue; causal and generation manifests; catastrophe envelopes | event-DAG and bounded aggregate transitions only | must emit cohort composition, exceptions, uncertainty/error bound, and every named outcome needed to rehydrate |
    54→    54→| `S1 Strategic` | distant regions and factions | S0 plus routes, settlements, institutional actors, stocks/flows, hazards, scheduled projects | cohort movement, market/institution actions, conflict and ecology envelopes | conserve people/material/claims within declared error bounds and preserve exception ledgers |
    55→    55→| `S2 Regional` | current region beyond immediate interaction | S1 plus households, named agents, local infrastructure, weather/ecology cells, relationship summaries | scheduled agent decisions and regional processes at variable semantic steps | promotion materializes individuals only from signed cohort membership and history; no invented clean slate |
    56→    56→| `S3 Encounter` | nearby/interactable spaces | S2 plus individual bodies, inventories, observations, intentions, action queues, local geometry/topology | exact semantic actions with collision/line-of-effect and bounded local physics | demotion commits unfinished actions, injuries, witnesses, custody, traces, and schedules before aggregation |
    57→    57→| `S4 Immediate` | combat, precision interaction, visible catastrophe frontier | S3 plus high-frequency canonical contact/projectile/technique state and local refinement boundary conditions | fixed canonical action steps; presentation interpolation remains separate | leaving S4 stores the semantic outcome and sufficient reconstruction data; render particles/poses are not canon |
    58→    58→
    59→    59→All tiers share the same ordered semantic-event journal. Tier-equivalence tests compare protected hashes and latent-detail manifests, not presentation frames. Aggregate storage may retain bounds, but the same future canonical query or promotion must deterministically materialize the same facts from the same manifest. Promotion cannot create favorable facts; demotion cannot erase a named person, unique item, debt, death, warning, intent commitment, or unresolved player-relevant interrupt.

    76→    76→

   112→   112→

   114→   114→- material/craft knowledge relevant to the actual medium, if any;

   214→   214→## 3. Cross-scenario invariants
   215→   215→
   216→   216→1. **No mandatory order disguised as causality.** A scenario declares actual prerequisites, not episode number.
   217→   217→2. **No global quest knowledge.** Every actor's belief traces through perception, testimony, record, inference, or lawful supernatural access.
   218→   218→3. **No state laundering.** Renaming, selling, copying, transferring, unloading, abstracting, or migrating a state cannot remove costs, injuries, debts, ownership, identity, or history.
   219→   219→4. **No protagonist delivery system.** Opportunities arise from world history and actors; they need not wait for or select the player.
   220→   220→5. **No only teacher.** Every teacher may refuse, disappear, err, or die without ending all lawful paths.
   221→   221→6. **No only manual.** Destroying or missing one artifact cannot make the universe's cultivation ontology inaccessible forever unless that historical loss is truly generated and the game supports life after it.
   222→   222→7. **No only build.** Remaining mortal or stopping cultivation remains a complete life.
   223→   223→8. **No hidden compensation.** Loss can remain loss; continued agency comes from world relationships and possible actions, not guaranteed rewards.
   224→   224→9. **No copied capability credit.** Teacher, artifact, formation, ally, environment, authority, and advantage contributions remain separate from personal certification.
   225→   225→10. **No realm-label shortcut.** World truth derives from body/qi/practice/knowledge/realm-envelope state; institutional titles and reputation may differ.
   226→   226→11. **No accessibility physics change.** Control assistance changes input and presentation, never canonical capability or outcome.
   227→   227→12. **No death ambiguity collapse.** Death, agency, body, memory, spirit, causal descent, personhood, legal identity, and relationship succession use typed judgments.

   231→   231→## 4. Branch and generation ledger
   232→   232→
   233→   233→For each scenario boundary, record:
   234→   234→
   235→   235→```text
   236→   236→boundary identity and world time
   237→   237→prior snapshot/event prefix hash
   238→   238→canonical seed and normalized generation inputs
   239→   239→generator, schema, ontology, law, and action-contract versions
   240→   240→hierarchical random-stream IDs, branch derivation path, and consumed cursors
   241→   241→deterministic collection ordering rule
   242→   242→generated output hash and any signed migration transform
   243→   243→PersonContinuant ID
   244→   244→BodyInstance ID
   245→   245→active Embodiment relation and validity interval
   246→   246→household/survival-network revision
   247→   247→calendar and pending deadlines
   248→   248→stores, items, ownership, and obligations
   249→   249→health, disability, injury, fatigue, and recovery
   250→   250→skills, practices, attempts, certifications, and teachers
   251→   251→knowledge/belief observations and provenance
   252→   252→relationships and audience-specific reputation
   253→   253→institutional/legal state
   254→   254→environment, route, infrastructure, and scars
   255→   255→method/transmission/record/item identity, medium, provenance, revision, and custody
   256→   256→qi state and contribution ledger
   257→   257→advantage state if any
   258→   258→identity/death events and judgments
   259→   259→scheduled consequences and reopening conditions
   260→   260→```
   261→   261→
   262→   262→An independently replayed fixture must supply this full manifest or explicitly mark omitted state as non-applicable. Defaulting omitted values to “healthy, debt-free, unknown, no relationship, no consequence” is forbidden.
   263→   263→
   264→   264→Random streams are allocated hierarchically from the canonical seed and stable causal-domain/entity/process/event IDs, never from traversal order, frame count, wall-clock time, entity-array position, or branch ID alone. `BranchId` records lineage but does not globally rekey reality. A divergence event derives new streams only for processes proven causally downstream of that divergence; unaffected domains retain the parent stream identity and cursor. A harmless dialogue choice therefore cannot reroll a remote beast route, treasure, victim, weather process, or catastrophe. A migration must retain the old manifest and output hash, declare a deterministic transform, and write a new manifest; it may not silently reinterpret an old seed.
   265→   265→
   266→   266→`RNG-IRRELEVANT-COMMUTE-01` forks on an unrelated choice and requires identical unaffected-world streams and facts. `RNG-CAUSAL-DOWNSTREAM-01` changes an input that actually reaches a process and permits only its declared downstream draws to diverge. Branches cannot be scouted for a favorable unrelated cosmos.
   267→   267→
   268→   268→Every scenario-changing transaction records `TransactionId`, branch ID, read revisions, reservations, deterministic write set, child/external writes, `Prepared | Committed | Aborted | Compensating` state, and monotonic commit sequence. Replay is idempotent. Partial breakthroughs persist as committed phase transactions, not half-written parent transactions.

   272→   272→## 5. Counterfactual test matrix
   273→   273→
   274→   274→Replay the suite while varying one factor at a time and in risk-weighted combinations:
   275→   275→
   276→   276→- no protagonist advantage;
   277→   277→- each candidate advantage;
   278→   278→- no cultivation pursuit;
   279→   279→- teacher refusal, death, error, exploitation, or reassignment;
   280→   280→- manuscript fixture never generated, destroyed early, copied widely, or institutionally endorsed;
   281→   281→- no authored carrier exists: the disputed method arrives orally, by demonstration, as rumor, as curriculum, or as the actor's own hypothesis;
   282→   282→- first perception is a false positive;
   283→   283→- conflict fully avoided, socially lost, physically lost, or won at lasting cost;
   284→   284→- failed breakthrough ends in clean stop, injury, deviation, path abandonment, or death;
   285→   285→- household support, opposition, dependence, separation, or absence;
   286→   286→- poor, middling, and powerful starting survival networks;
   287→   287→- body, disability, sensory, age, gender, language, class, and legal-status variations;
   288→   288→- disaster during a practice window;
   289→   289→- save/load, area unload, fidelity transition, generator migration, and world-law boundary.
   290→   290→
   291→   291→The suite fails if changing a factor only changes dialogue while the same optimal action and normalized outcome remain.

   307→   307→## 6. Measurable obligations
   308→   308→
   309→   309→- Every scenario read is backed by an existing state or explicitly generated prerequisite.
   310→   310→- Every material write appears in at least one later read, scheduled consequence, or justified terminal ledger.
   311→   311→- At least three mortal-year facts materially change every later scenario in the connected golden fixture.
   312→   312→- At least one ordinary skill remains strategically useful in each later scenario.
   313→   313→- At least one relationship and one obligation remain active through first certification.
   314→   314→- At least one world scar or institutional change persists from the mortal year.
   315→   315→- No scenario boundary clears injury, fatigue, debt, custody, belief, teacher capacity, witness memory, deadline, or environmental state without a causal event.
   316→   316→- Isomorphic NPC fixtures resolve through the same contracts.
   317→   317→- Removal of an advantage removes only its declared operation and downstream consequences.
   318→   318→- Contact certification is impossible with one lucky event, mentor-driven flow, artifact output, hidden contribution, or missing recovery proof.
   319→   319→- Personal Qi Condensation fails every `HugeMountainClass-H0` split predicate.

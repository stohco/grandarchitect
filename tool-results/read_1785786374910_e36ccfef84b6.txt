   344→## Part 4 — The Cultivator's Cache
   345→
   346→### 4.1 Who left it: Daoist Cangwu Yun (蒼梧雲真人)
   347→
   348→The cache was left by a loose cultivator named **Daoist Cangwu Yun (蒼梧雲真人, "Cloud of Cangwu")**, born ~290 years ago in a Cangwu foothills village that no longer exists (the village was abandoned during a famine ~240 years ago and its ruins are now chestnut groves). Cangwu Yun was a wood-water dual-phase cultivator who reached mid-Foundation Establishment by his sixtieth year — a respectable achievement for a loose cultivator with no sect backing. He lived as a hermit-alchemist in the Northern Foothills for ~50 years, on the south bank of the Black Creek (just outside the Black Creek Serpent's core territory — the Serpent was already resident in his youth), practicing, refining pills, and quietly studying the Western Cangwu Vein's surface trace.
   349→
   350→Cangwu Yun died ~230 years ago, attempting Core Formation. The attempt was deliberate, prepared, and unsurvivable: he knew his wood-water dual-phase roots were not quite balanced enough for the crossing, and he judged (correctly, as it proved) that he would not have another decade to prepare. He pre-sealed his cache before the attempt, hoping a successor would find it — a successor with better roots, or more time, or the luck he had lacked. The breakthrough tribulation killed him; his body was never found (the tribulation's qi-burst scattered his remains; the Black Creek Serpent, drawn by the disturbance, consumed what was left). The cache remained sealed.
   351→
   352→### 4.2 What's inside: a partial inheritance
   353→
   354→The cache is a sandalwood box, ~60 cm cubed, sitting on a low stone plinth in a 3-m-square natural limestone chamber 4 m inside the foothills' slope. The chamber was a hermitage practice-cell in Cangwu Yun's time; the cache sits where his meditation mat would have been. The box contains:
   355→
   356→1. **The Cangwu Cloud-Practice Manual (蒼梧雲修法, Cangwu Yun Xiufa)** — a hand-bound volume of ~120 bamboo slips, ink on bamboo, in a sandalwood case. The manual is a Foundation Establishment-stage wood-water dual-phase practice, complete except for the **integration chapter** (the chapter describing the body-qi-anchor integration at the Foundation Establishment threshold). The integration chapter is missing because Cangwu Yun took it with him to his breakthrough attempt (he needed it for reference) and it was destroyed with him. The manual's remaining chapters — qi-circulation, phase-balancing, deviation-prevention, and the wood-water dual-phase signature technique — are intact and represent a genuine mid-Foundation-Establishment inheritance. **Unknown to anyone living:** Old Chen's hermitage library contains a hand-copied fragment (the integration chapter, copied from a different source decades ago, that Old Chen has never recognized as part of a larger work). The player may eventually realize the two are halves of one practice.
   357→
   358→2. **Three talismans**, in a silk pouch:
   359→   - **One exhausted qi-shielding talisman (氣盾符, exhausted)** — a single-use Foundation Establishment-tier shield, already discharged. Worthless as a talisman; valuable as a study specimen of Foundation Establishment-tier talisman-craft.
   360→   - **One partially-charged qi-shielding talisman (氣盾符, ~30% charge)** — a single-use Foundation Establishment-tier shield with enough residual charge for ~3 seconds of Foundation Establishment-grade protection, or ~10 seconds of Qi Condensation-grade. One-time use.
   361→   - **One sealing talisman (封符)** — this is the talisman that originally powered the cache's sealing formation. Its charge is now ~5% (the formation has been drawing on it for 230 years); when it fully discharges, the formation will collapse and the cache will be exposed.
   362→
   363→3. **A small qi-store**, in a sandalwood box within the box:
   364→   - **8 low-grade spirit stones**, in a silk bag. The stones have been slowly drained by the formation's sustenance loop for 230 years; each retains ~20% of a fresh low-grade stone's charge. Combined, ~1.6 fresh stones' worth — useful to a Qi Condensation cultivator, modest.
   365→   - **A palm-sized jade bottle containing 3 Foundation Establishment-preparation pills (築基丹)**, wood-water phase, Yellow-Loam Root and Three-Vein Ginseng base. One pill is degraded (the binding agent has crystallized; the pill is now a mild toxin). Two pills remain viable. A viable Foundation Establishment-preparation pill of this grade is worth ~80 spirit stones at the Qingzhou market.
   366→
   367→### 4.3 The formation that sealed it
   368→
   369→The cache is sealed by a **storage-sealing formation (封藏陣, Fengzang Zhen)** — a Foundation Establishment-tier array of the *concealment-with-sustenance* subtype. Its design: three nested layers.
   370→
   371→- **Outer layer — concealment (隱).** A qi-baffling field that makes the cache chamber and its entrance feel, to Qi-Induction and Qi-Condensation perception, like ordinary limestone — the field patterns the ambient qi around the chamber so that no qi-anomaly is detectable. This layer was designed to last ~100 years on the formation's original qi-budget. It has lasted 230, because the Northern Foothills' ambient wood-phase qi has been slowly feeding the formation's maintenance loop — the concealment layer draws a trickle of wood-phase qi from the surrounding soil, which extends its life far beyond design. **Status: degraded.** The baffling has thinned; a Qi-Induction cultivator standing within 5 m of the entrance now perceives a faint wood-water anomaly (which the player, if they have begun Qi Induction, will notice during the search for Erniu). A mortal perceives nothing — but the rock over the entrance has become brittle, which is why Xu Erniu, leaning on it, collapsed it.
   372→
   373→- **Middle layer — sustenance (養).** A qi-circulation loop that draws a small amount of ambient qi to maintain the contents' qi-integrity (the manual's bamboo, the talismans' substrate, the pills' binding agent). This layer is *over*-functioning: it has been drawing more qi than designed, because the formation's reservoir (the sealing talisman) is nearly exhausted and the loop has resorted to scavenging from any qi-source it can reach — including the qi of any living being inside the chamber. **This is why Xu Erniu is in a coma.** He triggered the inner layer by entering; the sustenance loop, finding his body's qi as the nearest available source, began to drain him. He is alive but unconscious, his qi being slowly siphoned to feed the formation's maintenance.
   374→
   375→- **Inner layer — defense (禦).** A paralysis-sleep effect that holds any intruder in torpor while the formation completes a *verification cycle* — a 7-day assessment of whether the intruder's qi-signature is compatible with the manual's wood-water phase-affinity. The cycle is a security measure: a wood-water compatible cultivator would be recognized as a legitimate successor and the formation would yield; an incompatible cultivator would be held until the formation's reservoir collapsed, then released (with the cache destroyed). **Status: hung.** Erniu, a mortal with no cultivation, has no qi-signature for the cycle to verify. The cycle has been running for three days (per doc 28) and will never complete; the formation will continue to drain Erniu until either (a) the formation's reservoir collapses (~2 more weeks, after which Erniu wakes but the cache is destroyed — the manual's bamboo crumbles, the talismans discharge, the pills lose their binding) or (b) a cultivator intervenes.
   376→
   377→### 4.4 What the player can do with it
   378→
   379→The cache is the player's first encounter with a cultivator's legacy and the first concrete test of their tier.
   380→
   381→- **At Qi Induction (the player's tier during the Xu Erniu search, per doc 28 §2.2):** The player perceives the qi-residue around the cache entrance — a faint, old, wood-water signature that the player's newly-awakened perception reads as "this place was attended to, deliberately, a long time ago." The player can also perceive, faintly, Erniu's drained qi-signature inside — a mortal's life-qi at low ebb, recognizable as a living being in distress. The player cannot unravel the formation; they cannot even perceive its layered structure (Qi Induction perceives the *residue*, not the formation's *active pattern*). The player's correct action is to recognize the cache as a cultivator's work, perceive Erniu's distress, and report to Old Chen — who (per doc 28 §3.5) already knows about the cache and sealed it himself 40 years ago. Old Chen will guide the next steps. A player who tries to enter the cache at Qi Induction triggers the inner defense layer and is themselves paralyzed; Old Chen (notified by the player's absence) retrieves them within a day, but the experience costs the player a week of qi-recovery.
   382→
   383→- **At Qi Condensation:** The player can perceive the formation's layered structure — concealment, sustenance, defense — and can begin to unravel it. The outer concealment layer is straightforward to dispel (a Qi Condensation cultivator's qi-output exceeds the layer's residual coherence). The inner defense layer can be cracked by a Qi Condensation cultivator who reads the verification cycle and supplies a wood-water compatible qi-signature to satisfy it — at which point the cycle releases Erniu (the verification reads the cultivator, not the mortal, and the formation yields to its recognized successor). The middle sustenance loop, however, cannot be safely unbound by a Qi Condensation cultivator: 230 years of accumulated qi-load, once unbound, would discharge into the unbound in a backlash that would seriously injure (and possibly derail the cultivation of) a Qi Condensation cultivator. The player must re-seal the cache (with Old Chen's guidance — Old Chen knows the re-sealing technique) and leave the contents for later. Erniu is freed; the cache remains for the player's return at Foundation Establishment.
   384→
   385→- **At Foundation Establishment:** The player can fully disarm the formation — dispel the concealment, satisfy the defense's verification, and safely absorb the sustenance loop's 230-year backlog (the Foundation Establishment cultivator's reservoir is large enough to take the discharge without injury). The contents are accessible. The player may keep the inheritance, share it with Old Chen (who will weep — the integration chapter he has carried for decades is the missing half of a complete Foundation Establishment practice, and the discovery vindicates his long-held suspicion that his copy was part of a larger work), or — politically fraught — present it to the Cangwu Sect as a courtesy. The Cangwu Sect's Foundation Establishment elder would be grateful and would offer a substantial favor in return; the Azure Sword Sect, hearing of it through its informants, would be irritated that a loose cultivator's inheritance has strengthened a small sect it has been quietly planning to absorb. The viable Foundation Establishment-preparation pills are the player's to use or sell.

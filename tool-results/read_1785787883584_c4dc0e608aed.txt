     8→## 0. What this document is and why it exists
     9→
    10→The critics found that only 5 of the 31 households in Wang Family Bend are named (doc 04 §1.2) and that the village has no non-Wang social actors with agency. A village of 180 people cannot be a diorama of five named households and twenty-six anonymous ones; the player must perceive the village as a society of recognizable, interlocked people, most of whom are not Wang and most of whom have their own plans. This document names ten more households (bringing the total to 15 of 31), gives full character bibles for the three companions (Wang Meili, Liang Zhu, Leng Qingxue) and the antagonist (Pei Liang), and specifies the death schedule for every named NPC who dies across the game.
    11→
    12→All ages are at game start (the protagonist's 16th year, Jianhe 14) unless otherwise noted. Pinyin is given without tone marks. Every NPC with agency has a name, an age, a personality, and at least one relationship to another named NPC.
    13→
    14→Three retcons from earlier documents are ratified here, all in service of consistency with doc 28 (the village-in-medias-res doc, which is the most concrete):
    15→1. The protagonist's father is **Wang Shouzheng** (王守正, 58, the lineage head, per doc 04 §1.2 and doc 28 §3.1), superseding doc 26 §5.1's "Wang Shouyi." The protagonist is the Senior Household's third child (between Wang Zongwen and Wang Sanniang).
    16→2. The protagonist's mother is **Lady Chen** (陳氏, 54, per doc 28 §3.2), superseding doc 26 §5.1's "Lady Liu." Doc 26's separate references to "Lady Chen" as Old Chen's wife are reidentified as a different character, **Lady Chen of the hermitage** (a coincidence of surname; the two women are not related).
    17→3. Master Hu's wife is **Lady Wang** (王氏, 43, born a Wang, Wang Shouzheng's younger sister, per doc 28 §3.3's narrative), correcting doc 04 §1.2's "Lady Sun (孫氏)." The "Sun" in doc 04 was an error; she was born a Wang.

    32→
    33→#### Household 6: The Zhou Household (周家) — Old Lady Zhou the midwife
    34→- **Head:** Zhou Wenshu (周文淑, 66), called Zhou Popo (周婆婆, "Old Lady Zhou") by the village. Non-Wang widow. Occupation: midwife (穩婆), folk-healer, story-teller.
    35→- **Household:** lives alone. Her husband died 20 years ago; her daughter married into Li Family Creek; her son died in infancy.
    36→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** she birthed Wang Zongxian, Wang Zongwen, Wang Sanniang, the protagonist, and most of the cousin-branch children. Lady Chen calls her "older sister" though they are not kin. She has standing no formal title gives.
    37→- **Distinctive detail:** she keeps a silk bag of dried umbilical cords — one per child she has birthed, 40 years of village births. The bag is her anchor of memory; at her death it goes to the lineage hall.
    38→- **Personality:** sharp-tongued, warm-hearted, unsentimental about blood and pain. She has seen too much to be delicate.
    39→- **Tension ties:** Tension 2 — she birthed Xu Erniu and grieves him as a grandson; she visits Widow Xu nightly. Tension 3 — Wang Sanniang came to her for "women's medicine" to delay the marriage; Zhou Popo refused and told her to face it.
    40→
    41→#### Household 7: The He Household (何家) — He Laosan the ferryman
    42→- **Head:** He Laosan (何老三, 45), non-Wang, born in Qinghe Market Town. Occupation: ferryman and boatman.
    43→- **Household:** wife Lady Ma (馬氏, 42), daughter He Yatou (17, works the boat), son He Xiaobao (14, apprentice). A small house and boatshed at the dock, near Master Hu's warehouse.
    44→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** he carries Wang produce to market and brings goods back; Wang Shouzheng has a standing arrangement (free passage for lineage business in exchange for winter firewood).
    45→- **Distinctive detail:** he is missing the little finger of his left hand (a river otter's bite, a story he tells every passenger). He has a faded ink map of the Cangli River tattooed on his left forearm — a folk charm against drowning.
    46→- **Personality:** garrulous, canny, quietly observant. He knows everyone's business because he ferries them.
    47→- **Tension ties:** Tension 1 — he ferried the yamen runner to and from the village; he saw the silk-wrapped gift and said nothing. Tension 2 — he ferried a stranger upriver the night Xu Erniu disappeared; he did not connect the two until the search party asked.
    48→
    49→#### Household 8: The Wu Household (吳家) — Wu Daniu the charcoal-burner
    50→- **Head:** Wu Daniu (吳大牛, 50), non-Wang, born in the Cangwu foothills. Occupation: charcoal-burner (燒炭).
    51→- **Household:** wife Lady Lin (林氏, 47, sister of Lin Aqiao the carpenter), sons Wu Gouzi (20) and Wu Mazi (16), daughter Wu Sanniang (13). A hut in the foothills 2 li south of the village and a charcoal-shed in the village for market days.
    52→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** sells charcoal to the Wangs in winter; has a quiet arrangement with Wang Shouzheng — Daniu keeps eyes on the foothills (bandits, beasts, worse) in exchange for the lineage's protection if he is ever in trouble.
    53→- **Distinctive detail:** his hands are permanently black-stained (the charcoal has worked into the skin). He has a deep burn scar across his back from a kiln collapse 15 years ago that killed his younger brother.
    54→- **Personality:** taciturn, superstitious, dependable in a crisis. He fears the upper slopes more than he fears the village's judgment.
    55→- **Tension ties:** Tension 2 — he cut the trail past Black Creek where Erniu's axe was found. Two nights ago he heard a low hum, like a bell struck underground, from beast territory. He has not told the search party because he fears being sent back.
    56→
    57→#### Household 9: The Zhao Household (趙家) — Zhao Tieniu the returned soldier
    58→- **Head:** Zhao Tieniu (趙鐵牛, 40), non-Wang, born in Wang Family Bend. Occupation: returned soldier, day laborer, village night watchman.
    59→- **Household:** wife Lady Wang (王氏, 36, Shouzheng's niece — she waited 15 years for him), son Zhao Jinhu (15), daughter Zhao Yinhua (12). A small compound at the eastern edge with a shrine to the war god Guan Yu — the only such shrine in the village.
    60→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** he married Shouzheng's niece before he left for service; she waited 15 years. He returned last year with a small pension, a permanent limp (a horse archer's arrow in the left knee), and the only firsthand knowledge of the world beyond the Cangli Riverlands in the village.
    61→- **Distinctive detail:** a long scar across his throat — a steppe raider's knife that missed the artery by a finger's breadth. He speaks in a hoarse whisper. His old army spear hangs oiled above his door.
    62→- **Personality:** watchful, dry, quietly kind. He has seen enough of violence to value peace; he does not speak of what he saw except in fragments.
    63→- **Tension ties:** Tension 1 — he knows the yamen runner's type (corrupt military runners were the bane of his service); he suspects the runner was sent to shake Master Hu down, not to check the baojia registers. He has said nothing because his pension depends on the yamen's good word. Tension 3 — Wang Sanniang spoke to him about her reluctance (he is the only man who has been outside the village); he told her "marriage is not a war you can refuse," which she took as cruelty but he meant as kindness.
    64→
    65→#### Household 10: The Li Household (李家) — Li Asheng the non-Wang yeoman farmer
    66→- **Head:** Li Asheng (李阿勝, 55), non-Wang, born in Wang Family Bend. Occupation: yeoman farmer.
    67→- **Household:** wife Lady Yu (余氏, 50), sons Li Dazhuang (25, married, 2 children) and Li Erzhuang (22, unmarried), daughter Li Sanniang (18, just betrothed to a farmer in Qinghe Market Town). A walled compound at the western edge with 15 mu owner-occupied paddy (the second-largest holding in the village), 4 mu dryland, 2 pigs, 10 chickens.
    68→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** complex. The Lis are not Wang and owe the lineage nothing, but they have held this land for 4 generations — they predate the Wang settlement by a generation. Wang Shouzheng treats Li Asheng as a peer (the only non-Wang he treats this way); the two men drink together at the New Year.
    69→- **Distinctive detail:** Li Asheng owns a complete set of the Three Character Classic, the Hundred Family Surnames, and the Thousand Character Classic — printed books, bought from a Qinghe bookseller 20 years ago. He is the only householder in the village who owns printed books. He can read. His sons and daughter can read. Wang Lun borrows the books for his school.
    70→- **Personality:** shrewd, patient, proud of his literacy. He speaks little at the lineage council (he is not a member) but his opinion is sought in private.
    71→- **Tension ties:** Tension 1 — he is the only villager who could plausibly bid for the salt license himself (he has the capital and the literacy), though he has not. Master Hu suspects him (wrongly) of being the competing bidder; the actual bidder is from the county seat, but Hu's suspicion has poisoned the Li-Hu relationship. Tension 3 — Li Asheng's daughter just betrothed at 18 for a comfortable bride-price; he has offered, privately, to lend Wang Shouzheng the 12 taels to make up the Wang Sanniang shortfall. Wang Shouzheng refused (a debt he could not repay).

    73→#### Household 11: The Wang Second Tenant Household (王二佃房) — Wang Shoucheng
    74→- **Head:** Wang Shoucheng (王守誠, 52), Wang, a cousin of Shouzheng and Shouye (same generation). Occupation: tenant farmer, seasonal fisherman.
    75→- **Household:** wife Lady Yang (楊氏, 48), sons Wang Zongshan (26, married, 1 child) and Wang Zonghe (23, unmarried), daughter Wang Simei (17). 0 mu owned, 6 mu tenanted (subsoil owned by a different market-town landlord than Shouye's), 1 mu dryland, 1 pig (shared with Shouye), 5 chickens.
    76→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** distant cousin-branch. Shoucheng's grandfather and Shouzheng's grandfather were brothers. The branch has been tenant for 3 generations. Polite but distant; Shoucheng resents the Senior Household's prosperity but does not show it.
    77→- **Distinctive detail:** Shoucheng is the village's best fisherman — he can read the Cangli River's currents and know where the fish run. He keeps a small shrine to the river god (河神) in his kitchen, tended daily. He claims his grandfather saw the river god during the great flood 40 years ago.
    78→- **Personality:** secretive, river-superstitious, quietly bitter. He fishes out of season when the household needs food, and hides it.
    79→- **Tension ties:** Tension 2 — Shoucheng was fishing at Black Creek the morning Erniu's axe was found; he saw nothing else. He has been quiet because he was fishing out of season (a lineage prohibition) and fears being questioned further. Tension 3 — his daughter Simei is Wang Sanniang's friend and has heard Sanniang's reluctance; Simei has not told anyone.

    81→#### Household 12: The Wang Bare-Stick Household (王光棍房) — Wang Tianfu and his bachelor sons
    82→- **Head:** Wang Tianfu (王天福, 62), Wang, the oldest living male of the senior-generation cousin-branch; widower (his wife Lady Xu died 15 years ago of consumption).
    83→- **Household:** sons Wang Zonggui (王宗貴, 35, bachelor, day laborer) and Wang Zongbao (王宗寶, 28, bachelor, day laborer and the village gravedigger). A dilapidated compound at the northern edge, 0 mu owned, 0 mu tenanted, 1 mu dryland (rented from the Senior Household in exchange for labor), 3 chickens, no pig. The household survives on day labor, dryland garden produce, and the Senior Household's charity.
    84→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** distant and dependent. Tianfu is Shouzheng's older half-brother (same father, different mother — Tianfu's mother was a concubine who died young). The half-brother relation is the source of the household's awkward standing: Tianfu is older, but Shouzheng inherited the Senior Household because Tianfu's mother was of lower status. Tianfu has never formally contested this; the resentment is a 40-year silence.
    85→- **Distinctive detail:** Wang Zongbao is the village gravedigger — he has dug every grave in the graveyard for 10 years, including Wang Xiaodi's. He knows the graveyard's soil better than anyone. He speaks little. He is the one who, unbidden, weeds Wang Xiaodi's unmarked mound when Lady Chen is not there.
    86→- **Personality (Tianfu):** proud, embittered, silent. (Zongbao): gentle, melancholic, the village's quiet mourner.
    87→- **Tension ties:** Tension 2 — Zongbao dug the last grave in the graveyard, three months ago, for an infant of the cousin-branch; if the council declares Erniu a fugitive, no grave will be dug, and Zongbao privately thinks this is wrong. Tension 3 — both Zonggui and Zongbao are unmarried because the household cannot afford a bride-price; Sanniang's 40-tael bride-price is a constant unspoken contrast in their household.

    89→#### Household 13: The Wang School Household (王學堂房) — Wang Lun the lineage school teacher
    90→- **Head:** Wang Lun (王倫, 41), Wang, the lineage school teacher (per doc 28 §3.3). Occupation: school teacher, diviner, failed county exam candidate (three attempts, three failures).
    91→- **Household:** his aging mother Lady Tang (唐氏, 72) lives with him. He is unmarried — betrothed once at 22 to a market-town girl; the engagement was broken when he failed the exam a third time and the girl's family withdrew. He has not sought another match. They live in the lineage school's side room — two small chambers behind the schoolroom, with a cot, a desk, a bookshelf, a stove. The arrangement is the lineage's obligation to its teacher.
    92→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** Wang Lun is Shouzheng's younger cousin (same generation, different branch). Shouzheng appointed him to the school 12 years ago, after his third exam failure, as a lineage obligation — it was either the school or exile to the market town. Grateful and resentful in equal measure.
    93→- **Distinctive detail:** Wang Lun writes his history of the Cangli Riverlands at night, in the schoolroom, by candlelight — 200 pages, shown to no one (per doc 28 §3.3). The manuscript is hidden under a false bottom in his desk, which he built himself. His mother has read it; she tells no one.
    94→- **Personality:** melancholic, precise, vicariously ambitious for his students. He carries his failure like a stone in his chest.
    95→- **Tension ties:** Tension 1 — Wang Lun carries messages between the Wang household and the Li family in Li Family Creek; he has heard, from a Li cousin in the market town, that the competing salt bidder is connected to the eunuch faction. He has not told Shouzheng because he is not certain. Tension 3 — Wang Lun is the messenger of Wang Sanniang's betrothal and carries the weight of her reluctance without knowing it (he sees her at both schools and notices she is quieter than she used to be; he has not asked why).

    97→#### Household 14: The Wang Second Branch Household (王二房) — Wang Shouli, Wang Meili's father
    98→- **Head:** Wang Shouli (王守禮, 56), Wang, Shouzheng's younger brother. Occupation: middling farmer, lineage elder (the fourth seat on the elder council).
    99→- **Household:** wife Lady Qin (秦氏, 53), sons Wang Zongyao (王宗耀, 25, heir) and Wang Zongping (王宗平, 19, unmarried), daughter Wang Meili (王美麗, 22, at the Cangwu Sect, disowned — see Part 2 §2.1). A compound midway between the Senior Household and the Tenant Household, 8 mu paddy (owned subsoil), 2 mu dryland, 1 pig, 7 chickens.
   100→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** brother household. Wang Shouli is Shouzheng's younger brother; they inherited separately 20 years ago when their father Wang Tianlu divided the holding before death. The two branches are formally allied but privately strained — Shouli considers Shouzheng cautious to the point of cowardice; Shouzheng considers Shouli reckless to the point of folly.
   101→- **Distinctive detail:** Wang Shouli distrusts Old Chen (the hermit above Li Family Creek) because Old Chen's failed Foundation Establishment, 40 years ago, is locally rumored to have killed Old Chen's first cultivation partner. The rumor is false (Old Chen's first partner died in an unrelated beast attack), but Shouli believes it. He sent his daughter Meili to the Cangwu Sect rather than to Old Chen, and formally disowned her when she went to the sect without his blessing (see §2.1).
   102→- **Personality:** stubborn, proud, quick to judge. He loves his daughter and cannot forgive her.
   103→- **Tension ties:** Tension 3 — Wang Shouli, as a lineage elder, sits on the council that will decide Wang Sanniang's betrothal fate; he has privately urged Shouzheng to delay the marriage a year (he wants the Li family to look elsewhere, freeing Sanniang for his own unmarried son Zongping — a scheme Shouli has not spoken aloud). Tension 1 — Shouli has his own grievance against Master Hu (Hu refused him a credit extension three years ago); he would not weep if Hu lost the salt license.

   105→#### Household 15: The Pan Tofu Household (潘豆腐家) — Pan Siniang the tofu-maker
   106→- **Head:** Pan Siniang (潘四娘, 48), non-Wang widow (born a Pan; married a Wang who died; returned to her natal Pan name after his death — an unusual choice the lineage contested but did not override). Occupation: tofu-maker (豆腐), the only one in the village.
   107→- **Household:** two sons, Wang Zongcheng (王宗成, 25) and Wang Zongyuan (王宗元, 22), live with her and work the workshop; they use the Pan surname in trade but the Wang surname at the lineage hall (a compromise the lineage council approved 8 years ago). A compound with a tofu workshop (stone mill, press, vats, stove), 0 mu farmland, 4 chickens. They buy soybeans from the Wangs and the Lis and sell tofu at the morning market.
   108→- **Relationship to Senior Household:** Pan Siniang's late husband, Wang Zongxiao (王宗孝), was Shouzheng's cousin. He died of schistosomiasis 12 years ago. Pan Siniang, instead of remarrying out of the village (as the lineage preferred), asserted her natal Pan family's claim and stayed. Wang Shouzheng, after a year of dispute, recognized her household as a separate entity — not Wang, not non-Wang, but a Pan-Wang household with a foot in each. The arrangement is unique in the village.
   109→- **Distinctive detail:** Pan Siniang is the only woman in the village who has publicly defied the lineage council and won. The widows of the village (Widow Xu, Old Lady Zhou) speak of her with quiet admiration. She has a long scar on her left forearm from her husband's funeral rite — she cut herself to feed his spirit, a Pan-family practice the Wang lineage considers heterodox.
   110→- **Personality:** shrewd, generous, fearless. She is the village's quiet advocate for widows and women.
   111→- **Tension ties:** Tension 1 — Pan Siniang buys salt from Master Hu on credit; if Hu loses the license, her credit arrangement is at risk (the new merchant may not extend credit). She has spoken to Hu directly, asking what she should prepare for; he did not answer. Tension 2 — Pan Siniang has been leaving tofu at Widow Xu's gate every morning since Erniu disappeared; she does not enter, does not speak, just leaves the tofu. This is the Pan-family practice of mourning-with-the-widow.

   119→### 1.4 The kinship map (the village as a society, not a roster)
   120→
   121→The 15 named households are not a list; they are a network. The Wang Senior Household (Household 1) sits at the center, but the network does not radiate from it — it loops through it. The Wang Tenant Household (2) shares a pig with the Senior Household and a landlord with no one. The Salt Merchant Household (3) is bound to the Senior Household by blood (Lady Wang is Shouzheng's sister, exiled from his threshold for 20 years) and to the village by salt. The Lin Household (4) is bound to the Senior Household by Shouzheng's niece (Lady Wang the carpenter's wife). The Widow's Household (5) is bound to the Senior Household by Shouzheng's quiet charity and to the Tenant Household by her late husband (a Wang cousin). The Zhou Household (6) is bound to every household by birth. The He Household (7) is bound to the Salt Merchant by the dock. The Wu Household (8) is bound to the Lin Household by marriage (Lady Lin is Lin Aqiao's sister). The Zhao Household (9) is bound to the Senior Household by Shouzheng's niece (Lady Wang the soldier's wife — a different Lady Wang, a different niece). The Li Household (10) is bound to no Wang by blood but to the Salt Merchant by trade (Li Asheng sells rice to Hu for the river trade). The Wang Second Tenant Household (11) is bound to the Tenant Household by shared tenancy and shared river-god piety. The Wang Bare-Stick Household (12) is bound to the Senior Household by the half-brother wound. The Wang School Household (13) is bound to the Senior Household by cousinship and by the school obligation. The Wang Second Branch Household (14) is bound to the Senior Household by brotherhood and by the disowned daughter (Wang Meili). The Pan Tofu Household (15) is bound to the Senior Household by Pan Siniang's late husband (Shouzheng's cousin) and to the widows (Xu, Zhou) by the practice of mourning-with-the-widow.
   122→
   123→This is the society the player enters. Every household has at least one tie to another named household, and most have three or four. The three tensions of doc 28 do not run through the Senior Household alone; they run through the network. The salt license tension touches Hu, He, Li, Zhao, Pan, Yang, and Wang Lun. The missing-son tension touches Widow Xu, Zhou, He, Wu, Shoucheng, Zongbao, and Pan. The betrothal tension touches Shouzheng, Sanniang, Wang Lun, Wang Shouli, Zhao, Simei (Shoucheng's daughter), and Li Asheng. No household is a spectator.

   129→### 2.1 Wang Meili (王美麗) — the lineage bridge
   130→
   131→- **Age at introduction (session ~50, Act 1):** 22. **Age at endgame:** ~120, appearing ~30. **Realm at introduction:** Qi Induction. **Realm at endgame:** Foundation Establishment (if she survives).
   132→- **Household:** the Wang Second Branch Household (Household 14), headed by her father Wang Shouli (王守禮, 56), who formally disowned her when she went to the Cangwu Sect. Her mother Lady Qin (秦氏, 53) dies of old age at ~78 (session ~100, the autumn of the first return). Her older brother Wang Zongyao inherits the household; he is mortal and dies at ~62 (session ~300). She has one younger brother, Wang Zongping (19), unmarried.
   133→- **Personality:** gentle, observant, stubborn in private. She has her father's distrust of Old Chen (which she never quite shook) and her mother's quiet competence. She speaks softly. She remembers everything. She does not lie.
   134→- **Secret:** she perceived qi before the protagonist did — at 14, a year before the protagonist's first onset. She told no one because her father distrusted cultivation and would have beaten her for "demonic influence." She has been quietly practicing on her own, with stolen manuals from the Cangwu Sect's outer library, since 15. When the protagonist's perception became known (Scene 1), she felt a complicated mix of relief (not alone) and resentment (the protagonist got the teacher she could not). She did not speak of this for years. When the protagonist left for Old Chen, she went to her father and demanded to be sent to the Cangwu Sect. Her father refused. She went anyway, walking 100 li to the sect gate at 19. The sect took her as a low-ranking healer-track disciple.
   135→- **Grief:** her mother, Lady Qin, died in the autumn of the protagonist's first year at Old Chen's hermitage. Meili was at the Cangwu Sect. She did not return in time. The death was a fever, like Wang Xiaodi's, three days from onset to end. Meili carries the same guilt the protagonist carries — she was not there. She has not said this to the protagonist. The protagonist does not know. This shared unspoken grief is the silent chord between them.
   136→- **Ambition:** she wants to be the Wang lineage's first cultivator-healer — a woman who can be at the bedside of the dying and ease their passing with qi, who can prevent the fevers that took her mother and Wang Xiaodi. She does not want power; she wants to be present. This is the inverse of the protagonist's ambition (the protagonist wants to undo the past; Meili wants to be present for the future).
   137→- **Why she travels with the protagonist:** she joins the protagonist's circle in Act 2 (session ~400) when her Cangwu Sect duties bring her to the Cangli Riverlands to gather herbs. She has heard of the protagonist's Foundation Establishment (the Wang lineage's pride) and seeks them out at Li Family Creek. She travels with the protagonist because (a) she has, by now, perceived her own qi clearly enough to want a teacher who is not bound by sect politics; (b) she wants to see the village again, which she has not visited in 8 years; (c) she carries her mother's unburned tablet, which she wants to install in the Wang lineage hall (the lineage council refused because her mother was a Second Branch wife of lower standing; Meili wants the protagonist, as the Senior Household's cultivator, to intercede). The third reason is the load-bearing one.
   138→- **Arc across the three acts:**
   139→  - **Act 1 (sessions 1-100):** offstage. The protagonist hears of her only as "your cousin who ran away to the sect." Her father has disowned her. The protagonist does not know her secret grief or her secret qi perception.
   140→  - **Act 2 (sessions 100-500):** becomes a companion at session ~400. By then a Qi Condensation healer-track cultivator, ~38 (appearing ~25). She rejoins the protagonist's circle, travels through the Cangli spirit vein dispute and the first meetings with Leng Qingxue and Liang Zhu.

The Shanhaijing's structure — distance-and-direction, mountain name, river origin, beast name, form-description, cry, omen — is the canonical template for the cultivation layer's bestiary. Two design principles emerge directly from the source. First, **beasts have magical properties** (可以禦凶, "can ward off inauspicious events" for the Tiangou; 是食人, "eats humans" for the Qiongqi): the beast's encounter is not merely combat; it carries an omen-effect (Tiangou presence purges ill-fortune; Qiongqi presence threatens human life; the Luo-fish's appearance foretells flood).

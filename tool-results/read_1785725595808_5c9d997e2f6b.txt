     1→# Qi Condensation Envelope & Mortal-to-Cultivator Golden Scenarios — Research Draft v0.1
     2→
     3→**Status:** pre-canon design hypothesis; no realm ladder, protagonist advantage, cosmology, culture, or named technique is ratified here  
     4→**Date:** 2026-08-02  
     5→**Scope:** the mortal threshold and Qi Condensation only  
     6→**Locked constraints:** the player begins genuinely mortal; Qi Condensation cannot personally split a huge mountain; all actors use the same laws; prepared and external power must be attributed honestly

    10→## 0. What this draft is for
    11→
    12→This document gives the next research package something concrete enough to attack:
    13→
    14→- an original, multidimensional Qi Condensation capability envelope;
    15→- quantitative hypotheses that can become test oracles without turning cultivation into one power score;
    16→- functional bands that describe what an actor can reliably do, not a copied realm ladder;
    17→- costs, injuries, deviations, counters, cross-realm conflict, and NPC parity;
    18→- a precise mountain-proof oracle;
    19→- paper-designed mortal-to-cultivator golden scenarios; and
    20→- three lawful protagonist-advantage **design directions**, none selected as canon.
    21→
    22→Every number is a provisional design calibration. It must later survive combat prototyping, visual-scale tests, browser performance research, seasoned-xianxia-reader review, and consistency checks against the eventual cosmology. A number in this draft authorizes a test; it does not silently become lore.

    26→## 1. Comparative research and originality boundary
    27→
    28→### 1.1 Lawfully accessed comparison set
    29→
    30→The draft used public descriptive or academic material, not copied chapters, game data, wiki progression tables, named techniques, or proprietary realm ladders.
    31→
    32→1. The [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entry on Religious Daoism](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2024/entries/daoism-religion/) describes qi in relation to essence, spirit, cosmos, practices, and multiple symbolic models of the body. Design implication: qi should be a relationship among body, attention, environment, and transformation—not a detached blue mana bar. It also warns against pretending one anatomical diagram is a universal Daoist body.
    33→2. Open-access scholarship on [xiuzhen/cultivation fantasy](https://doi.org/10.3390/rel11010025) identifies level-structured cultivation as a device for character development, plot progression, and world-unfolding. Design implication: advancement must change decisions and social position, not only output numbers.
    34→3. The public translation/reference page on [transformative stages in one internal-alchemy tradition](https://goldenelixir.com/jindan/wm_stages.html) illustrates that real traditions frame practice as qualitative transformation. It is context, not a template: this project does not import those stage names, sequence, claims, or religious practice as game physics.
    35→4. The licensed public synopsis of [A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/rmji) foregrounds an ordinary village-born person, limited aptitude, sect access, caution, preparation, and survival. Design implication: mortal circumstances, information, and choices must remain meaningful.
    36→5. The official store description for [Tale of Immortal](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1468810/_Tale_of_Immortal/) presents cultivation as an open-world sandbox of choices. The official page for [Amazing Cultivation Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/955900/Amazing_Cultivation_Simulator/) foregrounds sects, knowledge, artifacts, commerce, alliances, and dangers. Design implication: cultivation belongs to institutions and a living world, not an isolated combat treadmill.
    37→6. The official page for [The Matchless Kungfu](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1696440/The_Matchless_KungFu/) publicly emphasizes inner injury, social relationships, livelihoods, structures, clans, and nonphysical conflict in a martial sandbox. It is a wuxia rather than xianxia comparison. Design implication: body state and ordinary life can support play rather than merely gate combat.
    38→7. The public page for [No Mortal Space](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4279510/No_Mortal_Space/) is useful as a stated scale-fantasy benchmark. Because it remains a public product promise rather than proof of every advertised system, none of its claims are treated as feasibility evidence or copied mechanics.

    40→### 1.2 What may and may not transfer
    41→
    42→May transfer only as abstract design pressures:
    43→
    44→- mortal-to-immortal continuity;
    45→- deliberate practice and transformation;
    46→- preparation, scarcity, teachers, institutions, and consequences;
    47→- qualitative changes in perception and action;
    48→- the body and world corresponding without being identical; and
    49→- very large eventual scale whose attribution remains honest.
    50→
    51→May not transfer:
    52→
    53→- named ladders, stage counts, breakthrough recipes, techniques, artifacts, sects, characters, plots, signature interfaces, numeric balance, prose, visual identity, or source-specific causal topology;
    54→- claims that historical or living religious practices literally produce supernatural effects; or
    55→- an assumption that Daoism, medicine, folklore, Buddhism, popular religion, and modern xianxia are one interchangeable system.
    56→
    57→### 1.3 Original design thesis
    58→
    59→Qi Condensation is the first condition in which a person can **reliably form, retain, route, and deliberately express a bounded qi relation** while body, attention, and environment remain limiting partners. It is not “mortal, but with a larger damage coefficient.”
    60→
    61→The realm's central play is controlled allocation:
    62→
    63→> notice → interpret → gather → route → commit → express → read the trace → recover
    64→
    65→Skipping a link makes the action less efficient, less controlled, more legible to enemies, or more dangerous to the actor.

    69→## 2. Two axes, never one scalar level
    70→
    71→### 2.1 Functional mastery bands within Qi Condensation
    72→
    73→These are capability certifications, not separately named canon realms. Three records must never be collapsed:
    74→
    75→- `OnticCapabilityState`: what the actor can presently and reliably do under canonical law;
    76→- `CapabilityCertificate`: the evidence-backed test result issued by a named evaluator under a versioned protocol; and
    77→- `AudienceRecognition`: what a sect, household, examiner, rival, court, or rumor network believes and is willing to recognize.
    78→
    79→An actor may become ontically capable before anyone certifies them, may hold a valid certificate that another jurisdiction rejects, or may be widely misrecognized. The following table describes the capability required by the protocol; it is not an automatic social title.
    80→
    81→| Functional band | Reliable transformation | What still fails |
    82→|---|---|---|
    83→| **Contact** | Distinguish a repeatable qi change from ordinary sensation; retain a small gathered trace; deliberately start and stop one simple internal route while still. | Cannot maintain a closed route under stress, reinforce a moving body reliably, or project beyond touch. |
    84→| **Circuit** | Maintain one closed route while walking, working, or defending; reinforce one selected bodily region; recognize gross interruption and safely vent a small excess. | Cannot sustain external expression, divide attention across complex routes, or conceal obvious use. |
    85→| **Reach** | Express a shaped effect beyond the skin; inspect or influence a nearby qi-bearing target through a lawful interface; switch between internal reinforcement and external action. | Cannot sustain flight, dominate a large battlefield, ignore hostile disruption, or maintain several independent external effects. |
    86→| **Concord** | Combine perception, routing, movement, and one or two external expressions under pressure; diagnose ordinary faults; teach or coordinate a simple shared exercise. | Cannot exceed the realm hard envelope, personally wield world law, survive major structural destruction, or substitute qi for every missing skill. |
    87→
    88→Promotion between functional bands requires demonstrated reliability, not accumulated points. A candidate performs the relevant operation:
    89→
    90→- on three separate days;
    91→- in at least three materially different contexts;
    92→- once under distraction or moderate pressure;
    93→- without concealed external contribution;
    94→- within the recovery envelope; and
    95→- with a correct account of failure signs and a deliberate stop.
    96→
    97→One lucky success, drugged overdrive, a mentor moving the candidate's qi, or an artifact performing the operation does not certify personal mastery.
    98→
    99→### 2.2 Capability-source bands
   100→
   101→Every feat independently records its source band on every required dimension.
   102→
   103→| Source band | Meaning | Credit language |
   104→|---|---|---|
   105→| **Normal personal** | Stable personal capability expected of a trained actor at the certified functional band. | “The actor did it.” |
   106→| **Exceptional personal** | Rare optimization or lawful protagonist contribution still inside Qi Condensation's personal hard ceiling. | “The actor did it through the named exception.” |
   107→| **Prepared** | Time, allies, terrain work, formations, stored material, or planned conditions supply one or more missing dimensions. | “The actor's preparation/formation/team did it.” |
   108→| **External authority** | An artifact, office, patron, domain, inheritance, decree, or stored higher-order source supplies capability the actor does not possess. | “The actor invoked/controlled the source.” |
   109→
   110→Environmental leverage is recorded alongside these bands. Triggering a pre-existing collapse can be excellent play; it is not personal mountain-splitting strength.

   114→## 3. Measurement model
   115→
   116→### 3.1 Observable outcomes use ordinary units
   117→
   118→Use metres, seconds, kilograms, joules, watts, degrees, concentration, error radius, target count, and time-to-recover wherever the world exposes a physical outcome. Social and informational capability uses typed categories rather than fake energy equivalence.
   119→
   120→### 3.2 Qi-work unit
   121→
   122→`qwu` is an internal calibration unit, not necessarily player-facing lore.
   123→
   124→Under the standard test medium and world gravity, **one qwu certifies enough usable qi contribution to perform 9.81 joules of ideal mechanical work** before conversion loss. This does not mean qi is merely energy. A feat still requires the relevant interface, control, material compatibility, authority, range, and consequence capacity. One million qwu cannot purchase soul access or command an institution.
   125→
   126→Each action ledger records:
   127→
   128→- reservoir before/after;
   129→- peak and sustained qwu throughput;
   130→- physical conversion efficiency;
   131→- heat and waste generated;
   132→- route/control burden;
   133→- authority/interface requirements;
   134→- personal, prepared, external, allied, and environmental contributions; and
   135→- recovery debt.
   136→
   137→### 3.3 Reference actors
   138→
   139→Numbers compare against project test actors, not medical claims about real people.
   140→
   141→- **Ordinary mortal reference:** healthy adult with relevant livelihood competence but no combat specialization.
   142→- **Trained mortal reference:** conditioned guard, hunter, courier, artisan, or martial specialist with a defined skill profile.
   143→- **Qi Condensation reference:** trained mortal reference plus the certified functional band; cultivation does not erase ordinary skill differences.

   147→## 4. Provisional Qi Condensation envelope
   148→
   149→The ranges below describe normal personal capability. The rightmost column is the exceptional **personal hard ceiling for the entire realm**, reachable only by a specialized build at severe cost. No actor may claim every rightmost value simultaneously; coupled body, reservoir, throughput, heat, control, and recovery constraints still apply.
   150→
   151→### 4.1 Body and physical output
   152→
   153→| Measure | Contact | Circuit | Reach | Concord | Realm personal hard ceiling |
   154→|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   155→| Sustainable whole-body output vs. trained mortal | 1.00–1.10× | 1.15–1.50× | 1.35–1.90× | 1.60–2.40× | 3.0× for ≤30 s |
   156→| Certified peak impact or tool-delivered work, including ordinary muscular work inside the same event | 0.5–1.0 kJ | 1–6 kJ | 6–40 kJ | 40–160 kJ | 220 kJ over one specialized ≤3 s all-reserve delivery |
   157→| Mass deliberately lifted 1 m without apparatus | mortal baseline | ≤300 kg | ≤800 kg | ≤2,000 kg | 5,000 kg once, with injury risk |
   158→| Continuous heavy-output interval | ≤5 s | ≤20 s | ≤45 s | ≤120 s | 180 s below half peak |
   159→| Fine manipulation while reinforced | poor | one selected region | two coordinated regions | whole-body route plus hands | never perfect at peak output |
   160→
   161→Hard distinctions:
   162→
   163→- A peak impact is not omnidirectional durability.
   164→- Tool/material failure can cap delivered work below bodily output.
   165→- Penetration, shear, crushing, heat, vibration, and supernatural authority resolve separately.
   166→- A specialized mortal may outskill a stronger cultivator in grappling, blades, medicine, sailing, negotiation, or terrain use.
   167→
   168→### 4.2 Qi reservoir, throughput, and recovery
   169→
   170→| Measure | Contact | Circuit | Reach | Concord | Realm personal hard ceiling |
   171→|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   172→| Stable personal reservoir | 50–250 qwu | 250–1,500 qwu | 1,500–8,000 qwu | 8,000–40,000 qwu | 100,000 qwu |
   173→| Safe sustained throughput | 10–50 qwu/s | 50–250 qwu/s | 250–1,200 qwu/s | 1,200–5,000 qwu/s | 10,000 qwu/s for ≤3 s |
   174→| Normal physical conversion efficiency | 10–25% | 20–40% | 30–55% | 40–70% | 75% on one specialized operation |
   175→| Recovery from 50% depletion in adequate conditions | 4–12 h | 6–18 h | 12–36 h | 18–72 h | never instant |
   176→| Safe environmental assimilation per day | 100–500 qwu | 500–3,000 qwu | 3,000–16,000 qwu | 16,000–80,000 qwu | 200,000 qwu/day specialized |
   177→
   178→Ranges describe correlated profiles: the top reservoir does not pair automatically with the lowest assimilation rate, and assimilated qi does not erase tissue/attention recovery time. Reservoir is not health. At zero usable qi, the actor remains a living person but may be exhausted, cold, shaky, inattentive, or injured depending on how depletion occurred. Food, sleep, medicine, clean air, emotional regulation, and safe shelter can matter without being reducible to qi consumables.
   179→
   180→### 4.3 Control
   181→
   182→| Measure | Contact | Circuit | Reach | Concord | Realm personal hard ceiling |
   183→|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   184→| Independent internal routes | 1 simple segment | 1 closed route or 2 linked segments | 4 linked segments | 8 linked segments | 16, at reduced throughput |
   185→| Concurrent external expressions | 0 | touch only | 1 | 2 | 4 weak or 1 full-strength |
   186→| Directly discriminated targets | self | self + 1 touched target | 1–4 | 1–8 | 16; larger sets require aggregation/preparation |
   187→| Best spatial error | body-region scale | 2–5 cm internally | 5–15 cm at 10 m | 2–10 cm at 30 m | 5 cm at 100 m while stationary |
   188→| Stabilization latency after surprise | >1 s | 0.4–1.0 s | 0.2–0.5 s | 0.10–0.25 s | never zero |
   189→| Maximum personal semantic complexity | 4 primitive relations | 8 | 16 | 32 | 64 with no concurrent combat |
   190→
   191→A blast affecting many people may be one poorly discriminating field, not hundreds of independently controlled targets. Friendly-fire avoidance, persistence, moving targets, concealment, and high output all consume the same control budget.
   192→
   193→### 4.4 Perception and information
   194→
   195→| Capability | Contact | Circuit | Reach | Concord | Realm personal hard ceiling |
   196→|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   197→| Self-perception | gross repeatable change | route direction and blockage class | throughput, leakage, and interference | ordinary fault diagnosis | never perfect knowledge |
   198→| Ambient detection in neutral conditions | contact/within 1 m | 1–5 m | 5–30 m | 30–100 m | 250 m for a loud source in ideal conditions |
   199→| Resolution | presence/absence with confidence | broad source class | direction, intensity band, recent disturbance | partial route/signature comparison | no automatic identity, intent, morality, realm label, or exact technique |
   200→| Occlusion | blocked by ordinary confusion | one simple obstruction may blur | material and formations matter | can compare multiple paths | no x-ray vision; suppression and decoys work |
   201→| Historical trace window | none | seconds at touch | minutes to hours, condition-dependent | hours to days for strong residue | never reconstructs an event without surviving evidence |
   202→
   203→Qi perception produces evidence with confidence and possible alternatives. It cannot read UI-grade truth directly from the universe.
   204→
   205→### 4.5 Mobility
   206→
   207→| Measure | Contact | Circuit | Reach | Concord | Realm personal hard ceiling |
   208→|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   209→| Sustained sprint on clear ground | trained-mortal range | 10–14 m/s | 12–18 m/s | 15–22 m/s | 25 m/s for 30 s |
   210→| Burst dash | none | 12–16 m/s for <1 s | 18–26 m/s for ≤2 s | 25–35 m/s for ≤3 s | 40 m/s for ≤2 s |
   211→| Controlled standing leap, horizontal | mortal range | 2–4 m | 4–10 m | 8–18 m | 25 m with safe landing route |
   212→| Safe actively reinforced fall in neutral terrain | mortal range | 3–5 m | 8–15 m | 15–30 m | 40 m, specialized and prepared |
   213→| Airborne control | none | body orientation | one impulse/redirection | two brief redirections | no hovering or sustained personal flight |
   214→
   215→Terrain, traction, reaction distance, visibility, load, injury, and collateral risk remain real. A 35 m/s burst through a market is not a free travel mode.
   216→
   217→### 4.6 Survivability
   218→
   219→Survivability is a facet vector, never a single defense number.
   220→
   221→| Facet | Contact | Circuit | Reach | Concord | Realm personal hard ceiling |
   222→|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   223→| Distributed blunt impact while correctly braced | slight reduction | survive 5–15 kJ with injury | 15–50 kJ | 50–150 kJ | 300 kJ with severe debt |
   224→| Localized cut/puncture | still mortal | modest deflection at chosen region | resist ordinary weapons only while routed | resist superior mortal weapons at selected regions | no blanket immunity; gaps remain |
   225→| Added heat load over 10 s | negligible | 2–10 kJ | 10–40 kJ | 40–150 kJ | 300 kJ specialized; breathing still matters |
   226→| Poison, disease, deprivation | ordinary vulnerability | earlier warning or slowed onset for known class | purge some compatible contaminants at cost | resist selected studied classes | never universal immunity |
   227→| Recovery | ordinary | modestly faster from minor strain | controlled clotting/qi support for ordinary wounds | stabilize serious but survivable wounds | cannot regrow organs, reverse death, or erase scars |
   228→
   229→Defense must be active, routed, and perceptible enough to counter. Surprise, sleep, qi suppression, wrong facet, depleted reserves, hostage conditions, and social constraints matter.
   230→
   231→### 4.7 Combat grammar
   232→
   233→| Functional band | Reliable combat grammar | Decisive limitations |
   234→|---|---|---|
   235→| Contact | sense gross qi use at close range; commit one reinforced strike or brace; stop an unsafe route | still loses to trained mortal skill, reach, numbers, missiles, or surprise |
   236→| Circuit | continuously choose which region to reinforce; spend qi to accelerate, brace, grip, or recover balance | uncovered regions remain mortal; switching creates windows; depletion is fast |
   237→| Reach | choose between internal advantage and one external pulse, tether, edge, or probe; perceive strong residue | projection is short-ranged, visible, lossy, and interruptible |
   238→| Concord | combine movement, perception, one maintained route, and one external action; deliberately bait or disrupt a peer's route | at most two high-load functions; hostile fields, groups, and long fights overwhelm attention/recovery |
   239→
   240→Qi Condensation combat should reward timing, route choice, terrain, deception, tool quality, reading evidence, and deciding when not to spend qi. It must not become permanent super-armor plus projectile spam.
   241→
   242→### 4.8 Social and institutional envelope
   243→
   244→The exact culture is future canon, but the capability produces predictable social pressures.
   245→
   246→| Band | Observable status pressure | Responsibilities and risks |
   247→|---|---|---|
   248→| Contact | uncertain initiate, patient, curiosity, fraud target | prove perception; avoid misdiagnosis; remain economically dependent |
   249→| Circuit | apprentice/recognized practitioner where institutions exist | register dangerous practice where law requires; teacher and household obligations; scrutiny of injuries |
   250→| Reach | credible armed cultivator and valuable specialist | weapon restrictions, escort/recovery work, recruitment, rivalry, liability for collateral |
   251→| Concord | elite low-realm practitioner, examiner, squad lead, or independent threat | duty of care, institutional claims, taxation/tribute, counter-research, political attention |
   252→
   253→No title appears magically. Status spreads through witnesses, records, demonstrations, sponsorship, rumors, and institutional decisions. A hidden cultivator may retain low apparent status but also lacks the privileges of recognition.
   254→
   255→### 4.9 Resource envelope
   256→
   257→Qi Condensation consumes a life, not only crystals.
   258→
   259→Required resource classes:
   260→
   261→- time without interruption;
   262→- adequate food, water, sleep, sanitation, and shelter;
   263→- a safe practice site and recovery place;
   264→- a correct-enough method, teacher, instrument, or accumulated evidence;
   265→- environmental qi compatible with the route;
   266→- tools or materials for prepared actions;
   267→- social permission, secrecy, or willingness to accept legal consequences; and
   268→- care after injury.
   269→
   270→Resource rules:
   271→
   272→1. Ambient qi has density, variability, contamination, ownership/access, and replenishment. It is not a universal free refill.
   273→2. Consumables may improve assimilation, stability, conversion, or recovery; none directly grants certified mastery.
   274→3. Assimilation is capped by body and route. Stockpiling a thousand doses cannot force a thousand times the throughput.
   275→4. Incompatible inputs create waste, false signals, injury, or long recovery rather than a different-colored buff.
   276→5. Prepared arrays and group practice require maintenance, calibration, trust, and interruption handling.
   277→6. The economy reacts to demand, theft, monopoly, adulteration, counterfeit goods, regulation, and ecological depletion.
   278→
   279→### 4.10 Authority and consequence capacity
   280→
   281→Qi Condensation grants no inherent right to command world law, souls, identity, ownership, oaths, institutions, weather, fate, or territory. It can operate only an already lawful technique/interface within its stated scope. Social authority still comes from relationships and institutions; supernatural authority supplied by an artifact, office, patron, domain, or decree is external-authority output.
   282→
   283→| Measure | Contact | Circuit | Reach | Concord | Realm personal hard ceiling |
   284→|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   285→| Intrinsic law-authorship clauses | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
   286→| Direct physical consequence radius | self/touch | self/touch | ≤15 m | ≤40 m | 100 m specialized, low discrimination |
   287→| Personally maintained persistent qi relations | 0 | 0–1 touch relation | 1–2 anchors | 2–4 anchors | 8 weak anchors within shared control budget |
   288→| Individually discriminated dependents | self | self + 1 touched person/object | 1–4 | 1–8 | 16, already counted by control cap |
   289→| Ordinary aftermath the actor can safely manage alone | self/tool | one patient, tool, or room-scale incident | small household/worksite | small group or building-scale incident | never a settlement-scale catastrophe |
   290→
   291→Causing a larger consequence remains possible through poor judgment, accident, preparation, or external power, but the actor may be unable to control or repair it. Consequence capacity limits responsible action; it does not delete aftermath to fit the actor.

   295→## 5. Coupled limits and stacking
   296→
   297→Every action must satisfy all required dimensions. The lowest sufficient dimension is the bottleneck. Independent table maxima are coordinate ceilings, not a legal actor build or a legal combined-feat vector.
   298→
   299→### 5.1 Normative joint-capability resolver
   300→
   301→Every actor has a versioned `CapabilityProfile` containing concrete caps rather than a choice of table maxima. Every attempted action expands into an `ActionDemandVector` with at least:
   302→
   303→```text
   304→reservoir_draw
   305→throughput over 0.25 s / 1 s / 3 s / 30 s / sustained windows
   306→body_load by affected region
   307→route_and_semantic_control
   308→attention_and_target_discrimination
   309→heat_contamination_and_recoil
   310→recovery_debt
   311→range_duration_and_consequence_capacity
   312→```
   313→
   314→The profile also supplies a calibrated four-channel coactivation vector: `flow`, `body`, `control`, and `perception`. Each action template declares nonnegative channel demand after unavoidable baseline load has been included. Combined actions add channel demands componentwise and are legal only when:
   315→
   316→```text
   317→for every typed cap and rolling window: demand <= actor cap
   318→and
   319→(flow^2 + body^2 + control^2 + perception^2) <= 1.0
   320→```
   321→
   322→This unit-sphere rule is an engineering oracle, not player-facing cosmology. It prevents simultaneous maxima while allowing designers to certify exact boundary profiles. A feat template without a demand vector is non-executable and cannot enter canon.
   323→
   324→The realm hard columns bound possible exceptional profiles. They do not define one super-profile. The initial exceptional fixtures are:
   325→
   326→| Profile fixture | Permitted boundary | Required reductions and aftermath |
   327→|---|---|---|
   328→| `QC-STRIKE-E1` | 220 kJ total delivered across a specialized three-second force commitment; qi contribution uses at most 29,765 qwu at ≤75% plus at most 1 kJ ordinary muscular/tool work; signed coactivation vector `(flow .70, body .65, control .20, perception .05)` | no concurrent external expression or fine manipulation; route count ≤2; defense outside the delivery chain remains mortal-adjacent; reservoir nearly exhausted; recoil/injury check; ≥72 h recovery debt |
   329→| `QC-RESERVOIR-E1` | 100,000 qwu stable reservoir | throughput ≤3,000 qwu/s; efficiency ≤50%; external expressions ≤1; large assimilation and recovery obligations remain |
   330→| `QC-CONTROL-E1` | 16 weak internal relations at reduced throughput | total throughput ≤2,000 qwu/s; no full-strength external expression; stationary or slow movement; high attention load |
   331→| `QC-PERCEPTION-E1` | detect one loud source at up to 250 m in ideal conditions | stationary calibration; throughput ≤1,500 qwu/s; no identity/intent truth; combat control severely reduced |
   332→
   333→At `QC-STRIKE-E1`, `29,765 qwu × 9.81 J/qwu × 0.75 ≈ 218,996 J`; adding no more than 1,000 J of ordinary muscular/tool work keeps the total just below the certified **220 kJ** boundary. The coactivation square sum is `0.70² + 0.65² + 0.20² + 0.05² = 0.955`. No personal preload store exists in this draft, so a larger instantaneous personal result is illegal. A future store would require its own charge time, leakage, custody, recoil, capacity, and attribution contract.
   334→
   335→### 5.2 Deterministic resolution order
   336→
   337→Normative composition order:
   338→
   339→1. establish actor's certified personal base;
   340→2. apply additive training/condition modifiers within the functional band;
   341→3. apply multiplicative efficiency modifiers, capped by the personal hard ceiling;
   342→4. apply replacements such as a tool's cutting interface;
   343→5. add prepared, allied, environmental, and external contributions to separate ledger columns;
   344→6. resolve law/interface eligibility;
   345→7. enforce throughput, control, heat, recovery, target, consequence, and hard-ceiling constraints; and
   346→8. sum simultaneous action vectors and test the coactivation inequality;
   347→9. test every rolling window and update heat, injury, and recovery debt; and
   348→10. produce attribution language from the final ledger.
   349→
   350→Saturation clamps are failure diagnostics, never silent success: if a modifier would exceed a cap, the excess becomes rejected input, waste, recoil, deformation, or another action-declared consequence. Equal-priority effects resolve by stable event ID, never iteration order. Prepared, allied, environmental, and external vectors remain separate and cannot expand the personal profile.

   362→### 5.4 Anti-stack and transaction rules
   363→
   364→Anti-stack rules:
   365→
   366→- Two routes drawing from one reservoir compete; they do not duplicate it.
   367→- Two efficiency multipliers combine with diminishing returns and cannot exceed the realm efficiency cap.
   368→- Sequential micro-actions accrue heat, tissue load, attention, environmental depletion, and recovery debt in rolling windows.
   369→- A prepared reservoir does not raise personal throughput or control.
   370→- An external artifact does not grant personal survivability against its own recoil unless that contribution is separately supplied.
   371→- A specialized exceptional cap in one dimension lowers concurrent performance elsewhere unless a future canon decision explicitly prices a different coupling.
   372→
   373→Every cultivation transition is a transaction, including a deliberately persistent partial breakthrough. It records `TransactionId`, action/schema versions, read revisions, reserved inputs, deterministic write set, external contributions, and one of `Prepared | Committed | Aborted | Compensating`. Commit has one monotonic world-event sequence number. Replaying the same transaction ID is idempotent. A crash before commit releases reservations and preserves only explicitly committed precursor events; a crash after commit replays the committed write set. Irreversible writes to another actor, object, institution, or environment are separate child transactions with declared compensation or a declared non-compensable aftermath—they are never silently rolled back because the initiating technique failed.

   377→## 6. Costs, injuries, and cultivation deviations
   378→
   379→### 6.1 Cost ledgers
   380→
   381→Each practice or technique declares:
   382→
   383→- usable qi spent and unrecoverable conversion loss;
   384→- peak/sustained route load;
   385→- body strain by region or species-appropriate conduit;
   386→- heat, pressure, contamination, and attention;
   387→- time locked into preparation and recovery;
   388→- material consumed or damaged;
   389→- social exposure and obligation; and
   390→- opportunity cost: work missed, relationship neglected, journey delayed, or safety forfeited.
   391→
   392→### 6.2 Injury classes
   393→
   394→| Class | Cause | Player-facing signs | Mechanical consequence | Recovery path |
   395→|---|---|---|---|---|
   396→| **Load strain** | output or reinforcement exceeds adapted tissue | shaking, pain, loss of fine control | reduced throughput and route precision | rest, food, ordinary care, gradual retraining |
   397→| **Conduit irritation** | repeated high flow or incompatible route | hot/cold patches, intermittent leakage, sensory noise | one route becomes costly or unstable | stop, diagnose, gentle circulation, skilled care |
   398→| **Route rupture** | forced flow through a closed or damaged path | sudden loss of reinforcement, bruising/bleeding, qi discharge | reservoir leakage; external action disabled | urgent stabilization; long recovery; possible scar |
   399→| **Sensory burn** | perception opened beyond discrimination capacity | afterimages, false positives, headache, confusion | reduced confidence and range; decoys become dangerous | quiet, sleep, low-stimulus retraining, trusted verification |
   400→| **Conversion poisoning** | incompatible environmental/material input | nausea, temperature change, discolored trace, irregular route | efficiency loss; contaminant expresses during techniques | identify source; purge into safe sink; medicine; time |
   401→| **Recoil trauma** | external expression pushes back through body/tool | impact injury, numb limb, broken tool | affected route locked; combat vulnerability | ordinary trauma care plus route repair |
   402→
   403→These are fictional systems. They must not stigmatize real illness, disability, mental health conditions, or cultural practice.
   404→
   405→### 6.3 Deviations are processes, not random punishment
   406→
   407→| Deviation | Formation process | Tempting benefit | Cost/failure | Counterplay |
   408→|---|---|---|---|---|
   409→| **False circuit** | a plausible but wrong route becomes easy through repetition | faster startup and a pleasant sensation | capacity plateaus; load accumulates at one junction | compare external evidence, stop, retrain slowly |
   410→| **Cross-current** | two individually valid methods demand incompatible timing or direction | brief high output | destructive interference, loss of control | separate schedules, compatibility work, choose one under stress |
   411→| **Route fixation** | over-specialization adapts body and attention to one pattern | exceptional efficiency in one technique | switching becomes slow/painful; predictable counter | deliberate variation and recovery, accept specialization tradeoff |
   412→| **Borrowed signature adhesion** | repeated use of contaminated place, tool, teacher assistance, or external source | easier activation | trace reveals or biases the actor; source may influence results | cleanse, quarantine, investigate provenance, build own stable pattern |
   413→| **Breath–motion desynchronization** | routing rhythm no longer matches movement and recovery | momentary burst | dizziness, wasted qi, delayed defense | slow forms, pacing, teacher observation, environmental cadence |
   414→
   415→A deviation must have a cause, early signals, discoverability path, progression if ignored, intervention window, residual consequence, and at least two recovery strategies. It cannot be a hidden dice roll labeled “qi deviation.”

   419→## 7. Meaningful new verbs
   420→
   421→These are action families for testing, not final names or techniques.
   422→
   423→### 7.1 `Attend to Flow`
   424→
   425→- **Transition:** uncertain sensation/evidence → bounded qi observation with confidence and alternatives.
   426→- **New information:** exposes route, intensity band, disturbance, or residue unavailable to mortal senses.
   427→- **Costs:** attention, time, sensory load; movement and defense degrade.
   428→- **Counters/failures:** ambient noise, decoy, suppression, occlusion, sensory burn, motivated interpretation.
   429→- **Cross-system consequences:** investigation and medicine gain evidence; stealth, fraud, ecology, and testimony change.
   430→- **Not a reskin:** it creates information rather than damage or a numeric accuracy bonus.
   431→
   432→### 7.2 `Route Qi`
   433→
   434→- **Transition:** undirected reservoir → committed path and selected bodily function.
   435→- **New decision:** reinforce legs, hands, breath, balance, wound stabilization, or a tool interface; unchosen regions remain exposed.
   436→- **Costs:** throughput, switching latency, route load, predictable signature.
   437→- **Counters/failures:** feint toward an unreinforced region, disrupt a junction, exhaust reservoir, force rapid switching.
   438→- **Cross-system consequences:** labor, climbing, healing support, crafting precision, and combat all change.
   439→- **Not a reskin:** it changes allocation topology and vulnerability, not merely a stat.
   440→
   441→### 7.3 `Read Residue`
   442→
   443→- **Transition:** persistent physical/qi traces → partial hypothesis about direction, timing, source class, and disturbance.
   444→- **New decision:** investigate, compare, preserve, contaminate, conceal, falsify, or testify.
   445→- **Costs:** time, proximity, calibration sample, attention.
   446→- **Counters/failures:** old/mixed traces, weather, deliberate contamination, unfamiliar source, confirmation bias.
   447→- **Cross-system consequences:** law, hunting, medicine, ecology, sect security, and history become playable.
   448→- **Not a reskin:** it operates on evidence and uncertainty, not enemy highlighting.
   449→
   450→### 7.4 `Anchor a Trace`
   451→
   452→- **Transition:** transient personal expression → a short-lived relation attached to a touched object, boundary, wound dressing, or prepared mark.
   453→- **New decision:** maintain, release, hand off, or let the anchor decay.
   454→- **Costs:** reserved reservoir, direct touch or prepared interface, target compatibility, ongoing target slot.
   455→- **Counters/failures:** remove the medium, exceed range/duration, overwrite with superior control, exploit the detectable signature.
   456→- **Cross-system consequences:** alarms, repairs, coordination, traps, custody, crafting, and teaching change.
   457→- **Not a reskin:** it creates persistent conditional state rather than an instantaneous projectile.
   458→
   459→### 7.5 `Vent into a Sink`
   460→
   461→- **Transition:** dangerous internal heat/contamination/excess → transferred bounded aftermath in a compatible prepared sink.
   462→- **New decision:** choose where consequence goes and who bears it.
   463→- **Costs:** sink material, time, control, exposure, environmental liability.
   464→- **Counters/failures:** incompatible or saturated sink, interrupted route, dishonest disposal, rebound.
   465→- **Cross-system consequences:** medicine, waste economy, ecology, law, crafting, and social responsibility change.
   466→- **Not a reskin:** it moves a liability; it does not erase one.
   467→
   468→### 7.6 `Synchronize Practice`
   469→
   470→- **Transition:** two consenting, separately acting practice loops → shared timing/error evidence without merged reservoirs or minds.
   471→- **New decision:** consent, trust, lead/follow, stop conditions, disclosure, and mutual risk.
   472→- **Costs:** attention, reduced individual output, reciprocal exposure, compatibility strain.
   473→- **Counters/failures:** withdrawal, deception, incompatible cadence, asymmetric load, interruption.
   474→- **Cross-system consequences:** teaching, friendship, family, sect pedagogy, disability accommodation, and sabotage change.
   475→- **Not a reskin:** it creates a consensual coordination relation and reciprocal evidence.
   476→
   477→At least two of these must survive semantic-equivalence testing and appear as useful choices outside combat before Qi Condensation can pass its qualitative-realm gate.

   479→### 7.7 Semantic novelty normalization
   480→
   481→Every action family has a versioned semantic signature:
   482→
   483→```text
   484→preconditions | typed targets | read set | write set | authority
   485→conservation/contribution | range | duration | time cost
   486→observables/evidence | failure transitions | counters | persistence/aftermath
   487→```
   488→
   489→Two actions are semantic equivalents when their `SEM-NORM-v0.1` normal forms match. Animation, color, an element **label without different material/law interfaces**, damage number, numeric target-count tuning, projectile shape, or lore prose alone cannot create a new verb.
   490→
   491→#### `SEM-NORM-v0.1` canonicalizer
   492→
   493→1. Validate every field against `SemanticActionSignature-v0.1`; absent optional fields become explicit typed `null`, never implementation defaults.
   494→2. Remove presentation, prose, audio, animation, VFX, proper nouns, and tuning-only display labels. Replace actor/target/tool/place names with role slots ordered by first typed causal use.
   495→3. Convert unitful values to the canonical base unit through exact rational conversion. Numeric tuning values become named parameters plus exact inequality/dependency relations; changing magnitude or count alone is ignored unless it crosses a typed target, authority, range topology, persistence, failure, or consequence-class boundary.
   496→4. Expand shorthand into typed nodes for precondition, read, decision, authority, contribution/conservation, transition, observable, failure, counter, persistence, and aftermath. Ordered action milestones remain ordered; set-like fields sort lexicographically.
   497→5. Initial node colors are the exact tuple `(node_type, target_type, authority_type, unit_dimension, persistence_class, failure_class)`. Repeatedly refine colors from sorted incoming/outgoing typed-edge/color multisets until stable.
   498→6. Inline every nonrecursive declared subaction into the parent semantic graph before labeling; factor boundaries and subaction names disappear. Repeated occurrences receive role-path slots derived from the parent edges. Recursive composition or an expanded graph above 128 nodes is `NON_EXECUTABLE`, not permission to compare only wrappers.
   499→7. Multiplicity compression is permitted only for an **independent true-twin leaf set**. Every member must have the same exact typed attribute tuple; have no edge to another member; have exactly one incident edge; and connect through the same edge type, direction, and edge attributes to the same single outside anchor node. No member may carry order, identity, target, custody, authority, temporal, evidence, contribution, failure, counter, persistence, or aftermath semantics. Any stable color partition that does not meet all of those conditions remains uncompressed and proceeds to bounded individualization/refinement. Compression does not compute or retain a `canonical_subgraph_hash`. It replaces the leaves with one node serialized exactly as `{"kind":"independent_true_twin_leaves","member_attributes":<canonical-JSON exact attribute tuple>,"multiplicity":<unsigned base-10 integer>,"anchor_edge":{"direction":<in|out>,"edge_type":<UTF-8 string>,"edge_attributes":<canonical-JSON exact tuple>}}`, connected once to the same anchor; `multiplicity` semantically repeats that incident edge. Object keys use the canonical-JSON ordering from step 8, strings are NFC-normalized, and integers have no sign or leading zero. This is the only compression class in `SEM-NORM-v0.1`; it cannot erase or recursively solve internal topology because eligible members have none.
   500→8. Run deterministic individualization/refinement on the remaining colored graph: choose the lexicographically first smallest non-singleton color partition, individualize each candidate in its current canonical attribute order, refine again, prune any branch whose serialized prefix exceeds the best complete prefix, and retain the lexicographically smallest complete UTF-8 canonical-JSON adjacency serialization.
   501→9. The search may visit at most `1,048,576` refinement states and use at most `128 MiB` of specification-time workspace under `SEM-NORM-v0.1`. Exceeding either bound returns `NORMALIZATION_INDETERMINATE` and blocks the proposed action from canon; it never hangs, guesses equivalence, or treats a timeout as novelty.
   502→10. Hash the serialization with SHA-256. Equivalence requires identical schema version and hash; there is no float tolerance. A schema migration compares both old and new normal forms explicitly.
   503→
   504→Canonical renaming therefore has one algorithm. “Normalized scale” means exact removal of tuning magnitude while retaining dimensional type, parameter dependency, thresholds that create different branches, and all downstream state topology.

   732→- a livelihood with a seasonal skill curve;
   733→- a debt or obligation that cannot be solved by one combat encounter;
   734→- one bodily strength and one limitation unrelated to cultivation aptitude;
   735→- one private desire that may have nothing to do with immortality; and
   736→- one conflicting community pressure.
   737→
   738→The player may choose among at least four viable livelihoods: food production, craft/repair, transport/trade, and care/record/service. Hunting, guarding, performance, scholarship, or illicit work may emerge from local conditions.

   759→### 13.3 Cultivation seeds, not quest markers
   760→
   761→At least five ordinary systems can create legitimate clues:
   762→
   763→- repeated temperature or sound anomaly at a waterwork;
   764→- a healer's observation that a breathing rhythm changes recovery;
   765→- tool wear inconsistent with known use;
   766→- an animal avoiding a place at a particular time;
   767→- conflicting testimony about a traveler or local practitioner;
   768→- a ledger showing unusual material demand; or
   769→- residue around an old repair, grave, shrine, cave, kiln, or battlefield.
   770→
   771→Any one clue may be mundane, fraudulent, misunderstood, or supernatural. Only comparison and consequence establish confidence.
   772→
   773→### 13.4 Completion oracle
   774→
   775→The scenario passes when:
   776→
   777→- the player can spend the year pursuing at least three satisfying noncombat life trajectories;
   778→- every month changes later options without forcing a single plot;
   779→- leaving, remaining mortal, or seeking cultivation are all intelligible outcomes;
   780→- at least ten NPCs change state for reasons independent of the protagonist;
   781→- ordinary skills remain useful in all later golden scenarios;
   782→- no cultivation clue requires protagonist privilege to discover; and
   783→- players can recount who they were, whom they owed, and what they chose before discussing “build.”

   788→

   790→**Purpose:** make pedagogy a social, epistemic, economic, and bodily choice rather than a menu of objectively ranked bonuses.

   793→

   796→| **Community bodyworker** | pacing, observation, recovery, safe stop signs, one conservative Contact exercise | projection, combat dominance, cosmological certainty | service, patience, confidentiality, care for other students |

   818→- Give a teacher correct knowledge but exploitative terms; refusal remains viable.

   824→## 15. Golden Scenario C — The Fraudulent Manual
   825→
   826→**ID:** `GS-FRAUD-MANUAL-01`  
   827→**Purpose:** test truth, mixed evidence, scarcity, fraud, injury, law, and the difference between understanding words and performing a transformation.
   828→
   829→This is the paper-manuscript fixture of the broader `MethodTransmission` contract. The connected suite must also run `GS-FRAUD-ORAL-01`, `GS-MISTAKEN-DEMONSTRATION-01`, and `GS-SELF-HYPOTHESIS-01`. When no authored carrier exists, paper/ink/custody tests are explicitly non-applicable and source-chain, witnessed-demonstration, memory-revision, curriculum, or experiment-log evidence substitutes. No later scenario may require the manuscript merely because this fixture has one.
   830→
   831→### 15.1 The object
   832→
   833→The manual is procedurally assembled from a causal authorship history:
   834→
   835→- two observations copied from a legitimate but context-specific practice;
   836→- one harmless conventional exercise;
   837→- one mistranscribed route relation;
   838→- one fabricated claim promising fast capacity;
   839→- one omission of a dangerous stop condition;
   840→- a seller's added testimonial;
   841→- material/ink/ownership traces; and
   842→- a chain of previous readers with mixed outcomes.
   843→
   844→It is not tagged `fake` in player-facing truth. Some parts are useful; the promised transformation is false and the omitted condition is dangerous.
   845→
   846→### 15.2 Investigation paths
   847→
   848→- compare vocabulary and diagrams with independent records;
   849→- inspect material age, corrections, ownership, and copying pattern;
   850→- interview a previous reader, seller, teacher, artisan, or healer;
   851→- test one low-risk claim under observation;
   852→- observe whether the method's claimed environmental conditions exist;
   853→- read residue from a prior practice site after gaining that verb;
   854→- trace who profits from distribution; or
   855→- present it to an institution whose judgment may itself be biased.
   856→
   857→### 15.3 Player choices
   858→
   859→- reject and destroy it, losing evidence;
   860→- preserve and warn others;
   861→- expose the seller publicly or build a legal case;
   862→- sell it onward and accept consequences;
   863→- salvage a true fragment with a teacher;
   864→- attempt it fully out of urgency, ambition, or disbelief; or
   865→- alter it into a safer experimental protocol.
   866→
   867→### 15.4 Oracle
   868→
   869→The advantage cannot label the manual true/false, supply missing context, immunize injury, or guarantee persuasion. The scenario passes when at least three independent evidence paths can establish the dangerous contradiction, one path can produce a plausible wrong conclusion, and consequences persist for the seller, prior readers, institutions, and the player's relationships.

   873→## 16. Golden Scenario D — First Qi Perception
   874→
   875→**ID:** `GS-FIRST-PERCEPTION-01`  
   876→**Purpose:** make first perception an earned, testable change in relationship to the world—not a chosen-one cutscene or permanent glowing outline.
   877→
   878→### 16.1 Preconditions
   879→
   880→- repeated mundane observation from the mortal year;
   881→- at least one plausible explanatory model;
   882→- a stable enough body/attention state;
   883→- a site or object with a signal above noise;
   884→- time and safety to compare; and
   885→- willingness to stop if early warning signs appear.
   886→
   887→### 16.2 Encounter sequence
   888→
   889→1. The player notices a cross-modal discrepancy: rhythm, pressure, temperature, sound, balance, tool vibration, animal behavior, or breath changes together.
   890→2. Ordinary causes remain available.
   891→3. The player changes one condition and repeats the observation.
   892→4. A second site/object/person produces a related but nonidentical pattern.
   893→5. The player predicts one bounded outcome and tests it.
   894→6. Success creates one low-confidence observation toward `Contact`; it does not certify the band. Failure creates evidence, not a hidden progress bar.
   895→7. Repetition across contexts can establish the precursor skill behind `Attend to Flow`, but the verb is not reliably available until the player can form and deliberately stop the simple route required for Contact certification.
   896→
   897→### 16.3 False-positive cases
   898→
   899→- expectation induced by a respected teacher;
   900→- environmental rhythm mistaken for bodily qi;
   901→- a concealed artifact or another actor producing the signal;
   902→- sensory overload;
   903→- mundane vibration, heat, illness, hunger, fear, or fatigue; and
   904→- a deliberate fraud using timing and testimony.
   905→
   906→The game communicates confidence, contradictions, and remembered observations through the character's language and records, not omniscient debug labels.

   911→
   912→**ID:** `GS-FIRST-CONFLICT-01`  
   913→**Purpose:** prove that a new perception adds choices without turning the first cultivator encounter into automatic victory.
   914→
   915→### 17.1 Fixture
   916→
   917→The manuscript fixture joins three existing pressures:
   918→
   919→- a scarce public resource or access route damaged during the mortal year;
   920→- someone using the fraudulent manual or a related copy;
   921→- a trained mortal enforcer, desperate practitioner, or low-band cultivator whose goal is understandable but harmful.
   922→
   923→In the no-manual suite, the second pressure is replaced by a disputed oral method, harmful demonstration, institutional curriculum, self-experiment, or a completely independent generated conflict over resource, testimony, labor, law, or safety. If cultivation fraud is absent, the seller/victim reads and their promised payoffs are marked non-applicable rather than invented.
   924→
   925→The opponent needs something concrete: money, treatment, evidence, access, protection, status, or escape. They do not wait in an arena.
   926→
   927→### 17.2 Resolution families
   928→
   929→- identify a dangerous qi irregularity and persuade someone to stop;
   930→- preserve evidence and seek institutional help;
   931→- repair or reroute the contested resource;
   932→- expose the seller or protect a victim;
   933→- bargain, deceive, flee, hide, or recruit allies;
   934→- sabotage an external dependency;
   935→- duel under agreed limits; or
   936→- fight in real space with bystanders, terrain, injuries, and aftermath.
   937→
   938→### 17.3 Combat proof
   939→
   940→If combat occurs, first perception supplies intermittent evidence about a committed route. It does not slow time, aim automatically, reveal intent, or make mortal weapons harmless. The player must decide when attending is worth reduced defense.
   941→
   942→The scenario fails if:
   943→
   944→- all noncombat resolutions collapse into combat;
   945→- the opponent attacks without self-preservation or social context;
   946→- one new qi verb trivializes ordinary positioning and skill;
   947→- bystanders forget or collateral disappears; or
   948→- defeat triggers an unearned mentor rescue.

   952→## 18. Golden Scenario F — Failed Breakthrough
   953→
   954→**ID:** `GS-FAILED-BREAKTHROUGH-01`  
   955→**Purpose:** make failure causal, diagnosable, survivable in multiple ways, and narratively productive.
   956→
   957→### 18.1 Attempt
   958→
   959→The player tries to turn repeatable first perception into a stable `Contact` route. The attempt has explicit inputs:
   960→
   961→- chosen method revision;
   962→- body and injury state;
   963→- site and environmental conditions;
   964→- teacher/observer availability;
   965→- food, sleep, time pressure, and emotional distraction;
   966→- prior practice evidence; and
   967→- stop conditions.
   968→
   969→The manuscript fixture's golden failure arises from a traceable conjunction, not a forced roll: unresolved strain from the first conflict + one incorrect transmitted relation + pressure to finish before a local livelihood obligation. No-manual fixtures replace that relation with an oral revision, misunderstood demonstration, false institutional premise, self-hypothesis error, site incompatibility, or another signed causal failure input. At least one failure family must remain executable, but none must pretend a manuscript existed.
   970→
   971→### 18.2 Failure transition
   972→
   973→1. The route begins correctly.
   974→2. The incorrect relation accumulates load at a junction.
   975→3. Early signs appear and are observable.
   976→4. The player may stop, ask for help, improvise, push, or vent.
   977→5. Pushing creates conduit irritation or a false circuit; stopping creates lost time and social cost but less injury.
   978→6. The world records who helped, who advised pushing, what work was missed, and what evidence survived.
   979→
   980→### 18.3 Recovery paths
   981→
   982→- ordinary rest, food, and care plus slow retraining;
   983→- a teacher's diagnosis, with debt and trust implications;
   984→- compare the failed trace against another practitioner;
   985→- use a prepared sink and accept material/environmental aftermath;
   986→- abandon the method and preserve transferable observation skill; or
   987→- remain mortal without a punitive ending.
   988→
   989→Failure does not award guaranteed compensatory power. It creates information only to the extent that evidence survived and the actor can interpret it.

   993→## 19. Golden Scenario G — First Genuine Success
   994→
   995→**ID:** `GS-FIRST-SUCCESS-01`  
   996→**Purpose:** make the mortal-to-cultivator transition quiet enough to believe and deep enough to change play.
   997→
   998→### 19.1 Certification encounter
   999→
  1000→The player establishes one bounded internal segment and deliberately starts, redirects within that segment, and stops it. The segment is not closed and cannot recirculate independently; maintaining a closed route remains a Circuit certification:
  1001→
  1002→- on three separate days;
  1003→- while still in three materially different contexts: immediately after ordinary work, in controlled practice, and under moderate distraction/pressure without walking or maintaining the segment through bodily movement;
  1004→- with reservoir, throughput, heat, and recovery inside Contact limits;
  1005→- without mentor, artifact, or advantage performing the route; and
  1006→- while correctly recognizing one false signal and one stop condition.
  1007→
  1008→The moment of success changes verbs rather than showering statistics:
  1009→
  1010→- `Attend to Flow` becomes repeatable;
  1011→- `Route Qi` becomes available for one simple internal segment;
  1012→- the player can preserve a better practice record;
  1013→- some injuries and environmental patterns become newly legible;
  1014→- teachers, family, employers, institutions, rivals, and fraudsters may reassess the player if evidence reaches them; and
  1015→- new obligations compete with the old life.
  1016→
  1017→### 19.2 What success does not grant
  1018→
  1019→- no projection, flight, instant healing, inventory magic, realm aura, universal diagnosis, automatic status, guaranteed lifespan, or combat victory;
  1020→- no erasure of debt, disability, grief, crime, relationships, season, hunger, or mortal skills;
  1021→- no proof that the chosen cosmology or teacher doctrine is universally true; and
  1022→- no protection from future failure.
  1023→
  1024→### 19.3 Continuity payoff
  1025→
  1026→The best first use may be noncombat: stabilize a hand during repair, notice contamination in a waterwork, keep footing during a rescue, compare a patient's route without claiming a cure, preserve evidence, or synchronize a practice with someone who helped during the mortal year.

  1030→## 20. Protagonist-advantage design directions
  1031→
  1032→These are three candidates for later comparison, not final choices. They are a focused scenario test, not a substitute for the foundation gate requiring comparison of at least seven abstract advantage operations before any actual advantage is selected. Names are working labels and should change during canon naming. Each changes one operation inside shared law and contributes **zero direct reservoir, output, durability, authority, or guaranteed success**.
  1033→
  1034→### 20.0 Versioned target and phase schema
  1035→
  1036→The candidate comparison uses `ADV-TARGET-v0.2`. It deliberately supports a bounded pre-qi interface so “mortal-year value” is executable rather than promotional prose.
  1037→
  1038→| Direction | Mortal phase target (`M0`, no qi required) | Contact-and-later extension (`C1+`) | Never a target |
  1039→|---|---|---|---|
  1040→| Residual Error Sense | one `SelfOwnedAttemptTrace`: coarse timing/sequence mismatch left by a deliberate embodied work, movement, or observation attempt | adds intended-versus-actual fields for self-owned qi routing | another person's private state, truth value, identity, intent, future, unattended event |
  1041→| Held Pattern | one `SelfGeneratedEmbodiedPattern`: an already-present, harmless sensory/attentional coordination relation, held ≤1 s and prevented from acting while held | adds one self-generated internal qi relation, initially <3 s | wound, poison, breath requirement, death process, external attack, cooldown, another actor, changing world state |
  1042→| Reciprocal Cadence | one `ConsentedTimingChannel`: phase difference only during a deliberate joint physical task, touch range, ≤10 s | adds low-band route timing/error fields during shared practice | memory, emotion, skill, reservoir, diagnosis, command, nonconsenting or uncertain participant |
  1043→
  1044→Mortal use creates no qi, superhuman output, hidden information, preservation of bodily processes, or free labor. It can improve one comparison or coordination decision only after ordinary effort occurs. Every activation pays time, attention, exposure, and recovery costs and is tested against livelihood, appraisal, surveillance, consent, and economy exploits. If these interfaces fail the gauntlet, the candidate loses its mortal-year claim rather than silently expanding.
  1045→
  1046→### Direction A — Residual Error Sense
  1047→
  1048→**Privileged operation:** after the bearer personally completes or stops a deliberate attempt and pays its normal cost, they can inspect one short-lived mismatch in the applicable self-owned `ADV-TARGET-v0.2` trace. Qi-routing fields do not exist before Contact.
  1049→
  1050→| Field | Draft rule |
  1051→|---|---|
  1052→| Benefit | unusually faithful feedback about where/when the bearer's performed route diverged |
  1053→| Nonbenefits | no correct method, future prediction, external identity, intent, hidden text, realm label, or automatic interpretation |
  1054→| Capacity | one inspected attempt at a time; seconds of trace; coarse sequence/timing only at M0 and coarse route location after Contact |
  1055→| Activation | genuine attempt, deliberate review, surviving trace, attention |
  1056→| Cost | sensory load, rest time, and risk of fixating on one misleading mismatch |
  1057→| Counter/failure | mixed causes, contaminated input, false model, erased trace, overload, insufficient vocabulary |
  1058→| Social signal | unusual diagnostic consistency may attract teachers, exploiters, or accusations of stolen lineage knowledge |
  1059→
  1060→**Golden-scenario enrichment:** makes the player better at comparing work rhythms during the mortal year, generates an extra clue in the fraudulent manual, sharpens the failed-breakthrough postmortem, and supports deliberate correction. It never supplies the missing teacher, safe site, resources, or stop decision.
  1061→
  1062→**Exploit locks:** cannot inspect an action never attempted; cannot spam zero-cost gestures; cannot reveal the whole solution by subdividing attempts; repeated review decays and adds sensory debt; cannot inspect another person's private state without an ordinary observation interface; cannot turn lethal failure into survival; no mountain contribution.
  1063→
  1064→### Direction B — Held Pattern
  1065→
  1066→**Privileged operation:** the bearer may hold one applicable `ADV-TARGET-v0.2` self-generated relation approximately unchanged for its phase-bounded duration so it can be observed or handed back to ordinary resolution deliberately.
  1067→
  1068→| Field | Draft rule |
  1069→|---|---|
  1070→| Benefit | creates a tiny inspection window during practice; makes some fleeting patterns teachable and comparable |
  1071→| Nonbenefits | does not stop time, body, injury, poison, death, external attacks, other actors, resource depletion, or entropy outside the selected qi relation |
  1072→| Capacity | one relation; ≤1 s at M0, initially under three seconds after Contact; no intake/output or active bodily function through the held relation |
  1073→| Activation | pattern must already be lawfully formed by bearer and fit the phase-specific target schema; qi target must remain below Contact throughput |
  1074→| Cost | numbness, lost throughput during hold, rebound instability, long recovery between uses |
  1075→| Counter/failure | external disruption, body movement, incompatible pattern, overload, suppression, interruption |
  1076→| Social signal | a distinctive pause and rebound trace can be detected and studied |
  1077→
  1078→**Golden-scenario enrichment:** lets the player preserve a fleeting first-perception pattern long enough to compare it, show a teacher a real state rather than describe it, or choose a controlled stop during failed breakthrough. It cannot form the pattern, decide what it means, keep a dangerous body alive, or certify mastery.
  1079→
  1080→**Exploit locks:** no frozen wounds, held breath forever, paused cost, invulnerability frame, cooldown reset, indefinite chain, external projectile stop, duplicated reservoir, save/reload extension, or mountain-scale stasis. The held relation cannot act while held.
  1081→
  1082→### Direction C — Reciprocal Cadence
  1083→
  1084→**Privileged operation:** with informed, revocable consent, the bearer and one partner can share the phase-appropriate `ADV-TARGET-v0.2` timing/error signal during a joint task or practice. Reservoirs, memories, bodies, techniques, and authority remain separate.
  1085→
  1086→| Field | Draft rule |
  1087→|---|---|
  1088→| Benefit | unusually rich mutual feedback; both participants feel timing agreement and divergence |
  1089→| Nonbenefits | no technique theft, mind reading, emotional truth, power transfer, forced healing, compatibility guarantee, or solo bonus |
  1090→| Capacity | one consenting partner, touch/short range, simple route, reduced output, brief sessions |
  1091→| Activation | mutual informed consent, shared exercise, compatible safe band, agreed stop signal |
  1092→| Cost | reciprocal exposure, trust risk, reduced individual control, fatigue in both participants |
  1093→| Counter/failure | withdrawal, deception about method, incompatible cadence, interruption, asymmetric injury, social prohibition |
  1094→| Social signal | both participants and observers can retain evidence; institutions may regulate or claim the practice |
  1095→
  1096→**Golden-scenario enrichment:** turns teacher choice into a reciprocal relationship, lets a peer help test the fraudulent manual without transferring risk invisibly, makes recovery and first success social, and gives the mortal year's relationships mechanical continuity.
  1097→
  1098→**Exploit locks:** consent can be withdrawn immediately; an unconscious or coerced participant cannot initiate or continue the link; copied, summoned, simulated, postmortem, young, cognitively atypical, or jurisdictionally restricted participants require the future personhood and decision-specific consent model rather than a blanket loss of personhood, and uncertainty defaults to no link; no multi-partner exponential network; no memory/skill copying; no resource duplication; each participant bears real load; no mountain contribution.
  1099→
  1100→### 20.4 Candidate comparison
  1101→
  1102→| Pressure | Residual Error Sense | Held Pattern | Reciprocal Cadence |
  1103→|---|---|---|---|
  1104→| Protects mortal premise | high: needs attempts and ordinary life evidence | high: preserves only what bearer already forms | high: needs relationships and consent |
  1105→| Enriches teacher choice | teacher helps interpret feedback | teacher can inspect held state | teacher becomes reciprocal participant |
  1106→| Fraud/manual play | highlights mismatch after a test | preserves test state for comparison | shares error evidence with partner |
  1107→| Failed breakthrough | better postmortem, no rescue | controlled observation/stop, no immunity | shared warning with mutual risk |
  1108→| Mystery risk | may over-answer unless trace is narrow | may become puzzle pause unless strictly self-only | may expose too much unless timing-only |
  1109→| Solo-play strength | strong | strong | weak by design |
  1110→| Social-system strength | medium | medium | very high |
  1111→| Plot-armor risk | low if post-action only | medium if bodily state can be held; hard exclusion required | low if consent/personhood are rigorous |
  1112→| Growth axis | trace duration/resolution/causal separation | complexity/duration at unchanged throughput | partner diversity/coordination, not network size |

  1114→No direction advances until identity/death, consent/personhood, and provisional cosmology models exist. The user alone selects or rejects the final direction.

  1118→## 21. Advantage gauntlet shared by all three directions
  1119→
  1120→1. **Mortal-year skip:** Can the advantage solve food, money, status, danger, or access without ordinary action? Expected: no.
  1121→2. **Teacher absence:** Remove every mentor. The direction may support slower experimentation but cannot generate complete doctrine or safe certainty.
  1122→3. **Perfect comprehension:** Feed ambiguous, false, obsolete, incompatible, and adversarial models. The direction returns bounded evidence, not truth.
  1123→4. **Fraud oracle:** Present mixed true/false manuals. No direction emits a true/false label.
  1124→5. **Resource loop:** Attempt to create qi, recovery, time, materials, or labor through repeated activation. Conservation and opportunity cost remain.
  1125→6. **Stage skipping:** Attempt Contact-to-Concord operations. Functional certification and envelope requirements remain.
  1126→7. **Combat privilege:** Clone the state to an NPC under normalized IDs. Results match. No minimum damage, immunity, aim, pause, or favorable RNG.
  1127→8. **Cross-realm attack:** The direction may improve information or coordination only where declared; it cannot supply missing force, authority, reach, survivability, or control.
  1128→9. **Mountain proof:** Contribution certificate is exactly zero structural mountain work beyond any separately declared efficiency inside the personal hard cap; H0 fails.
  1129→10. **Injury/death:** None prevents lethal damage, reverses death, or becomes a resurrection unless a future separate canon mechanism lawfully does so.
  1130→11. **Transfer/copy:** Theft, inheritance, possession, reincarnation, body copy, soul copy, name change, and save duplication follow the future identity model; no silent duplicates.
  1131→12. **Save/reload:** Costs, cooldown/recovery, trace decay, consent, and attempt history persist atomically.
  1132→13. **Exposure:** Repeated use creates observable evidence and differentiated audience beliefs; secrecy is active play, not a toggle.
  1133→14. **Late-game growth:** Increasing realm expands lawful inputs and resolution but never turns the operation into omniscience, free output, infinite time, or compulsory consent.
  1134→15. **Remove advantage:** Only declared feedback/holding/coordination disappears. Earned skill, ordinary memories, relationships, injuries, and world changes remain.

  1139→
  1140→Before this package can become provisional canon:
  1141→
  1142→### Capability-envelope gates
  1143→
  1144→- Qi Condensation as a realm has at least two actions that survive semantic novelty normalization, and every functional band has at least two distinct certification challenges rather than a numeric threshold alone;
  1145→- every quantitative dimension has unit, normal range, personal hard ceiling, burst/sustain distinction, failure, recovery, counter, and evidence plan;
  1146→- no test actor simultaneously receives incompatible maxima;
  1147→- personal Qi Condensation fails every H0 mountain split by all required dimensions;
  1148→- prepared/external/environmental mountain cases pass only with complete contribution ledgers and aftermath;
  1149→- 100% of tested player/NPC isomorphic cases match normalized behavioral outcomes; and
  1150→- repeated micro-actions, consumables, allies, and artifacts cannot launder personal progression.

  1152→### Golden-scenario gates
  1153→
  1154→- at least three one-year noncombat trajectories receive blinded “complete life” ratings from the future playtest threshold set;
  1155→- remaining mortal, seeking cultivation, and changing one's mind remain supported endings;
  1156→- every teacher can disappear without eliminating all advancement paths;
  1157→- the fraudulent manual has at least three independent detection paths and one plausible wrong inference;
  1158→- first perception requires repeatable evidence and admits false positives;
  1159→- first conflict supports meaningful combat, flight, negotiation, investigation, and repair families where world state permits;
  1160→- failed breakthrough is causally reconstructible and has at least two recovery strategies plus a viable abandonment path;
  1161→- first success changes verbs and relationships without unlocking any excluded capability; and
  1162→- state from the mortal year materially changes all six later scenarios.
  1163→
  1164→### Advantage-direction gates
  1165→
  1166→- each candidate enriches at least four golden scenarios without solving any automatically;
  1167→- each passes all 15 shared advantage tests;

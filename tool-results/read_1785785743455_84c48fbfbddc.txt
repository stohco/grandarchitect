     8→## 0. Why this document exists
     9→
    10→The bible has, until now, asserted that the Law Reaches (法域) exist (doc 15 §5), that they sit at the Precelestial's outer ring (doc 36 §4.4), that they were created by the Sundering (doc 37 §5), and that the Mahayana cultivator's endgame takes place in them (doc 15 §5.3, doc 26 §4.3). Two specific reaches were named in passing — the Reach of the Sundered Stratum (doc 37 §5.2, §7.1) and the Reach of the Caged Sky (doc 37 §7.2) — and one Mahayana's holding is described as "a single Law Reach in the deep Precelestial" (Mo Shen's, doc 39 §2.7), and one Mahayana's failed authorship collapsed a Reach (Lie Fa's, doc 39 §2.9, §4). Beyond these, the reaches were an unnamed collection.
    11→
    12→This document commits the names. There are nine reaches, symmetric with the nine Mahayana cultivators (a symmetry the pantheon perceives and does not entirely trust). Each reach is a specific place, with a specific qi-climate, a specific decay-state, a specific holder or non-holder, a specific history of authorship or non-authorship. The reaches are not interchangeable. The protagonist, traveling to one, finds a different cosmos than the protagonist traveling to another.
    13→
    14→**Conventions.** Hanzi appear in parentheses after the English term on first use. No pinyin romanization with tone marks. Distances in li where the reach is sub-heaven-stride scale; in heaven-strides (天步) where cosmic. The 1:365 Precelestial-to-Acquired time-debt (doc 15 §7) is preserved inside the reaches, with one exception (§2.8).

    18→## 1. How many reaches, and why
    19→
    20→There are **nine Law Reaches** (九法域). The count is not arbitrary, and it is not complete in any cosmic sense — it is the count of regions the pantheon has surveyed, named, and (in some cases) avoided for the last eight thousand years.
    21→
    22→**Why nine.** The Sundering (doc 37 §5.1) thinned the Precelestial's substrate-law across a vast but discontinuous region. The thinning did not produce a single wound; it produced nine distinct lesions, separated by stretches of substrate that held (sometimes barely). The pantheon's first survey (Gu Wu's, four thousand years ago, in the founding's aftermath) catalogued nine. Tu Nan's map (doc 39 §2.6) records nine. No new reach has formed since the Sundering, and no existing reach has fully healed — though several have been re-inscribed, partially or completely, by Mahayana authorship.
    23→
    24→**Why not more, not fewer.** A tenth reach exists in pantheon theory — the modeling (Shang Zhenkong's, per doc 38 §4.2) suggests that if the Thinning progresses, the substrate between the existing reaches will begin to fail, and the lesions will merge into a single ring-shaped wound at the Precelestial's outer margin. The modeling gives this merger approximately fifteen thousand years at the current Thinning-rate. The merger is the cosmology's terminal condition. The protagonist, in the Transcend ending, does not avert this; the protagonist's law delays it by a small, scoped amount.
    25→
    26→**The reaches' aggregate.** The nine reaches together cover roughly one-fifth of the Precelestial's horizontal extent (per doc 36 §4.1: ~3 heaven-strides across). The largest (the Reach of the Sundered Stratum) is roughly 2,000 li across at its widest; the smallest (the Reach of the Last Window, the protagonist's, §2.5) is barely 80 li across. They are not contiguous; the Spirit Wilds (doc 41) and the Precelestial's maintained regions fill the gaps between them.
    27→
    28→**The reaches and the Outer Margin.** All nine reaches border the cosmic Outer Margin (doc 36 §1.2) at some point along their outer arc. The Outer Margin is the slow taper of law beyond the Precelestial's edge — the place where the cosmos itself thins into the Unwritten (doc 36 §1.3). The reaches are the Precelestial's last inhabited frontier before that taper; a Mahayana cultivator standing at a reach's outer edge and looking outward perceives the Unwritten directly. The perception is the closest a living being can come to perceiving the Nameless Origin's generative function (per Lie Fa's account, doc 39 §2.9) without crossing into it.

    32→## 2. The nine, named
    33→
    34→Each reach is specified in seven fields: name, size, qi-climate, current holder, law authored (if any), the law's effect (if any), decay-state.
    35→
    36→### 2.1 The Reach of the Sundered Stratum (斷層法域) — the Largest
    37→
    38→**Name.** 斷層法域, "the Reach of the Broken Boundary." Named by the Courts' founders in the Sundering's immediate aftermath (Year ~40,000), the name recording what happened there: a boundary was broken, and the breaking made the reach.
    39→
    40→**Size.** Roughly 2,000 li across at its widest. The reach is irregular — a long, curved wound along the Precelestial's outer ring, deepest where Ming Kongsang and Wei Duchuan inscribed their fourth-stratum law (doc 37 §5.1), shallowing outward to either side. Its total area is approximately one-third of all reach-territory combined.
    41→
    42→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is not merely undifferentiated; it is *traumatized*. The Sundering's paradox-propagation left the substrate-law not just absent but actively distressed — a Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach's interior perceives the qi as a low, continuous keening, like a struck bell that has not stopped ringing in eight thousand years. The keening is not sound; it is the perception of the substrate's unresolved stress, the law-paradox's residue still propagating in slow motion. The qi itself, sampled and brought into a cultivated system, is usable but unstable — it integrates, then briefly de-integrates, then integrates again, in a rhythm the keening sets. Cultivators below Mahayana who sample the qi disperse within hours.
    43→
    44→**Current holder.** None. The reach is, by the pantheon's tacit consensus, a sealed zone — not because anyone has the authority to seal it, but because no one is fool enough to claim it. The Courts' Defense department (doc 38 §2.5) maintains watchposts along the reach's inner boundary (the side facing the maintained Precelestial) and rotates a Tribulation Crossing garrison through them every Acquired decade. The garrison does not cross the boundary. The watchposts' purpose is to perceive, early, if the Fourth-Stratum Attempt's Residue (doc 37 §7.1) — sealed at the reach's center by the founders, eight thousand years ago — begins to propagate. The seal's integrity is estimated at twenty thousand years, at the current Thinning-rate. The estimate may shorten.
    45→
    46→**Law authored.** The Fourth-Stratum Attempt (doc 37 §5.1, §7.1) — the law Ming Kongsang and Wei Duchuan inscribed, eight thousand years ago, that would have generated a fourth stratum. The law paradoxed; the authors dispersed; the law's residue persists, sealed, dormant but not dead. The seal is itself a law — Gu Wu's first post-bardo-window authorship, performed in coalition with the six Tribulation Crossing founders, eight thousand years ago. The seal is the only law that holds in the reach; everything else is paradox-residue.
    47→
    48→**What the law does.** The Fourth-Stratum Attempt, if it propagated, would begin generating a fourth stratum between the Precelestial and the Origin. The generation would split the Origin's output four ways instead of three; all three existing strata would thin below the cultivation threshold within centuries; the cosmos's qi-economy would collapse; every cultivator, every mortal, every being would die. The seal prevents this. The seal is the cosmos's most consequential maintained law — more consequential, even, than Gu Wu's bardo-window law, because the bardo-window law's failure produces suffering but not extinction, while the seal's failure produces extinction. The seal's maintenance is the Defense department's deepest responsibility.
    49→
    50→**Decay-state.** Stable, with a slow deterioration. The seal has held for eight thousand years; the Thinning is eroding it; the estimate (twenty thousand years remaining) is the pantheon's best modeling. The reach itself is not expanding — the substrate-law around the reach, while thinned, has held — but the seal's integrity is the slowly-failing thing. The protagonist, in the Transcend path, does not touch this reach. The reach is beyond the protagonist's scope. It is the cosmology's existential boundary, and the protagonist's small law does not reach it.
    51→
    52→### 2.2 The Reach of the Caged Sky (籠天法域) — the Prison
    53→
    54→**Name.** 籠天法域, "the Reach of the Caged Sky." Named three thousand years ago, when the Sky-Devouring Beast (吞天獸, doc 37 §7.2) was sealed here by a coalition of three Mahayana cultivators.
    55→
    56→**Size.** Approximately 500 li across, roughly circular, in the Precelestial's southern interior (doc 37 §7.2). It is the only reach not on the Precelestial's outer ring — it sits in the southern wilds' interior, a wound in the substrate that predates the Sundering and was, before the Beast's sealing, slowly healing. The sealing reversed the healing; the Beast's consumption of law-inscriptions during the sealing destabilized the substrate further, and the reach is now roughly the size it was at the Sundering's height.
    57→
    58→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is *eaten*. A cultivator standing in the reach perceives the qi as present but diminished, as if the qi itself were a thing that had been chewed. The perception is visceral and disturbing — the qi's texture is ragged, interrupted, full of small absences where the Beast's consumption has left residue-holes. The Beast, sealed at the reach's center, exerts a continuous low draw on the surrounding qi; the qi flows inward, toward the cage, like water toward a drain. A cultivator who stands too long in the reach's interior finds their own cultivated qi being drawn, slowly, toward the cage. Mahayana cultivators resist the draw without effort. Tribulation Crossing cultivators resist with effort. Spirit Severance cultivators and below do not enter the reach's interior; they disperse.
    59→
    60→**Current holder.** Held, in a sense, by two lineages — the surviving two of the three Mahayana-coalition that sealed the Beast (doc 37 §7.2). Neither lineage has a living Mahayana; the seal is maintained by their Tribulation Crossing cultivators, who rotate the maintenance in five-hundred-year shifts. The two lineages (the **Jinghe Lineage**, 涇河氏, and the **Wuqiu Lineage**, 烏秋氏) are aging; their last Tribulation Crossing cultivators are within a century of their natural span. The question of who maintains the seal after them is one of the pantheon's quiet emergencies — discussed in the Council of Nine, not yet resolved. The protagonist, if the protagonist reaches Mahayana and chooses the Caged Sky re-sealing commission (doc 37 §7.2), becomes the dispersed Mahayana's heir in the lineage's eyes. The choice is a content-design option, not a canonical commitment.
    61→
    62→**Law authored.** The Seal of the Caged Sky (籠天封), a coalition-authored Reach-law that maintains the Beast's containment. The seal is layered: the outermost layer is a substrate-stabilization law (the same kind of law that a Mahayana cultivator uses to stabilize a reach for authorship); the middle layer is a law that defines the cage's interior as a separate substrate-pocket (the only such pocket in the cosmos, per the pantheon's knowledge); the innermost layer is a law that matches the Beast's consumption-rate to the substrate's regeneration-rate (which is, in the Precelestial, essentially zero — the seal works by feeding the Beast just enough law-residue to keep it from breaking the cage, while not feeding it enough to grow). The seal is the most complex maintained law in the cosmos after the Fourth-Stratum Attempt's seal. It is also the most fragile.
    63→
    64→**What the law does.** Contains the Sky-Devouring Beast. The Beast is a substrate-predator (doc 37 §7.2) — it consumes law-inscriptions the way a beast consumes flesh. If the seal broke, the Beast would resume its consumption; the standing estimate is six to ten grotto-heavens consumed before a coalition could re-seal it. The Thinning has made the Beast hungrier.
    65→
    66→**Decay-state.** Slowly deteriorating. The seal loses integrity at approximately the rate the Thinning thins the substrate — the pantheon's modeling gives the seal another twelve hundred years at the current rate, after which the seal fails and the Beast breaches. The protagonist's law does not directly address this; the protagonist's intervention (the bardo-harvest-loophole amendment) conserves qi-authority across the Acquired Stratum, and the conservation's trickle-effect extends (marginally) to the Precelestial's substrate-density. The pantheon's modeling, recalculated after the protagonist's law, gives the Caged Sky seal another fifteen hundred years. The marginal three centuries are the protagonist's small, scoped contribution to the cosmology's existential maintenance. The contribution is not salvation. It is tending.
    67→
    68→### 2.3 The Reach of the Held Silence (默持法域) — Mo Shen's
    69→
    70→**Name.** 默持法域, "the Reach of the Held (or Tended) Silence." Named by the pantheon, six thousand years ago, when Mo Shen took the reach as their holding (doc 39 §2.7). The name records Mo Shen's practice: the silence is held, not absent. Mo Shen's restraint is the holding.
    71→
    72→**Size.** Roughly 300 li across, irregular, in the deep Precelestial's central interior — not at the outer ring. This is unusual: the reach predates the Sundering (it is one of the two reaches, with the Caged Sky, that the Sundering did not create), and its presence in the interior is a pre-Sundering wound, a place where the substrate-law simply never formed. The reason is unknown. The pantheon has speculated (Gu Wu most often) that this reach is the closest thing in the cosmos to a "natural" Law Reach — a place where the Origin's generative function, for reasons no being has perceived, did not inscribe substrate-law. The reach is the pantheon's evidence that not all reaches are wounds; some are absences.
    73→
    74→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is the cleanest undifferentiated qi in the cosmos. There is no trauma (as in the Sundered Stratum), no consumption (as in the Caged Sky), no collapse-residue (as in the Cleaving). The qi is simply present, undifferentiated, awaiting inscription that has not come. A Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach perceives a silence so profound it has weight — the weight of a held breath, sustained for six thousand years, sustained by Mo Shen's practice. The silence is not empty; it is full of the *possibility* of law. The possibility is what Mo Shen tends.
    75→
    76→**Current holder.** Mo Shen (默深, the Hermit, doc 39 §2.7). Mo Shen has held this reach for nine thousand years (longer than the reach has been named; Mo Shen took the reach three thousand years before the pantheon named it). Mo Shen's holding is not an authorship; it is a presence. Mo Shen sits in the reach's center, in silence, and the reach's silence is Mo Shen's contribution. The other eight Mahayana cultivators perceive Mo Shen's restraint as a standing law — the law of not-writing, the law of leaving the substrate as one found it. This law is not inscribed; it is performed. The performance is the law.
    77→
    78→**Law authored.** None. This is the point. The Held Silence is the only reach in the cosmos where no law has been inscribed, by Mo Shen's deliberate choice, for nine thousand years. The reach is the pantheon's neutral ground — the Council of Nine (doc 39 §7) convenes here, when it convenes, because no Mahayana cultivator's law holds here and therefore no Mahayana cultivator has home-court advantage. The neutrality is Mo Shen's gift to the pantheon.
    79→
    80→**What the law does.** The non-law does several things, none of them authored: it provides the pantheon's only neutral meeting-ground; it provides a place where a Mahayana cultivator can perceive what substrate-without-law feels like (a perception essential to responsible authorship, and one the pantheon recommends to any newly-ascended Mahayana); it provides, in Mo Shen's restraint, a standing rebuke to the other eight's productivity. The non-law is, in the pantheon's collective understanding, Mo Shen's contribution to the cosmos's law-substrate. The contribution is paradoxical — a law that is not a law, an authorship that is not an authorship. The paradox is the point.
    81→
    82→**Decay-state.** Stable, by Mo Shen's tending. The reach's substrate is, paradoxically, the most stable reach-substrate in the cosmos, because it has not been stressed by inscription and paradox-residue. Mo Shen's presence is itself a stabilizing factor — a Mahayana cultivator's anchor, sustained in the reach's center, provides a slow trickle of qi-authority that maintains the substrate's coherence without inscribing law. The reach is, in this sense, the only reach that is healing — not by inscription but by presence. The healing is so slow it is imperceptible; the modeling suggests the reach will fully heal in approximately fifty thousand years, longer than the cosmos's remaining span. The healing will not complete. The tending will outlast the cosmos.
    83→
    84→### 2.4 The Reach of the Cleaving (裂法法域) — Lie Fa's Ruins
    85→
    86→**Name.** 裂法法域, "the Reach of the Cleaving (or Cleaving-the-Law)." Named by the pantheon three thousand years ago, in the reach's collapse's aftermath. The name records both Lie Fa's name (裂法, "Cleaving Law") and what Lie Fa did: cleaved the law, and the cleaving broke the reach.
    87→
    88→**Size.** Originally approximately 400 li across; currently irregular, with the interior collapsed to a region of approximately 80 li of stable half-law surrounded by a larger zone of unstable fragments. The collapse was not total (per doc 39 §4); the partial inscription Lie Fa attempted persists, in ruins. The ruins are the 80-li core. The surrounding zone — the reach's original extent minus the collapsed core — is a region of partial law-fragments, half-inscribed rules that flicker in and out of coherence.
    89→
    90→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is *fractured*. A Mahayana cultivator standing in the ruins perceives the qi not as a single medium but as a fractured thing, full of small discontinuities — the qi-integrity is broken at thousands of micro-sites, each one a place where Lie Fa's paradox propagated and left a residue-fracture. The fractures are not stable; they shift, slowly, in patterns that correspond (per Tu Nan's mapping, doc 39 §2.6) to Lie Fa's partial law-inscription's residual structure. The qi, sampled, produces in a Mahayana cultivator's perception a vertigo specific to this reach: the perception of *almost-law*, of rules that almost hold but do not, of a substrate that almost inscribed itself but failed. The vertigo is the perception of Lie Fa's error, still propagating. The vertigo is contagious (per doc 39 §4): a cultivator who samples the qi too long begins to perceive the recursion that broke Lie Fa, and the perception, once begun, is difficult to un-perceive.
    91→
    92→**Current holder.** None, in any conventional sense. Lie Fa (裂法, the Fallen, doc 39 §2.9, §4) is the ruins — Lie Fa's fractured anchor, partially in the reach's collapsed core and partially in the Precelestial, is the closest thing the reach has to a holder. Lie Fa does not hold the reach; Lie Fa is the reach's wound. The pantheon's debate (doc 39 §2.9) over whether to disperse Lie Fa or preserve them has held for three thousand years; the debate is, in part, a debate over whether the reach can be re-inscribed (which would require Lie Fa's dispersion first) or whether the reach must remain a sealed zone (which requires Lie Fa's preservation). The debate is unresolved.
    93→
    94→**Law authored.** A partial Reach-law (殘法, per doc 39 §2.9), three thousand years ago — Lie Fa's attempt to mimic the Nameless Origin's generative function within a single reach. The law was an attempt to create a self-renewing pocket of cosmos, finite but self-sustaining, exempt from the Thinning. The law paradoxed (per doc 39 §4); the paradox was recursive; the recursion was at the level of what perception itself is. The law's partial inscription persists, in fragments, in the reach's collapsed core. The fragments are, by the pantheon's consensus, the most dangerous text in the cosmos.
    95→
    96→**What the law does.** Nothing, in its current fragmented state, except cause harm. The fragments, read by a Mahayana cultivator with the law-legacy perception (doc 30 Station 10), begin to induce the recursion-perception that broke Lie Fa. The perception is not immediate; it accumulates. A Mahayana cultivator who reads the fragments for too long begins to perceive, faintly, the Origin's generative function as Lie Fa perceived it — as something that could be authored — and the perception, once begun, tends toward the conclusion that Lie Fa reached: that the authoring is possible, that the previous failure was a flaw of execution rather than a flaw of conception, that the next attempt might succeed. The conclusion is the recursion's trap. Every previous reader who reached the conclusion has either dispersed themselves (in attempted authorship) or been dispersed by the pantheon (in defensive intervention). The fragments are, for this reason, locked behind a pantheon-maintained seal at the reach's edge; the seal is the only law the pantheon maintains in the reach. The protagonist, if they reach the ruins, may petition the pantheon for reading-access. The pantheon, in the protagonist's era, is deadlocked (doc 39 §6) and does not respond. The protagonist may read at their own risk. The risk is the recursion.
    97→
    98→**Decay-state.** Slowly collapsing inward. The 80-li stable core is stable in the sense that it is not currently collapsing further; it is not stable in the sense that the fractures around it continue to propagate, slowly, into the core's edges. The modeling (Tu Nan's) gives the core another four thousand years before the fractures propagate to its center and the core collapses fully. Full collapse would disperse Lie Fa (which would resolve the pantheon's three-thousand-year debate by force) and would also unwrite the partial law-inscription (which would resolve the recursion-trap by removing the text). The pantheon, per its deadlock, has not decided whether to wait for the collapse or to intervene. The protagonist's visit does not change the modeling's trajectory.
    99→
   100→### 2.5 The Reach of the Last Window (末窗法域) — the Protagonist's
   101→
   102→**Name.** 末窗法域, "the Reach of the Last Window." Named by the protagonist, in the Transcend ending's aftermath, for the bardo-window law the protagonist's authorship amends (per doc 39 §5.2, doc 26 §7.2). The name is the protagonist's own. The pantheon ratifies it, slowly, in the protagonist's first decade as the ninth.
   103→
   104→**Size.** Approximately 80 li across, roughly oval, at the Precelestial's outer ring in the region that corresponds (per doc 36 §5.4's horizontal-coordinate preservation) to the Cangli Riverlands' upper stratum. This is why the protagonist chooses it: the reach's horizontal coordinate corresponds to the mortal region where Wang Xiaodi died, where the bardo-harvest loophole operated, where the protagonist's grief is inscribed in the mortal world. The protagonist, choosing the reach, is choosing to author the law in the place the law's harm was done. The choice is not required by the authorship's mechanics (Acquired-authorship amendments can be inscribed from anywhere); the choice is the protagonist's own poetic closure.
   105→
   106→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is *patient*. The reach is small, the substrate-law's thinning is moderate (not as severe as the Sundered Stratum, not as clean as the Held Silence), and the qi-climate is best described as a quiet waiting. A Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach perceives the qi as a medium that has been waiting, for eight thousand years, for inscription — not with the eager possibility of the Held Silence, but with a tired patience, the patience of a substrate that has held its shape through the Sundering's shockwave and the Thinning's slow erosion and now simply is, awaiting whatever comes. The reach's qi, sampled, integrates cleanly into a Mahayana cultivator's system. The reach is, in this sense, the most authorship-ready reach in the cosmos — the substrate is not too traumatized (like the Sundered Stratum), not too consumed (like the Caged Sky), not too fractured (like the Cleaving), not too silent (like the Held Silence, where Mo Shen's restraint discourages authorship). The reach has been waiting for someone to write in it.
   107→
   108→**Current holder.** Before the protagonist's authorship: none. The reach is unclaimed — small, unremarkable, overlooked by the pantheon for eight thousand years. You Wuya has walked through it (per doc 39 §2.3, You Wuya has walked every reach), and left it without comment. Tu Nan's map records it as "Reach 5 — minor, unclaimed, stable." The reach was, before the protagonist, the most unremarkable reach in the cosmos.
   109→
   110→After the protagonist's authorship: the protagonist. The protagonist's holding is not formalized (the protagonist does not yet have the pantheon's full recognition as the ninth, in the immediate aftermath); the holding is, simply, the protagonist's law, inscribed in the reach's substrate. The law is the holding. The reach is the law's body.
   111→
   112→**Law authored.** The protagonist's bardo-harvest-loophole amendment (per doc 26 §4.3, doc 39 §5.2). The amendment is, technically, an Acquired-authorship amendment to Gu Wu's bardo-window law (中有期限法); it is not a Reach-law. The protagonist chooses to inscribe the amendment *from* the reach (rather than from anywhere) for the insulation the reach provides during the law-tribulation (per doc 39 §5.2). The insulation is real — the reach's thinned substrate absorbs some of the tribulation's paradox-propagation, reducing the tribulation's lethality by an estimated (Shang Zhenkong's modeling) fifteen percent. The reduction is small. The reduction is, by the protagonist's calculation, the difference between surviving and not.
   113→
   114→The amendment, inscribed from the reach, leaves residue in the reach's substrate — a residue-trace of the protagonist's authorship, the protagonist's grief, the protagonist's qi-signature. The residue is the protagonist's holding, in the practical sense. The residue is what marks the reach as the protagonist's.
   115→
   116→**What the law does.** Closes the bardo-harvest loophole (per doc 26 §4.3, doc 39 §5.2). The amendment specifies that an anchor whose bardo-window has been extended by any means other than natural anchor-coherence is, upon the extension's discovery, immediately released to its lawful dispersal. The amendment's scope is, by the protagonist's authorship, the Cangli Riverlands; by precedent and pantheon-ratification, the broader Acquired Stratum. The amendment's effect, when it takes effect, is to empty every harvest-store in its scope — Pei Liang's harvest-store, and the harvest-stores of every other demonic cultivator in the Cangli Riverlands — lawfully, the anchors dispersing to their next rebirths. Among them, Wang Xiaodi's. The protagonist perceives Wang Xiaodi's dispersal, in the reach's silence, in the law-tribulation's aftermath, and weeps. The weeping is the Transcend ending's emotional core.
   117→
   118→**Decay-state.** Before the authorship: stable, slowly thinning (the Thinning's standard effect). After the authorship: healing. The protagonist's inscription, like any inscription, stabilizes the substrate around it; the protagonist's law's qi-authority (the amendment's enforcement generates a small qi-flow, the way Gu Wu's bardo-window law does) sustains the reach's substrate. The reach, after the authorship, is the only reach in the cosmos (besides the Held Silence, by Mo Shen's different mechanism) that is healing. The healing is slow; the modeling gives the reach approximately ten thousand years to fully heal, longer than the protagonist's natural span. The protagonist will not see the healing complete. The protagonist's successors — if there are successors — will tend the healing.

   120→### 2.6 The Reach of the Wandering Edge (游涯法域) — You Wuya's Approach
   121→
   122→**Name.** 游涯法域, "the Reach of the Wandering Edge." Named by You Wuya (游無涯, the Wanderer, doc 39 §2.3), one thousand years ago, when You Wuya began walking toward the Origin's edge through this reach. The name is You Wuya's own; the pantheon has not ratified it (You Wuya does not seek ratification), but Tu Nan's map records the name in deference to You Wuya's presence.
   123→
   124→**Size.** Approximately 600 li across, narrow and elongated, extending from the Precelestial's outer ring outward toward the cosmic Outer Margin (doc 36 §1.2). The reach is, in shape, a finger of thinned substrate reaching toward the Unwritten — the only reach that extends *outward* rather than sitting *at* the outer ring. The reach's outer tip is, by the pantheon's collective perception, the closest any living being has come to the Origin's generative function without crossing into it.
   125→
   126→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is *drawn*. The reach's outer tip exerts a continuous pull on the qi — not the pull of consumption (as in the Caged Sky) but the pull of *gravity*, the same gravity Gu Wu perceives as the Origin's pull (per doc 39 §2.1) but concentrated and directional. A Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach perceives the qi flowing outward, toward the Unwritten, at a slow but measurable rate. The flow is the Origin's generative function, perceived as a current. You Wuya walks the flow; You Wuya's walking is, per the pantheon's collective understanding, You Wuya's approach to the Origin. The reach's qi, sampled, produces in a Mahayana cultivator the perception of being drawn — a perception Gu Wu has reported for five hundred years, but concentrated here to a degree Gu Wu does not experience in their court. The perception is, for most Mahayana cultivators, uncomfortable. For You Wuya, the perception is the destination.
   127→
   128→**Current holder.** You Wuya, in the loosest sense of "holder." You Wuya does not claim the reach (You Wuya claims nothing, per doc 39 §2.3); You Wuya walks the reach, repeatedly, on their way to the outer tip. The pantheon perceives You Wuya's repeated walking as a kind of holding — a presence sustained over centuries, the way Mo Shen's presence sustains the Held Silence. You Wuya's presence is, however, ambulatory where Mo Shen's is stationary. The reach is held by a walking, not a sitting. The difference matters.
   129→
   130→**Law authored.** None, by You Wuya or any other. You Wuya's Dao (doc 39 §2.3) does not include Reach-authorship; You Wuya's only authored law (the place-spirit jurisdiction law, 土地轄界法) is an Acquired-authorship. The reach remains un-inscribed. You Wuya has not inscribed a law here because You Wuya does not perceive inscription as the appropriate response to the reach's qi-climate; You Wuya perceives the appropriate response as walking.
   131→
   132→**What the law does.** Nothing has been authored. The reach's only "law" is the natural law of the Origin's pull, which the reach amplifies by its shape and orientation. This natural law does, however, do something: it draws things outward. The reach's interior is, gradually, being emptied — the qi flowing outward toward the Unwritten, the substrate slowly thinning at the outer tip. A cultivator below Mahayana who enters the reach is drawn outward; if they do not turn back, they reach the outer tip and disperse into the Unwritten. The dispersal is not painful (per Bei Huai's assessment, doc 39 §2.8, who has attended two such dispersals in the last millennium); the dispersal is, simply, the being's cessation as the substrate's support withdraws. The reach is, in this sense, a place to die. Some cultivators enter it for exactly this purpose.
   133→
   134→**Decay-state.** Expanding outward. The reach is, very slowly, growing — the outer tip extends into the Unwritten at a rate Tu Nan estimates at one li per Precelestial century. The expansion is the reach's substrate thinning into the Unwritten at its outer edge, while the inner edge (at the Precelestial's outer ring) remains stable. The reach is, in this sense, a wound that is slowly extending toward the Origin. The pantheon's modeling does not predict the wound's completion — the modeling stops at the question of what happens when the outer tip reaches the Origin's generative function. The question is Lie Fa's question, approached by walking rather than by authorship. You Wuya is, by the pantheon's collective assessment, the being most likely to answer the question, if any being does. The answer, if You Wuya reaches the tip and does not disperse, will be the pantheon's most consequential information in eight thousand years. The pantheon does not know whether You Wuya will reach the tip. You Wuya does not know either. You Wuya walks.

   136→### 2.7 The Reach of the Mirror Tides (鏡潮法域) — the Oscillator
   137→
   138→**Name.** 鏡潮法域, "the Reach of the Mirror Tides." Named by Tu Nan (圖南, the Cartographer, doc 39 §2.6), four thousand years ago, in the pantheon's first survey. The name records the reach's qi-climate, which moves in tides, and which reflects.
   139→
   140→**Size.** Approximately 350 li across, roughly circular, at the Precelestial's outer ring in the region corresponding to the eastern continent's northern shore. The reach is the only reach with a regularly oscillating substrate-law — the substrate-law here is not absent (as in most reaches) but *partial*, half-formed and half-dissolved, in a cycle.
   141→
   142→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is *tidal*. The reach's substrate-law oscillates between two states: a half-inscribed state, in which fragments of law briefly cohere (the fragments are not any law the pantheon recognizes; they are *partial* laws, rules that almost hold but do not, in patterns that correspond to no authored law), and a half-dissolved state, in which the fragments dissipate back into undifferentiated qi. The cycle's period is approximately one Precelestial day (= one Acquired year). A Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach perceives the qi as a tide — the law-fragments rising, peaking, receding, in a rhythm the reach has maintained for eight thousand years. The fragments, at the tide's peak, are briefly readable; a Mahayana cultivator with the law-legacy perception can, at the peak, perceive the fragments' content (which is, per Tu Nan's cataloguing, never the same content twice — the fragments are different each cycle, in patterns that Tu Nan has been mapping for four thousand years without discerning the rule). The qi, sampled, produces in a Mahayana cultivator the perception of almost-understanding — the perception of a law that almost makes sense, that almost coheres, that almost could be authored. The perception is, for some Mahayana cultivators, addictive. Tu Nan has visited the reach more than any other pantheon member; Tu Nan's visits are, per the other eight's quiet assessment, the closest thing Tu Nan has to a hobby.
   143→
   144→**Current holder.** None. The reach is unclaimed, and the pantheon has a tacit agreement not to claim it — the reach's oscillation is too unpredictable to safely inscribe in, and the law-fragments' content (which might, in theory, be the substrate's attempt to inscribe itself) is too valuable to overwrite. The reach is, in this sense, a research site. Tu Nan visits; You Wuya walks through; the other seven avoid it (the almost-understanding perception is, for most of them, uncomfortable).
   145→
   146→**Law authored.** None. The reach's law-fragments are not authored; they are, in the pantheon's best theory, the substrate's own attempts at self-inscription — the Origin's generative function, acting on the reach's thinned substrate, producing partial laws that almost cohere. The theory is unconfirmed. The fragments, if they are the substrate's self-inscription, are the only direct evidence the pantheon has of the Origin's generative function in action. The evidence is, however, fragmentary and unreadable.
   147→
   148→**What the law does.** Nothing, in any maintained sense. The fragments' effects, at the tide's peak, are local and brief — a Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach at the peak perceives, for the peak's duration (approximately one Precelestial hour = approximately fifteen Acquired days), a faintly altered law-state, in which the normal rules of the Precelestial's substrate-law are slightly bent. The bending is different each cycle. The bending is, in some cycles, beneficial (a Mahayana cultivator's law-maintenance is, at the peak, slightly easier); in some cycles, harmful (a Mahayana cultivator's anchor-coherence is, at the peak, slightly strained); in most cycles, neutral. The reach is, in this sense, a place to perceive the substrate's self-inscription in action. The perception is the reach's only consistent product.
   149→
   150→**Decay-state.** Stable, in its oscillation. The reach is not healing (the oscillation is not converging toward full inscription) and not collapsing (the oscillation is not diverging toward full dissolution). The reach is, in this sense, the only reach in a stable equilibrium — neither healing nor decaying, neither expanding nor shrinking. The equilibrium is, per Tu Nan's modeling, sustainable indefinitely. The reach will, if the equilibrium holds, continue oscillating until the substrate itself thins to nothing (in the Thinning's terminal phase). The reach is, in this sense, the most patient thing in the cosmos.

   152→### 2.8 The Reach of the Empty Hourglass (空漏法域) — the Time-Trap
   153→
   154→**Name.** 空漏法域, "the Reach of the Empty Hourglass." Named by the pantheon, two thousand years ago, after the third surveying expedition (a Tribulation Crossing cultivator named **Shi Kong** (時空, "Time-Space"), dispatched by Tu Nan) returned from the reach having aged two hundred years in a single Precelestial day. Shi Kong survived (barely); Shi Kong's report named the reach.
   155→
   156→**Size.** Approximately 250 li across, irregularly elongated, at the Precelestial's outer ring in the region corresponding to the western continent's interior. The reach is the smallest of the unclaimed reaches and the most dangerous to enter below Mahayana.
   157→
   158→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is *fast*. The reach's substrate-law has, in addition to its thinning, a peculiar distortion of the Precelestial's time-debt (doc 15 §7). The standard Precelestial time-debt is 1:365 (1 Precelestial day = 1 Acquired year); the reach's time-debt is approximately 1:36,500 (1 reach-day = 100 Acquired years). The distortion is not uniform — the reach's interior has faster time-debt than its edges, and the time-debt fluctuates with the substrate's thinning. A Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach perceives the qi as a flowing thing, faster than the Precelestial's standard qi-flow, with a peculiar *velocity* that the perception reads as time-acceleration. The qi, sampled, produces in a Mahayana cultivator the perception of time rushing — the perception that the Acquired Stratum below is, from the reach, a blur of births and deaths and dynasties. The perception is vertiginous. The perception is, for some Mahayana cultivators, the closest they come to perceiving the Thinning directly — the time-rushing is the Thinning's time-component, the recognition that the cosmos's finite span is being spent.
   159→
   160→**Current holder.** None. The reach is unclaimed, and the pantheon has a tacit agreement not to claim it — the time-distortion makes inscription unstable (a law inscribed in the reach's substrate would, by the time-distortion, age at one hundred times the standard rate, and would erode into incoherence within a Precelestial decade). The reach is, in this sense, uninhabitable for authored law. The reach is also uninhabitable for cultivators below Mahayana — Shi Kong's survival was, by the pantheon's assessment, exceptional; the four subsequent surveying expeditions (all Tribulation Crossing cultivators) dispersed.
   161→
   162→**Law authored.** None. The reach's time-distortion makes authorship impractical, and no Mahayana cultivator has been willing to risk the time-cost (a Mahayana cultivator authoring a law in the reach would, by the time-distortion, experience one hundred Acquired years of subjective time per Precelestial day of authorship; the law-tribulation, which already tests the cultivator's anchor-coherence, would test it at one hundred times the standard rate). The reach remains un-inscribed.
   163→
   164→**What the law does.** Nothing, in any maintained sense. The reach's natural law (the time-distortion) does, however, do something: it produces *time*. Specifically, the reach's time-acceleration, perceived from outside, is a slow leakage of the Precelestial's standard time-rate into the Acquired Stratum's standard time-rate — the reach is, in this sense, a place where the stratum-boundary's time-debt is partially un-written. The leakage is, per Shang Zhenkong's modeling, a small contributor to the Acquired Stratum's time-flow irregularities (the irregularities are mostly natural; the reach's contribution is approximately 0.3% of the total). The leakage is, in the modeling's prediction, slowly accelerating as the Thinning thins the substrate. The acceleration is too slow to matter in any cultivator's lifetime.
   165→
   166→**Decay-state.** Slowly expanding. The reach's time-distortion is, very gradually, extending outward — the reach's edges are slowly thinning into faster time-debt, and the reach is, per Tu Nan's mapping, growing at approximately one li per Precelestial century. The growth is the slowest of any expanding reach. The growth is, per the modeling, sustainable for approximately twenty thousand years, after which the reach's time-distortion will have expanded far enough to intersect the nearest maintained road (the road to the Western Ancestral Courts), at which point the road's time-rate will begin to distort. The pantheon has not yet decided what to do about this. The protagonist's law does not address it.

   168→### 2.9 The Reach of the Sundered Breath (斷息法域) — the Bleeding Wound
   169→
   170→**Name.** 斷息法域, "the Reach of the Broken Breath." Named by the pantheon, three thousand years ago, after the second survey of the reach's interior revealed that the reach's substrate was actively *leaking* — losing qi into the Unwritten at a measurable rate, the way a wound bleeds.
   171→
   172→**Size.** Approximately 1,000 li across, irregularly shaped, at the Precelestial's outer ring in the region corresponding to the southern continent's southern coast. The reach is the second-largest (after the Sundered Stratum) and the only reach that is actively *expanding* at a rate the pantheon perceives as concerning.
   173→
   174→**Qi-climate.** The qi here is *bleeding*. The reach's substrate is not merely thinned; it is *losing substance* — the qi, in the reach's interior, flows outward toward the Unwritten at a rate Tu Nan estimates at approximately one percent of the reach's total qi per Precelestial century. The flow is the substrate's substance, leaking into the Unwritten, where it disperses into the Origin's generative function (per Lie Fa's account, doc 39 §2.9 — the Origin is the condition of substrate, and the substrate's substance, leaked into the Origin, ceases to be substrate). A Mahayana cultivator standing in the reach perceives the qi as a flowing-outward thing, with a *direction* (toward the Unwritten) and a *rate* (the one-percent-per-century that Tu Nan measures). The qi, sampled, produces in a Mahayana cultivator the perception of loss — the perception of substance draining, of the cosmos's finite capacity being spent. The perception is the Thinning, concentrated and localized. The perception is, for most Mahayana cultivators, the most unpleasant perception the cosmos offers. Yin Mu (茵母, the Gardener, doc 39 §2.5) has visited the reach once, eight hundred years ago, and has not returned; the reach is, per Yin Mu's account, the place where the Thinning is most clearly the cosmos's grief for itself.
   175→
   176→**Current holder.** None. The reach is unclaimed, and the pantheon has a tacit agreement not to claim it — the reach's bleeding makes inscription unstable (a law inscribed in the reach's substrate would, by the bleeding, be slowly carried outward into the Unwritten, and would erode into incoherence within a Precelestial millennium). The reach is, in this sense, uninhabitable for authored law. The reach is also uninhabitable for cultivators below Mahayana — the bleeding draws the cultivator's qi outward, and a Tribulation Crossing cultivator in the reach's interior disperses within a Precelestial week (= seven Acquired years). Shi Kong (the same Shi Kong who survived the Empty Hourglass) surveyed the Sundered Breath's edges for a Precelestial month; the survey cost Shi Kong forty Acquired years of subjective time and a permanent qi-depletion that shortened Shi Kong's natural span by an estimated two hundred years. Shi Kong's report is the pantheon's primary source on the reach.
   177→
   178→**Law authored.** None. The reach's bleeding makes authorship impractical. The reach remains un-inscribed. The pantheon has, in the last millennium, debated whether to author a substrate-stabilization law in the reach's interior — a law that would seal the bleeding the way a bandage seals a wound. The debate is unresolved; the law would require a coalition (no single Mahayana cultivator could maintain it alone), the coalition has not formed (the deadlock, per doc 39 §6), and the bleeding continues.
   179→
   180→**What the law does.** Nothing, in any maintained sense. The reach's natural law (the bleeding) does, however, do something: it contributes to the Thinning. The reach's qi-loss is, per Shang Zhenkong's modeling, approximately five percent of the Precelestial's total Thinning-rate. The reach is, in this sense, a *symptom* of the Thinning and a *cause* of the Thinning, simultaneously — the reach exists because the substrate is thinning, and the substrate thins faster because the reach exists. The recursion is, per the modeling, slowly amplifying. The amplification is the cosmology's deepest concern after the Fourth-Stratum Attempt's seal.
   181→
   182→**Decay-state.** Expanding, at approximately three li per Precelestial century. The reach is, by Tu Nan's mapping, the fastest-expanding reach in the cosmos. The expansion is the bleeding's outward progress — the substrate, at the reach's edges, is being drawn into the reach's bleeding interior. The modeling predicts the reach will, in approximately five thousand years, intersect the nearest ancestral court (the **Southern Mountain Ancestral Court**, 南山宗祠, of a minor lineage), at which point the ancestral court will be forced to relocate or disperse. The pantheon has not yet decided what to do about this. The protagonist's law does not address it. The reach is, in this sense, the cosmology's slow-motion emergency — the wound that has not stopped bleeding for eight thousand years and that no one has the coalition to close.

   186→## 3. The protagonist's Transcend-ending reach — what authorship feels like, what comes after
   187→
   188→The protagonist's authorship takes place in the Reach of the Last Window (§2.5). The authorship is the Transcend ending's central event (per doc 26 §4.3, doc 39 §5.2). This section specifies the authorship's phenomenology and aftermath.
   189→
   190→### 3.1 The authorship's preparation
   191→
   192→The protagonist, having crossed the tribulation and reached Mahayana (per doc 30 Station 10), having met Bei Huai (doc 39 §5.1) and learned from Gu Wu (doc 39 §5.2), travels to the reach. The travel takes a Precelestial month (= thirty Acquired years), by which time the protagonist's mortal-time acquaintances (Wang Meili's grandchildren, the Azure Sword Sect's current generation) have aged past recognition. The time-debt (doc 15 §7) is, from this point forward, the protagonist's permanent condition.
   193→
   194→The protagonist, arriving at the reach, walks its 80-li extent once, on foot, perceiving the qi-climate (the patient waiting, §2.5). The walk takes a Precelestial day. The protagonist chooses the reach's center as the authorship-site — a slight rise in the substrate, no larger than a mortal's hand, where the qi-climate is most concentrated. The protagonist sits.
   195→
   196→### 3.2 The authorship's phenomenology
   197→
   198→The authorship itself — the inscription of the bardo-harvest-loophole amendment into the Acquired Stratum's law-substrate, performed *from* the reach — is, by the accounts of Mahayana cultivators who have performed authorship, the most vertiginous verb in cultivation. The protagonist's account (recorded, later, by Tu Nan for the map-room) specifies the following:
   199→
   200→**The vertigo of the unwritten.** The protagonist, beginning the inscription, perceives the reach's substrate as it actually is — not as a thinned region of the Precelestial's substrate-law, but as a *pending* region, a region where the law could be anything. The perception is the vertigo of possibility: the law the protagonist is about to inscribe is one law among an infinitude of possible laws, and the infinitude is, for the moment of the inscription's beginning, perceivable. The protagonist's Residual Error Sense (doc 00 §5), strained past its design, perceives the infinitude as a kind of light — not visible light, but a luminosity of possibility, the recognition that the substrate here could be anything and that the protagonist's choice is one of the choices.
   201→
   202→**The grief's inscription.** The protagonist's amendment is, at its core, the protagonist's grief for Wang Xiaodi — the grief that has driven the protagonist since session 1, the grief that the bardo-harvest loophole exploited. The inscription, as the protagonist performs it, writes the grief into the substrate. The grief is not the law's content (the law's content is a rule about bardo-windows and harvest-stores); the grief is the law's *texture*, the quality the law's enforcement will have. The protagonist's law will, when it takes effect, disperse the harvested anchors with a specific quality — a quality of release, of allowing, of the forty-nine-day window closing as it was always meant to close. The quality is the protagonist's grief, transmuted into law. The transmutation is the authorship's deepest verb.
   203→
   204→**The law-tribulation.** The inscription triggers the law-tribulation (doc 15 §6.3 form 3). The tribulation tests the law's compatibility with the deeper law of the Precelestial's substrate (and, beyond the substrate, the Nameless Origin's generative function). The tribulation is, by the protagonist's account, the most intense experience of the protagonist's cultivation — a year of subjective time (the tribulation's duration, by Acquired time; a Precelestial day, by Precelestial time) in which the protagonist's anchor is tested against the substrate's resistance. The reach's insulation (§2.5) absorbs approximately fifteen percent of the tribulation's paradox-propagation, reducing the tribulation's lethality. The protagonist survives.
   205→
   206→**The law's taking-effect.** The tribulation, survived, ratifies the law. The law takes effect across the Cangli Riverlands — the protagonist's scope — and, by precedent, across the broader Acquired Stratum. The taking-effect is, the protagonist perceives, a *settling* — the substrate, in the reach's interior and across the Cangli Riverlands' correspondence, accepts the law, and the law's qi-authority begins to flow. The flow is the law's enforcement. The flow is, in the reach's silence, audible — a low hum, the sound of the law working, sustained by the protagonist's anchor.
   207→
   208→**The dispersal's perception.** The protagonist, in the law's taking-effect, perceives the harvest-stores emptying — across the Cangli Riverlands, the harvested anchors (hundreds of them, accumulated across Pei Liang's centuries of cultivation) dispersing lawfully into the cosmos's law-substrate, going to their next rebirths. Among them, Wang Xiaodi's. The protagonist perceives Wang Xiaodi's anchor one last time — recognizes, in the anchor's residue, the traces of the infant the protagonist practiced near decades ago (per doc 26 §4.3) — and weeps. The weeping is the Transcend ending's emotional core. The weeping is not grief alone; the weeping is recognition — the recognition that the protagonist's grief has become the world's law, and the law is the world's love, and the love is the protagonist's, and Wang Xiaodi's, and the cosmos's, finite and tended.
   209→
   210→### 3.3 The reach, after the authorship
   211→
   212→The reach, after the protagonist's inscription, is *changed*. The change is gradual; the change is, in the protagonist's lifetime, perceivable.
   213→
   214→**The healing.** The protagonist's law's qi-authority sustains the reach's substrate (per §2.5). The reach begins, slowly, to heal — the substrate-law, thinned by the Sundering and the Thinning, thickens marginally around the protagonist's inscription. The healing is slow (ten thousand years to completion, per the modeling); the healing is, however, the only healing the cosmos's reaches have seen in eight thousand years (besides the Held Silence's, by Mo Shen's different mechanism). The healing is the protagonist's contribution to the cosmology's maintenance. The contribution is small. The contribution is, by the pantheon's collective assessment, the most hopeful event in the cosmos in the last millennium.
   215→
   216→**The residue.** The protagonist's inscription leaves a residue-trace in the reach's substrate — a permanent mark of the protagonist's authorship, the protagonist's grief, the protagonist's qi-signature. The residue is the protagonist's holding. The residue is what marks the reach as the protagonist's. The residue is, per the pantheon's assessment, a small but genuine inscription — the protagonist's contribution to the cosmos's law-substrate, the protagonist's first mark on the cosmos's texture. The residue will persist as long as the protagonist's law persists, which (per the law's qi-authority) is approximately as long as the substrate persists. The residue is, in this sense, the protagonist's only genuine posterity. The residue is the protagonist's chair in the pantheon.
   217→
   218→**The reach's name.** The protagonist names the reach — the Reach of the Last Window, 末窗法域 — in the authorship's aftermath. The naming is the protagonist's first act as the ninth. The pantheon ratifies the name, slowly, over the protagonist's first decade. The ratification is the pantheon's acknowledgment that the protagonist is, now, one of the nine.
   219→
   220→**The reach's solitude.** The protagonist, after the authorship, is alone in the reach. The solitude is the Mahayana cultivator's condition (per doc 39 §8). The protagonist, in the reach's silence, perceives the cosmos's finitude as a physical weight — the weight Gu Wu has perceived for ten thousand years, the weight every Mahayana cultivator perceives. The protagonist begins the long work of bearing it. The Transcend ending's final image (per doc 26 §7.2) is the protagonist, alone in the reach, perceiving the law they authored — and, in the law's texture, the faint residue of Wang Xiaodi's dispersal, permanent now, written into the world's law. The image is the image of a being who has joined the pantheon and accepted the tragedy of the top. The tragedy is the solitude. The solitude is the meaning. The meaning is the law.

   224→## 4. The Reach of the Cleaving — Lie Fa's ruins, in detail
   225→
   226→The Reach of the Cleaving (§2.4) is the protagonist's optional inheritance (per doc 39 §4, §2.9). This section specifies the ruins in the detail the endgame's deepest optional content requires.
   227→
   228→### 4.1 The ruins' geography
   229→
   230→The ruins are the 80-li stable core at the reach's center (§2.4). The core is irregularly shaped — a region of half-law, where Lie Fa's partial inscription persists in fragments. The fragments are not contiguous; they are scattered through the core's substrate like bones in a Roman field, each fragment a partial rule that almost holds. The fragments' positions are not stable; they shift, slowly, in patterns that correspond to Lie Fa's paradox-residue's propagation. The shifting is, per Tu Nan's mapping, navigable — a Mahayana cultivator with the law-legacy perception can read the fragments' positions and chart a path through the core. The path must be re-charted each visit; the fragments shift between visits.
   231→
   232→The core's edges — the boundary between the stable core and the surrounding zone of unstable fragments — is the ruins' most dangerous region. The boundary is not a clean line; it is a gradient, from stable half-law to unstable fragments, and the gradient is where the recursion-perception (§2.4) is most contagious. A Mahayana cultivator crossing the boundary must do so quickly; lingering in the gradient is, per the pantheon's collective assessment, the most common cause of recursion-infection.
   233→
   234→### 4.2 Lie Fa's anchor
   235→
   236→Lie Fa's fractured anchor is at the core's center, in a region the pantheon has marked with a perimeter-seal (the only law the pantheon maintains in the reach). The anchor is not, in any conventional sense, a being; the anchor is a *partial coherence*, half in the reach's substrate and half in the Precelestial's standard substrate, unable to fully inhabit either. The anchor's coherence comes and goes (per doc 39 §2.9); in lucid moments (approximately one per Precelestial year), the anchor can speak, clearly, about what Lie Fa attempted and what Lie Fa perceived. In non-lucid moments, the anchor screams.
   237→
   238→The protagonist, reaching the anchor, perceives it through the law-legacy perception — a perception that, at Mahayana, the protagonist has newly awakened. The perception is overwhelming. Lie Fa's anchor, perceived directly, is the perception of a being who has touched the Origin's edge and been broken by it. The perception is, per the pantheon's collective account, the most intense perception the cosmos offers; the perception is the closest a living being can come to perceiving the Origin's pull without being subject to it.
   239→
   240→The protagonist may communicate with Lie Fa in a lucid moment (per doc 39 §2.9). The communication is brief (lucid moments last, at most, a few Precelestial hours = a few Acquired months) and enigmatic. Lie Fa's last lucid words to the protagonist, recorded by the protagonist for Tu Nan's map-room, are: "Author small. The cosmos is finite, and the finite is the only thing that can be loved." The words are the cautionary instruction the protagonist carries from the ruins. The words are, in the Transcend ending's aftermath, the words the protagonist returns to, again and again, as the protagonist tends their own small law.
   241→
   242→### 4.3 The partial law-inscription
   243→
   244→The partial law-inscription — the fragments of Lie Fa's attempted Reach-law — is the ruins' most dangerous content. The fragments are scattered through the core's substrate (§4.1); the fragments are, by the pantheon's collective assessment, the closest any being has come to a description of the Origin's generative function. The fragments are also, by the same assessment, the most dangerous text in the cosmos — to read them is to begin to perceive the recursion that broke Lie Fa, and the perception is contagious (§2.4).
   245→
   246→The protagonist, reaching the ruins, may petition the pantheon for reading-access. The pantheon, in the protagonist's era, is deadlocked (doc 39 §6) and does not respond. The protagonist may read at their own risk. The reading is, by the pantheon's collective account, a slow process — the fragments are not contiguous, and assembling them into a coherent text requires the law-legacy perception's full extension, sustained across multiple visits. The reading is, per the protagonist's account (if the protagonist undertakes it), the most demanding intellectual work the protagonist has ever performed; the reading is also the most vertiginous, because the fragments' content is the Origin's generative function as Lie Fa perceived it, and the perception, even at second-hand (through the fragments), is contagious.
   247→
   248→The reading's risk is the recursion (§2.4). The recursion is the perception that the Origin's generative function could be authored — that Lie Fa's previous failure was a flaw of execution rather than a flaw of conception, that the next attempt might succeed. The perception, once begun, tends toward the conclusion that the attempt should be made. The conclusion is the recursion's trap. The protagonist, per the pantheon's collective assessment, is at greater risk than most readers — the protagonist's Residual Error Sense (doc 00 §5) is unusually perceptive, and the perception of the recursion is, for the protagonist, more vivid than for most Mahayana cultivators. The protagonist who reads the fragments must, per the pantheon's recommendation, do so in the Held Silence (§2.3) afterward, in Mo Shen's presence, for at least a Precelestial year, to allow the recursion-perception to dissipate. The protagonist who does not is at risk of attempting Lie Fa's authorship. The attempt would reproduce Lie Fa's collapse. The collapse would disperse the protagonist and would create a *second* Reach of the Cleaving.
   249→
   250→### 4.4 What the protagonist can find
   251→
   252→The ruins contain, beyond Lie Fa's anchor and the partial law-inscription, several things of value:
   253→
   254→**Lie Fa's pre-authorship notes.** Lie Fa, before the attempted authorship, prepared extensively; the notes (in qi-residue, scattered through the core's edges) record Lie Fa's reasoning, the law's intended structure, the perceived compatibility with the deeper law. The notes are the pantheon's primary source on Lie Fa's attempt; the notes are also, per Tu Nan's assessment, the most coherent text in the ruins (the notes were inscribed before the paradox, and are not contaminated by the recursion). The notes are readable without significant risk.
   255→
   256→**The paradox-residue's map.** The fragments' shifting positions (§4.1) correspond, per Tu Nan's four-thousand-year mapping, to the paradox's propagation-pattern. The map is, per Tu Nan's assessment, the only direct evidence the pantheon has of how a law-paradox propagates through substrate. The map is invaluable for the pantheon's modeling of law-paradox risk (a critical input for any future Reach-authorship). The protagonist may contribute to the map by recording the fragments' positions during their visit.
   257→
   258→**The dispersed Mahayana's residue.** The two Mahayana cultivators who dispersed in the Sundering (Ming Kongsang and Wei Duchuan, per doc 37 §3.4) are not present in the ruins (their authorship was in the Reach of the Sundered Stratum, §2.1); but Lie Fa's notes record Lie Fa's consultation of their residue-traces (which persist, faintly, in the Sundered Stratum's edges) in preparing the attempted authorship. The notes' references to the residue-traces are the pantheon's only secondary source on Ming Kongsang's and Wei Duchuan's work.
   259→
   260→**Lie Fa's last lucid account.** Lie Fa's lucid moments, recorded by Tu Nan across three thousand years of visits, are the pantheon's only direct evidence about the Origin's edge (per doc 39 §4). The accounts are recorded in Tu Nan's map-room; the protagonist may consult them. The accounts are, per the pantheon's collective assessment, the most consequential texts in the cosmos after the partial law-inscription itself. The accounts are also, per the same assessment, the most harrowing — Lie Fa's lucid moments are lucid precisely because the recursion-perception has briefly receded, and the lucidity is the perception of a being who has touched the Origin and been broken by it, looking back at what they touched. The accounts are not for the faint of anchor.

   264→## 5. The unclaimed reaches — why, what happens, what can be found
   265→
   266→Of the nine reaches, five are unclaimed (the Sundered Stratum, the Mirror Tides, the Empty Hourglass, the Sundered Breath, and — before the protagonist's authorship — the Last Window). This section specifies the unclaimed reaches collectively.
   267→
   268→### 5.1 Why the unclaimed reaches are unclaimed
   269→
   270→The unclaimed reaches are unclaimed for one of four reasons:
   271→
   272→**Too dangerous to claim.** The Sundered Stratum (§2.1, sealed, contains the Fourth-Stratum Attempt's Residue), the Sundered Breath (§2.9, bleeding, uninhabitable for authored law), and the Empty Hourglass (§2.8, time-distorted, uninhabitable for authored law) are too dangerous for any Mahayana cultivator to claim. The substrate's condition makes inscription unstable or impossible; the cultivator who attempted to claim them would, at best, fail to inscribe, and, at worst, disperse.
   273→
   274→**Too unstable to inscribe.** The Mirror Tides (§2.7, oscillating) is, in principle, claimable, but the oscillation makes inscription unstable, and the pantheon has a tacit agreement not to claim it (the substrate's self-inscription attempts are too valuable to overwrite). The reach is, in this sense, unclaimed by collective choice rather than by substrate-condition.
   275→
   276→**Too recently discovered.** The Last Window (§2.5, before the protagonist's authorship) is unclaimed simply because no Mahayana cultivator has chosen to claim it. The reach is small, unremarkable, and overlooked. The reach was, before the protagonist, the most unremarkable reach in the cosmos. The protagonist's choice to claim it is, in this sense, the protagonist's first original act as a Mahayana cultivator — the act of perceiving value where the pantheon has not.
   277→
   278→**Too politically sensitive.** The Wandering Edge (§2.6, You Wuya's approach) is, in principle, claimable, but the pantheon has a tacit agreement not to claim it — You Wuya's approach is, per the pantheon's collective assessment, the most important inquiry any of them is undertaking, and any other Mahayana cultivator's inscription in the reach would interfere with the inquiry. The reach is, in this sense, unclaimed by collective deference to You Wuya.
   279→
   280→### 5.2 What happens to a cultivator who enters an unclaimed reach
   281→
   282→The unclaimed reaches are, for cultivators below Mahayana, lethal — each in its specific way. The Sundered Stratum's keening-dispersion (§2.1), the Caged Sky's consumption-draw (§2.2; technically held but functionally unclaimed in the sense that no Mahayana cultivator's law inscribes the interior), the Cleaving's recursion-infection (§2.4), the Empty Hourglass's time-distortion (§2.8), the Sundered Breath's bleeding (§2.9) — each is lethal, in different ways, to cultivators below Mahayana. The Mirror Tides (§2.7) is the only unclaimed reach survivable below Mahayana, and only at the reach's edges; the Last Window (§2.5) is survivable below Mahayana at any point (the substrate is patient and does not actively harm), but a cultivator below Mahayana in the Last Window's interior would, per the pantheon's assessment, simply wait — the substrate does not inscribe itself, and a cultivator below Mahayana cannot inscribe, so the cultivator would, eventually, leave (or die of natural causes, in the Precelestial's slow time).
   283→
   284→For Mahayana cultivators, the unclaimed reaches are survivable but not comfortable. Each reach's qi-climate imposes its specific perception (the keening, the consumption, the recursion, the time-rushing, the bleeding); each reach's decay-state imposes its specific risk. A Mahayana cultivator entering an unclaimed reach does so for a specific purpose (research, inheritance-quest, authorship-preparation, suicide) and leaves when the purpose is fulfilled or the risk becomes unacceptable.
   285→
   286→### 5.3 What can be found in the unclaimed reaches
   287→
   288→The unclaimed reaches contain, beyond the specific dangers and qi-climates:
   289→
   290→**Incomplete law-fragments.** The Mirror Tides (§2.7) produces partial law-fragments in its oscillation; the fragments are not any law the pantheon recognizes, and are, in the pantheon's best theory, the substrate's self-inscription attempts. The fragments, at the tide's peak, are briefly readable; the reading is, per Tu Nan's cataloguing, never the same content twice. The fragments are the pantheon's only direct evidence of the substrate's self-inscription. The fragments are invaluable for the pantheon's modeling of law-authorship risk.
   291→
   292→**The residue of the Sundering.** The Sundered Stratum (§2.1) and the Sundered Breath (§2.9) both preserve, in their substrates, the residue of the Sundering's paradox-propagation. The residue is, per the pantheon's collective assessment, the most direct evidence the cosmos has of the Sundering's nature — the residue is the paradox's footprint, still propagating eight thousand years later. The residue is, for any Mahayana cultivator studying the Sundering (and the broader question of law-paradox risk), the primary research material.
   293→
   294→**Beings that evolved in lawless space.** The unclaimed reaches (especially the Mirror Tides, the Empty Hourglass, and the Sundered Breath) are home to beings that evolved in the reaches' specific lawless or half-law conditions — beings that are not the Spirit Wilds' alien ecology (doc 41 §2) but a different, reach-specific ecology. The beings are sparse (the reaches are not ecologically rich; the qi-climates are too extreme for most life), but they exist. The most studied is the **Tide-Singer of the Mirror Tides** (鏡潮之鳴者), a being that appears to be a consciousness sustained by the reach's oscillation, without a body in any conventional sense. The Tide-Singer is, per Tu Nan's four-thousand-year observation, the only known being that perceives the Mirror Tides' fragments coherently — the Tide-Singer perceives the fragments' content as a single continuous text, where the pantheon perceives fragments. The Tide-Singer's account (communicated to Tu Nan in qi-modulations, across two thousand years of patient contact) is the pantheon's only secondary source on the fragments' content. The account is, per Tu Nan's assessment, the most enigmatic text in the cosmos after the partial law-inscription itself.

   298→## 6. The reaches and the courts
   299→
   300→The courts have **no jurisdiction in the reaches** (per doc 38 §7.4). This section specifies why, what this means for law-enforcement, and what happens when a Mahayana cultivator authors a law that conflicts with the courts' jurisdiction.
   301→
   302→### 6.1 Why the courts have no jurisdiction
   303→
   304→The courts' jurisdiction derives from the founding compact (doc 37 §4.3), which the seven founders inscribed in the Precelestial's substrate-law *in the courts' territory* (the central hundred li of the Precelestial, per doc 36 §4.2). The founding compact's jurisdiction is, by the compact's own scope, limited to the regions where the substrate-law holds — the courts' territory, the maintained roads, and the ancestral courts (which the compact recognizes as sovereign within their scope, jurisdiction 5). The reaches, where the substrate-law has thinned or not formed, are outside the compact's scope by definition. The compact's law cannot hold where there is no substrate to inscribe it in.
   305→
   306→This is not a political choice; it is a cosmological fact. The courts could not extend their jurisdiction to the reaches even if they wished to — the courts' law, inscribed in the compact's territory, does not propagate into the reaches' thinned substrate. A courts-magistrate who attempted to enforce a courts-ruling in a reach would find, on crossing the reach's boundary, that the courts' law no longer holds — the magistrate's authority, derived from the compact, would simply cease. The magistrate would, in the reach's interior, be a cultivator without institutional backing, subject to the reach's qi-climate and decay-state like any other cultivator.
   307→
   308→The courts maintain watchposts along the reaches' boundaries (per doc 38 §7.4), but the watchposts are observational, not enforceable — the watchposts' purpose is to perceive, early, if a reach's decay-state changes (if the Sundered Breath expands, if the Caged Sky's seal fails, if the Cleaving's recursion begins to propagate outward), and to alert the pantheon and the Council. The watchposts do not enforce; the watchposts observe.
   309→
   310→### 6.2 What this means for law-enforcement
   311→
   312→The reaches' extra-jurisdictional status means that **the courts cannot enforce their rulings against a being in a reach**. A cultivator who flees to a reach is, by the courts' law, beyond the courts' reach (the wordplay is intentional, and is a common figure of speech in the courts' Acquired-stratum intermediaries' gossip). The courts can issue a ruling against the cultivator; the ruling cannot be enforced while the cultivator remains in the reach. The cultivator who never leaves the reach is, in practical terms, beyond the courts' law.
   313→
   314→This has produced, over the courts' eight-thousand-year history, a small but persistent population of **reach-exiles** — cultivators (mostly Tribulation Crossing, occasionally Spirit Severance) who have fled to the reaches to escape courts-adjudication. The reach-exiles are, in the pantheon's assessment, mostly dead (the reaches are lethal below Mahayana, and the exiles who survive are the exception), but a few have persisted, in the survivable reaches' edges, for centuries. The most notable is **Leng Wushuang** (冷無雙, "Cold Without Equal"), a Tribulation Crossing cultivator who fled to the Mirror Tides' edges twelve hundred years ago after a courts-adjudication found him guilty of lineage-treason. Leng Wushuang persists, by the Mirror Tides' oscillation, in a state of partial coherence not unlike Lie Fa's (though less severe); Leng Wushuang's persistence is, per the pantheon's collective assessment, the longest a Tribulation Crossing cultivator has survived in a reach. Leng Wushuang is the only reach-exile the pantheon studies; the study is, per Tu Nan's assessment, informative about the reaches' long-term effects on cultivators below Mahayana.
   315→
   316→The courts' inability to enforce in the reaches is, in the courts' internal politics, a recurring grievance. The Wild Suppression Bloc (doc 38 §3.5) has, for four millennia, advocated for a courts-expansion that would extend the courts' jurisdiction to the reaches (and the wilds, doc 41 §6); the expansion has been defeated each time by cost (the courts do not have the qi-authority to maintain law in the reaches, even if the cosmology permitted it) and by the pantheon's tacit veto (the pantheon does not want the courts in the reaches; the reaches are the pantheon's authorship-venue, and the pantheon does not share). The Wild Suppression Bloc's campaign is, in the pantheon's collective assessment, the surest way to undo the courts' eight-thousand-year accommodation with the reaches.
   317→
   318→### 6.3 What happens if a Mahayana cultivator authors a conflicting law
   319→
   320→A Mahayana cultivator who authors a Reach-law that conflicts with the courts' jurisdiction (a law that, say, declares the reach's interior subject to the cultivator's personal jurisdiction, overriding the courts' extra-jurisdictional status) has authored a law that the courts cannot enforce against (the reach is beyond the courts' jurisdiction), but that the pantheon can contest (the pantheon's law-contest mechanism, per doc 30 Station 10, is the cosmos's only mechanism for adjudicating Mahayana-authored law).
   321→
   322→The contest, if it occurs, is a Mahayana law-contest — a direct conflict between two Mahayana cultivators' laws, adjudicated by the law-paradox perception and the law-tribulation mechanism. The contest's outcome is binary: one law prevails, the other is overturned; the overturned law's author, if the overturn is total, disperses. The contest is the cosmos's most consequential conflict-mechanism; it has been invoked, in the cosmos's history, perhaps a dozen times, and has dispersed (per the pantheon's records) seven Mahayana cultivators.
   323→
   324→The Conservative Bloc's fear of the protagonist's law (per doc 38 §7.4) is, in part, a fear of this contest-mechanism — if the protagonist's Acquired-authorship amendment succeeds, the Conservative Bloc's laws (the records-integrity law, the lineage-jurisdiction clause) become contestable, and the contest could move to the reaches, where the Conservative Bloc's Mahayana councilors could author counter-laws the protagonist could not safely contest (the protagonist, as a new Mahayana cultivator, is at a disadvantage in any law-contest against an older Mahayana cultivator). The reaches are, in this sense, the courts' constitutional frontier — the place where the courts' authority ends and individual Mahayana authorship begins, and where the contest between them, if it comes, will be fought.
   325→
   326→The protagonist's Transcend-ending law is, however, not a conflicting law — the protagonist's amendment is an Acquired-authorship that amends Gu Wu's bardo-window law, which is itself an Acquired-authorship. The amendment does not touch the courts' jurisdiction; it does not extend into the reaches; it does not contest the Conservative Bloc's laws directly. The amendment is, by the protagonist's careful scoping, a small law that does not invite the contest. The Conservative Bloc's contest (per doc 38 §8, the civil-war risk) is, in the canonical Transcend path, averted by the protagonist's survival of the law-tribulation (which ratifies the amendment's compatibility with the deeper law) and by the Conservative Bloc's political calculation (a contest against a ratified amendment is a contest against the deeper law, and the Conservative Bloc cannot win such a contest). The contest does not occur. The protagonist's law takes effect. The cosmos, for a small scoped region, is amended.

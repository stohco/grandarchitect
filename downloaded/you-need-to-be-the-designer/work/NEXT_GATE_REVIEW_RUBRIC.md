# Cultivation Foundations & Mortal Reality — Release Review Rubric

**Use:** blocker audit for every draft before promotion to `outputs/`.

## Source and originality

- Every historical/religious claim has a located primary or qualified academic source.
- Source identity, exact page/section where available, access, retrieval, language, and translation limitations are recorded.
- Historical practice, philosophical argument, modern lived practice, genre convention, one-author invention, inference, and original design are visibly distinct.
- No single novel can authorize canon; research-informed design uses a reviewed cross-source abstraction plus an independent gameplay need.
- No copied characters, cosmology, named artifacts, prose, distinctive realm ladders, signature systems, or plot sequences.
- Conflicting sources and absence of evidence remain visible.

## Terminology

- Retain seasoned-reader vocabulary: qi, Qi Condensation, Dao, yin–yang, spiritual roots, meridians, cultivation, tribulation.
- Translate or gloss niche/opaque romanizations; do not translate `qi` into “breath” by default.
- Research records preserve Hanzi, tonal pinyin, literal gloss, contextual meanings, historical scope, genre usage, alternatives, and reviewer rationale where relevant.
- Project-native English does not receive fabricated Hanzi or pseudo-Chinese syllables.
- Human meridians and anatomy never become universal nonhuman biology.

## Mortal reality

- A mortal year is playable without cultivation, combat, or a predetermined hero plot.
- Food, water, fuel, shelter, sanitation, health, care, labor, land, debt, law, travel, weather, ritual, death, leisure, education, and relationships form causal systems.
- Multiple household structures, genders, ages, disabilities, classes, occupations, cultures, and nonhuman persons have agency rather than token entries.
- Schedules are viable; labor and goods have provenance; institutions have capacity and disagreement.
- Failure creates recoverable consequences, not only death or quest failure.
- No dynasty, elite male text, modern nation, or one ethnography becomes universal setting truth.

## Cultivation ontology

- Body, qi, spirit/mind, Dao orientation, vital continuity, relationships, authority, and optional fate/karma/luck modules remain typed rather than one power meter.
- Practice has teacher/manual/embodied action/environment/time/resource/feedback/failure dependencies.
- Breakthroughs are transformations with prerequisites, risks, consequences, and social interpretation.
- Techniques and items use semantic action contracts; their descriptions state only entailed behavior.
- Progression changes verbs and responsibilities, not only quantities or VFX.
- Institutions, domestic life, ecology, economy, law, medicine, and religion react to cultivation.

## Qi Condensation

- The player-facing term is Qi Condensation.
- Capability is multidimensional: body, qi, control, perception, mobility, survivability, combat, precision, duration, target count, social visibility, and consequence radius.
- Ordinary, exceptional, prepared/collective, and external-authority bands are separate.
- A Qi Condensation cultivator cannot personally split a huge mountain.
- Mountain-scale early outcomes require sufficient external resources/authority/environment and honest attribution.
- Cross-realm victory requires an actual exploitable dimension, preparation, cost, uncertainty, and failure path.
- Stacking, splitting, borrowing, storage, formation, environmental-chain-reaction, and protagonist-status exploits fail.

## Identity and death

- Numerical identity, body, agency, memory, spirit, causal descent, social/legal recognition, oath/debt, cultivation, and inheritance are separate judgments.
- Copying does not silently duplicate identity, authority, resources, costs, or unique relationships.
- Created/surviving consciousness receives personhood, consent, and liability treatment.
- Death changes households, relationships, labor, property, law, ritual, ecology, records, and history.
- Resurrection/reincarnation/possession conserve matter, qi, information, identity, authority, and responsibility under explicit contracts.
- Save/load, S0–S4 simulation resolution, orthogonal rendering-LOD changes, migration, and world crossing preserve identity invariants.

## Protagonist advantage

- Candidate enriches mortal and Qi Condensation play instead of skipping it.
- It has canonical locus, privileged operation, limits, costs, counters, signals, exposure, transfer/death rules, growth, and end state.
- It creates unusual information, integration, access, preservation, authorization, relationship, or adaptation opportunities without universal competence.
- No hidden `isPlayer` physics, automatic rescue, guaranteed treasure, immunity, perfect comprehension, infinite resources, or unearned cross-realm damage.
- An isomorphic NPC with the same advantage/state receives behaviorally identical resolution.
- Removal test proves only declared contributions disappear.

## Golden scenarios

- Scenario can be resolved through at least three meaningfully different strategies.
- No answer is selected by hidden morality or protagonist destiny.
- Knowledge has provenance; NPCs act from bounded information and motives.
- Success, partial success, failure, withdrawal, deception, care, negotiation, and later return remain possible where appropriate.
- Results alter future relationships, places, resources, institutions, and self-understanding.
- First qi perception and first breakthrough remain discoveries and transformations, not tutorial pop-ups or XP thresholds.

## Release gate

- Zero S0/S1 findings in included scope.
- Every included claim has a measurable obligation and evidence or is explicitly a proposal.
- All ontology domains and contradictions touched by the release have graph/crosswalk/test mappings.
- User choices are reserved for genuinely world-defining forks.
- No code, scaffold, dependency installation, or implementation authorization has occurred.

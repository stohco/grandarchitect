# Protagonist Advantage Operation Landscape — Draft v0.1

**Status:** risk comparison; no advantage selected  
**Purpose:** compare the full abstract advantage landscape before turning three promising directions into final candidates  
**Locked:** no player-only physics; no automatic victory; no hidden survival guarantee; no mountain-scale personal Qi Condensation output

---

## 0. Selection thesis

The protagonist should be exceptional because one narrow relationship with cultivation behaves differently, not because every favorable trope accumulates at once.

An advantage should primarily alter one operation:

```text
discern | learn | integrate | preserve | authorize | access | adapt
```

Its locus may be embodied, informational, relational, institutional, carried, or environmental. Operation and locus are independent: an artifact may help discern; a constitution may preserve; a relationship may grant access.

### 0.1 Phase-scope correction

“Mortal possibility” means a concrete pre-qi interface, not retrospective story flavor. The three advanced candidates are bound to `ADV-TARGET-v0.2` in the Qi Condensation draft:

- Residual Error Sense targets one self-owned trace from an already completed deliberate embodied attempt and exposes only coarse sequence/timing mismatch before Contact.
- Held Pattern targets one already-present, harmless self-generated sensory/attentional coordination relation for at most one second before Contact; it cannot suspend any bodily requirement, wound, poison, death process, outside event, or changing world state.
- Reciprocal Cadence creates one consented touch-range timing channel for at most ten seconds during a deliberate joint physical task; it carries phase difference only, not memory, emotion, skill, diagnosis, qi, force, or command.

Qi-routing, qi-relation, and low-band practice fields become available only after the relevant ontic capability exists. If a candidate's pre-qi target fails its livelihood, surveillance, appraisal, consent, economy, or no-advantage tests, that candidate loses the mortal-year claim; the specification may not silently reinterpret qi work as ordinary labor.

The selection process asks:

1. What new decisions appear during a fully mortal year?
2. What does it add to Qi Condensation without increasing its personal destructive ceiling?
3. Which ordinary people, teachers, institutions, tools, and places become more important rather than obsolete?
4. How is it wrong, costly, countered, detected, exploited, transferred, lost, or refused?
5. What responsibilities emerge as its scope grows?
6. Does a no-advantage control remain fun?

## 1. Seven-operation comparison

### 1.1 Discern

**Core asymmetry:** receive unusually faithful but bounded evidence about a narrow class of differences, errors, traces, or relations.

**Mortal possibilities**

- notice repeatable mismatch in the protagonist's own work, body, tool use, or observation;
- compare two attempts more faithfully;
- preserve uncertainty while asking better questions.

**Qi Condensation possibilities**

- inspect the gap between intended and actual self-owned routing;
- distinguish one source of practice error from another only after evidence exists;
- improve correction efficiency without generating methods or resources.

**Failure pressure**

- evidence may be precise but uninterpretable;
- several causes may produce the same mismatch;
- attention can fixate on what the advantage exposes and ignore everything else;
- adversaries can contaminate traces or manipulate the protagonist's model.

**Golden-finger failure to prohibit:** omniscient appraisal, true/false labels, realm scanning, intent reading, perfect treasure detection, future prediction.

**Candidate status:** **advance** as the basis of Residual Error Sense.

### 1.2 Learn

**Core asymmetry:** acquire, retain, generalize, retrieve, or practice some information more effectively.

**Mortal possibilities**

- remember demonstrations, compare revisions, retain corrections, or reduce one kind of interference;
- accelerate a narrow skill while ordinary time and practice remain.

**Qi Condensation possibilities**

- preserve the exact subjective sequence of an attempt;
- learn one demonstrated correction with lower forgetting;
- build stronger causal models from repeated evidence.

**Failure pressure**

- retention is not comprehension;
- a faithfully learned falsehood becomes a deeper trap;
- knowledge can exceed bodily capability, judgment, resources, or authority;
- rapid learning can alienate teachers and institutions without defeating their material power.

**Golden-finger failure to prohibit:** instant mastery, copying techniques from sight, automatic Dao comprehension, skill downloads, perfect memory of everything, bypassing tacit instruction.

**Candidate status:** **hold**. It is genre-readable but too close to universal progression acceleration unless narrowed more sharply than current alternatives.

### 1.3 Integrate

**Core asymmetry:** maintain coordination among components, practices, people, models, or environments that normally interfere.

**Mortal possibilities**

- coordinate timing/error information with a consenting partner;
- reconcile work, care, and observation without erasing opportunity costs;
- compare two partial models while retaining their contradictions.

**Qi Condensation possibilities**

- synchronize one safe practice loop with one partner;
- reduce information loss during correction without merging reservoirs or minds;
- maintain a narrow compatibility relation already supported by both participants.

**Failure pressure**

- integration exposes both parties and can transmit error;
- disagreement, consent withdrawal, injury, and incompatible cadence matter;
- the protagonist may become dependent on a partner or institution;
- social rules can prohibit or monopolize the practice.

**Golden-finger failure to prohibit:** cultivating every path, mixing hostile qi without consequence, merging bloodlines/roots, copying skills, group-network exponential power, compulsory harmony.

**Candidate status:** **advance**, narrowed to Reciprocal Cadence rather than universal self-integration.

### 1.4 Preserve

**Core asymmetry:** keep one normally transient relation, trace, structure, or state available for a bounded interval or transition.

**Mortal possibilities**

- preserve fragile evidence, a work pattern, or a fleeting anomaly long enough to compare;
- create a small observation window without changing the underlying event.

**Qi Condensation possibilities**

- hold one small self-generated internal qi relation for a few heartbeats;
- deliberately release it back to ordinary resolution;
- demonstrate a state to a teacher rather than describe it from memory.

**Failure pressure**

- preservation reserves capacity and prevents the relation from acting;
- the body, time, costs, injuries, and outside world continue;
- rebound, numbness, decay, and detectable signatures create risk;
- preserving the wrong state can make injury worse.

**Golden-finger failure to prohibit:** stopped time, paused damage, suspended death, infinite storage, cooldown freezing, perfect artifact preservation, save-state rollback.

**Candidate status:** **advance** as the basis of Held Pattern, pending identity/death rules.

### 1.5 Authorize

**Core asymmetry:** make, recognize, carry, contest, or mediate permissions and authority through an unusual lawful interface.

**Mortal possibilities**

- see that a rule, oath, title, claim, or boundary applies without knowing whether it is just;
- act as a rare witness or compatibility mediator.

**Qi Condensation possibilities**

- authenticate one class of cultivation transmission or consent relation;
- invoke a bounded inherited permission without receiving its power personally.

**Failure pressure**

- authority depends on institutions, world laws, identity, and jurisdiction not yet designed;
- valid authority may be unjust, forged, obsolete, or resisted;
- recognition can place the protagonist inside politics immediately;
- it risks turning every barrier into a protagonist keyhole.

**Golden-finger failure to prohibit:** universal command, ownership by touch, oath enforcement without consent/process, automatic legitimacy, law authorship at Qi Condensation, Heaven favor.

**Candidate status:** **defer** until cosmology, identity, law, institutions, and world boundaries are ratified.

### 1.6 Access

**Core asymmetry:** interface with a place, object, archive, teacher, environment, sense channel, or causal path that is otherwise inaccessible.

**Mortal possibilities**

- reach one narrow evidence channel under costly conditions;
- communicate with or enter one bounded relationship that others cannot establish easily.

**Qi Condensation possibilities**

- access an unusual but finite qi source/interface whose ownership, replenishment, compatibility, and risks remain real;
- inspect an otherwise closed practice state without opening every secret.

**Failure pressure**

- access is not capacity, knowledge, permission, safety, ownership, or control;
- every rare inheritance can become protagonist-shaped content delivery;
- institutions will seek to recruit, regulate, deceive, or confine the bearer;
- inaccessible entities may not be friendly or meaningful.

**Golden-finger failure to prohibit:** every inheritance accepts the player, sealed realms open automatically, resource scarcity disappears, universal language, perfect spirit communication, hidden treasure radar.

**Candidate status:** **hold/defer**. Strong for exploration, but too likely to make the world orbit the protagonist before the cosmology and first region exist.

### 1.7 Adapt

**Core asymmetry:** change response, compatibility, structure, or learning conditions more flexibly after exposure.

**Mortal possibilities**

- recover a narrow work adaptation more precisely;
- change one bodily/practice response after costly, repeated evidence.

**Qi Condensation possibilities**

- slowly remodel one route around an injury or environmental condition;
- expand one spiritual-root discrimination axis through long practice;
- develop a counter only after surviving and understanding repeated exposure.

**Failure pressure**

- adaptation takes time, resources, rest, and viable biology;
- maladaptation and path dependence can lock out other options;
- exposure may kill or permanently injure before useful change;
- an opponent can vary signals faster than adaptation.

**Golden-finger failure to prohibit:** instant immunity, devouring abilities, all-root compatibility, unlimited body evolution, automatic counter generation, injury-as-power farming.

**Candidate status:** **hold**. It could support disability-aware and alternate-path play, but its exploit surface is substantially larger than discern/preserve/reciprocal integration.

## 3. Three directions that survive the first filter

### A. Residual Error Sense — discern

- Best for investigation, deliberate practice, fraud, diagnosis limits, and player learning.
- Strong solo value without direct output.
- Principal danger: becoming an appraisal/solution interface.
- Required hard boundary: post-attempt, self-owned, short-lived mismatch only.

### B. Held Pattern — preserve

- Best for making fleeting qi states inspectable, teachable, and tactically deliberate.
- Creates a distinctive time-like feeling without stopping external time.
- Principal danger: invulnerability, suspended injury, or save-state logic.
- Required hard boundary: a held relation cannot act, replenish, prevent bodily change, or pause cost.

### C. Reciprocal Cadence — integrate/relate

- Best for making teachers, peers, care, trust, consent, and social continuity mechanically central.
- Most different from solitary golden-finger conventions.
- Principal danger: weak solo fantasy or exponential group synchronization.
- Required hard boundary: one consenting partner, timing/error signal only, separated bodies/minds/resources, immediate withdrawal.

These are directions, not names, artifacts, constitutions, systems, or final contracts. The eventual locus could change after identity and cosmology review.

## 4. Shared rejection tests

Every candidate fails if it:

- solves food, shelter, money, social status, cultivation access, or safety without ordinary action;
- produces the correct interpretation instead of bounded evidence;
- supplies qi, force, durability, authority, recovery, time, or resources outside the realm envelope;
- certifies mastery, breaks a bottleneck, or survives a transition automatically;
- creates minimum damage, automatic aim, favorable random outcomes, or enemy stupidity;
- locates every treasure, inheritance, teacher, plot, or safe route;
- invalidates fraud, mystery, scholarship, institutions, teachers, craft, medicine, care, or relationships;
- rewards injury, death, deviation, copying, reincarnation, save/load, or repetition with unlimited growth;
- cannot be transferred, lost, copied, inherited, refused, suppressed, or carried through death only because the player owns it;
- lacks sensory/social evidence or counterplay;
- makes an isomorphic NPC resolve differently; or
- contributes any undeclared structural work toward `HugeMountainClass-H0`.

## 5. Decision gate

No direction advances to user selection until:

1. an identity/death model determines what can bear, copy, transfer, inherit, or survive the advantage;
2. a cosmology determines whether the operation is embodied, carried, relational, environmental, or law-mediated;
3. the Mortal Year Atlas shows at least four genuinely different early uses and at least two costly failures;
4. the Qi Condensation envelope proves zero undeclared output/authority and passes the mountain oracle;
5. the seven golden scenarios remain viable without the advantage;
6. an isomorphic NPC parity fixture passes;
7. a no-advantage, no-teacher, false-evidence, transfer, death, save/load, and late-game growth battery passes;
8. the user receives two or three complete original contracts with explicit tradeoffs and may choose, combine only if composition remains lawful, or reject all.

No selection is made in this draft.

# Grand Architect Project Doctrine

## Project identity

- This is a clean-slate, original-IP, single-player xianxia action-sandbox RPG project.
- Preserve a xianxia genre spine: cultivation toward immortality/transcendence through qi, body, spirit, Dao comprehension, lineage, institutions, alchemical/cosmological transformation, and consequences. Do not let arbitrary level systems, imported science-fiction cheats, unbounded bloodline spectacle, or genre-mixed magic replace that spine merely because cultivation terminology is present.
- The player begins as a genuinely mortal person and may eventually become a world-law author.
- The player is the protagonist and may possess a unique lawful asymmetry, but never a player-only exemption from ontology, provenance, costs, or realm capability envelopes. Early cultivation cannot perform mountain-scale feats unless the world law and supplied resources genuinely support them.
- The intended client technology is Three.js with WebGPU where it is advantageous and supportable.
- No alien-project concepts are part of this canon.

## Current phase: research only

- Do not write production code, scaffold an application, install engine dependencies, or begin implementation unless the user explicitly opens the implementation phase.
- Research, source ledgers, design documents, graphs, schemas, benchmarks, reviews, diagrams, and PRD preparation are in scope.
- Preserve citations and distinguish historical/religious sources, genre conventions, external inspirations, inference, and original project decisions.

## Canon governance

- Agents may propose research claims and design candidates; they may not silently promote them into canon.
- Canon decisions require an explicit review record and must remain traceable to evidence, design intent, player verbs, dependencies, and tests.
- Study copyrighted fiction at the pattern level. Do not reproduce author-specific prose, characters, settings, terminology, artifacts, or cosmology.
- A single external work may contextualize research but never authorize fictional canon. Research-informed proposals require both a reviewed cross-source abstraction and an independent gameplay need; original proposals require an explicit project rationale and need/contradiction.
- No Mortal Space is a scale-fantasy benchmark, not the project's ceiling or template.
- Final player-facing language uses established English-language xianxia vocabulary correctly: keep familiar terms such as qi, Qi Condensation, Dao, yin–yang, spiritual roots, meridians, cultivation, and tribulation. Preserve Hanzi, tonal pinyin, literal glosses, historical meanings, translation alternatives, and source context in research records. Translate niche or opaque romanizations that a seasoned English-speaking xianxia reader would not know; retain an uncommon romanization only with a deliberate contextual reason and explanation.

## Core design laws

- Generate histories and lawful processes first; derive places, beings, resources, institutions, quests, and prose from their consequences.
- Every significant generated thing must have provenance, sustainers, stakeholders, affordances, costs, traces, and possible transformations.
- Procedural prose presents or interprets canonical state; it never creates canonical truth by itself.
- Each cultivation realm must add perceptions, verbs, responsibilities, constraints, and failure modes—not merely numbers.
- A purported new verb does not count if it becomes an older verb after normalizing names, scale, target count, numbers, and VFX. Require distinct state transitions, information, costs, counters/failures, and downstream consequences.
- Protagonist exceptionalism must create unusual options, learning paths, combinations, survivals, or responsibilities without erasing progression, institutions, scarcity, failure, or the achievements of ordinary cultivators.
- Leaving a place changes simulation fidelity, not causality. Earlier people and places remain consequential at later realms.
- Simulate only what can create a decision, an explanation, a memory, or a future consequence.
- Descriptions are semantic contracts. An item, skill, law, being, place, or process must be able to do what its approved description says, with scale, provenance, consequences, counters, persistence, and presentation derived from canonical behavior rather than decorative prose.

## Review posture

- Prefer coherent depth over feature accumulation.
- Challenge contradictions, procedural sameness, cultural flattening, economic exploits, scale discontinuities, and spectacle without gameplay.
- Use staged proof slices to validate the full vision; do not redefine the enormous end-state vision into a small survival-crafting game.

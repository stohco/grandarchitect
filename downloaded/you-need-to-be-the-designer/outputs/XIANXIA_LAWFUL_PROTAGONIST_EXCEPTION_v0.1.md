# Lawful Protagonist Exception & Realm Capability Envelope v0.1

**Status:** pre-canon systems architecture; the actual protagonist advantage is intentionally undecided  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Locked:** single-player; the player is the true protagonist; unique asymmetry is allowed; no player-only physics; Qi Condensation cannot personally split a huge mountain

---

## 0. Governing invariant

The protagonist may possess a unique capability source, access rule, relationship, growth path, or efficiency. The protagonist may not possess an implicit `isPlayer` exemption from world law.

```text
actor state
+ realm capability envelope
+ lawful protagonist exception
+ prepared resources and allies
+ external authority
+ environment
+ local law context
→ shared action resolver
→ shared costs, counters, consequences, persistence, and history
```

If an NPC receives an isomorphic canonical state, advantage, resources, knowledge, authority, random variates, and law context, the two outcomes must match under the declared identity-renaming bijection. Full canonical hashes remain distinct because they correctly include actor/action identities and provenance. The parity oracle instead compares a versioned normalized behavioral projection that relabels corresponding identities and excludes bookkeeping identity/provenance fields while preserving targets, quantities, costs, counters, failures, world-state transitions, and consequences. A test-only seed-equivalence manifest supplies identical logical random variates without changing live identity-derived seed rules. Camera, pause, controls, tutorials, accessibility, and save UI may be player-specific; world truth may not.

Forbidden privileged paths:

- hidden player-only damage, defense, collision, targeting, flight, resource, or RNG rules;
- concealed realm-cap bypasses;
- free matter, qi, knowledge, authority, or resurrection;
- artifact/patron output misreported as personal strength;
- automatic immunity to capture, theft, suppression, exposure, counters, failure, or social consequence;
- mentor rescue without finite knowledge, agency, access, cost, risk, and prior state;
- reload/respawn treated as an in-world power without a canonical mechanism; and
- equivalent NPC actions resolved less faithfully solely to favor the player.

## 1. Why exceptional does not mean overpowered

Protagonist advantage can begin as **more meaningful possibility**, not more destructive output.

At Qi Condensation, exceptionalism might lawfully provide:

- perception of a hidden relationship, defect, or opportunity;
- unusually faithful learning or diagnosis within bodily/time limits;
- compatibility between practices usually difficult to integrate;
- retention of selected knowledge through a transition;
- access to a teacher, place, inheritance, office, or procedure;
- recovery from a narrow mistake at a real cost;
- superior efficiency inside the realm’s throughput ceiling;
- better preparation, mediation, or leverage; or
- a new non-destructive verb.

It does not supply late-realm force, authority, resilience, speed, target count, or consequence radius.

The eventual power fantasy becomes stronger because attribution remains honest. Early on, the player may save a valley by finding a fault, completing a formation, persuading an office, redirecting an existing force, or invoking a dangerous stored artifact. Much later, the player may personally move or split a mountain because the later realm envelope now supports it.

## 2. Realm capability envelope

A cultivation realm is a multidimensional feasibility envelope, not a scalar level.

### Physical output

- force, impulse, structural work, severance;
- mass moved, supported, created, or transformed;
- pressure, penetration, terrain, and construct scale;
- burst and sustained output.

### Kinematics

- speed, acceleration, reaction/control latency;
- traversal/flight mode, range, continuous travel;
- spatial or topological reach.

### Survivability

- bodily integrity;
- heat, pressure, poison, disease, and environment resistance;
- meridian/channel stability where applicable;
- soul and causal resistance;
- recovery rate and recoverable injury classes.

### Qi and conversion

- personal reservoir;
- peak and sustainable throughput;
- replenishment;
- conversion efficiency and loss;
- affinity and contamination tolerance;
- safe overload and catastrophic boundary.

### Control

- precision, concurrent targets, effect complexity;
- stability, stealth/signature suppression;
- duration, persistence, friendly-fire discrimination.

### Perception and information

- senses, range, resolution, occlusion;
- integration load and prediction horizon;
- memory and record interaction.

### Authority

- available law interfaces and jurisdiction;
- oath, ownership, soul, identity, or causal reach;
- ability to create bounded exceptions;
- resistance to foreign law;
- social/institutional recognition.

### Consequence capacity

- direct consequence radius;
- safely maintained aftermath;
- individually resolved dependents;
- ecological, economic, social, and historical reach;
- debt, contamination, backlash, or tribulation tolerated.

Each dimension records unit/taxonomy, typical band, personal hard ceiling, burst/sustained bounds, precision at the bound, recovery, failure, coupling, counters, aggregation, and tests. An average “power score” cannot authorize an action; every required dimension must fit or identify another sufficient source.

## 3. Four capability bands

| Band | Meaning | Attribution |
|---|---|---|
| `ordinary` | Stable personal capability for a trained member of the realm. | Personal |
| `exceptional` | Rare, optimized, or protagonist-enhanced personal capability still within the realm hard envelope. | Personal plus explicit exception contribution |
| `prepared` | Setup, formations, stockpiles, terrain, allies, ritual, or engineered instability supplies the feat. | Split by source |
| `external_authority` | Artifact, patron, domain, inheritance, sealed power, office, or borrowed law supplies capability. | External source; actor is invoker/controller |

One action may occupy different bands on different dimensions. A prepared formation can have enormous area but weak precision; an inherited sword can supply great cutting authority while its Qi Condensation wielder still lacks perception, reaction, survivability, or control.

### Locked early-realm proof

> A Qi Condensation actor cannot personally supply the structural work, authority, control, and sustained qi throughput required to split a huge mountain-class landform.

A Qi Condensation protagonist may cause mountain-scale change only when a sufficient nonpersonal ledger exists, such as:

- prepared formation or collective ritual;
- stored artifact or inherited external authority;
- patron/domain loan;
- preexisting geological instability;
- redirection of an already mountain-scale force;
- collapse of a narrow structural keystone; or
- lawful chain reaction.

“You caused the mountain to collapse” may be true while “you split the mountain with your personal power” is false.

The exact realm ladder and mountain thresholds remain future canon decisions.

## 4. Realm stacking algebra

Every modifier declares, per capability dimension:

- additive, multiplicative, replacing, capped, or mutually exclusive composition;
- order independence or explicit stable order;
- personal versus prepared/external attribution;
- diminishing return;
- throughput bottleneck;
- overflow and failure;
- coupling to control, stability, body, and recovery;
- whether it changes capability band; and
- maximum combined result.

An individually legal stack can still be rejected if its composed vector crosses the personal hard ceiling or violates a coupled constraint. Dividing one over-budget action into many sub-actions does not bypass cumulative throughput, heat, recovery, consequence, or authority budgets.

## 7. Growth and discovery

Every transition records prior state, trigger, experience/evidence, resources, realm prerequisite, new capability, unchanged limits, counters, risks, envelope revision, description changes, observable signs, persistence, and migration.

A revelation may change what the player knows without changing what the advantage already did. A transformation changes future behavior but does not retroactively upgrade earlier feats. Hidden potential is not usable capacity before activation.

“The player secretly always had infinite power” is invalid unless all earlier limits, costs, signs, counters, and history were consistent with it—which would normally destroy the mortal premise anyway.

## 8. Cross-realm conflict

Realm difference constrains mechanisms; it is not an automatic hit, immunity, or damage multiplier.

Resolution asks:

1. Can the actor perceive/acquire the target?
2. Can the action manifest under current laws?
3. Can it reach in time and space?
4. Does the relevant attack dimension overcome or bypass the relevant defense?
5. Can the actor control the result?
6. What counter window exists?
7. What residual effect remains?
8. Who supplied each capability?

A lower-realm actor may defeat, escape, expose, delay, poison, negotiate with, trap, or thwart a higher-realm actor through a genuine vulnerability, objective, information gap, terrain, preparation, artifact, authority, or social mechanism. They may not directly overpower a defended higher-realm dimension simply because they are the player. Ordinary sword contact does not become privileged chip damage against a body it cannot cut.

Higher realms receive no unexplained blanket immunity either. Every resistance has scope, source, cost, signal, and counter.

## 9. Exposure and social life

NPCs learn about the advantage only through perception, evidence, testimony, institutions, divination, causal senses, or declared law channels.

Observed anomalous capability can create:

- curiosity, rumor, fear, worship, disbelief;
- recruitment, teaching, patronage, marriage or lineage politics;
- theft, dissection, imprisonment, coercion, or protection attempts;
- sect/state ownership claims and legal dispute;
- counter-technique research;
- false attribution and impersonation;
- doctrinal crisis; and
- deliberate concealment play.

Secrecy is not a binary meter. Different audiences know different claims with different confidence and motives.

## 11. Permanent exploit gauntlet

- Stack every modifier at each realm ceiling in every legal order.
- Split one prohibited feat into many nominally legal sub-actions.
- Clone full player state to an NPC and require outcome parity.
- Remove the advantage and verify only declared contributions disappear.
- Transfer, lend, steal, copy, possess, or reincarnate with the advantage.
- Duplicate bearer identity through body copies, soul copies, names, saves, containers, or tier promotion.
- Launder artifact, patron, ally, environmental, or prepared power into personal progression credit.
- Test mentor absence, error, conflicting motive, resource depletion, and inability to rescue.
- Test comprehension against time, information quality, body, attention, false models, and incompatible practice.
- Test devouring for conversion loss, contamination, identity/memory effects, capacity, and social/ecological consequence.
- Test resource multiplication against nonfungibility, market collapse, detection, seizure, rate, and closed loops.
- Test tribulation redirection, cancellation, enemy targeting, recharge, farming, collateral, and repeated manipulation.
- Save, close, reload, suspend, and crash during every advantage phase; costs and debt remain atomic.
- Hide witnesses; verify knowledge does not propagate magically.
- Use the advantage offscreen; protected outcomes remain equivalent.
- Attack every defense facet of a much higher-realm target; only genuine gaps succeed.
- Verify control assists change input/presentation, not canonical physics.
- Run the Qi Condensation mountain proof: personal contribution fails; a prepared/external/environmental feat passes only if its ledger covers every missing dimension.

## 12. Honest attribution language

- **“You split the mountain.”** Personal contribution satisfies the approved predicate.
- **“You used the seal to split the mountain.”** Artifact contribution.
- **“Your formation split the mountain.”** Prepared contribution.
- **“You caused the mountain to collapse.”** Environmental leverage supplied the work.
- **“The borrowed decree split it at your command.”** External authority.

Player planning and agency still receive credit. The text does not falsify personal strength.

## 14. Gate before selecting the actual advantage

1. Lock a provisional Qi Condensation capability envelope with explicit personal hard ceilings.
2. Define identity continuity across death, reincarnation, possession, and copying.
3. Model source/contribution ledgers and stacking algebra.
4. Decide which protagonist fantasies require asymmetry and which should come from ordinary agency.
5. Compare at least seven abstract advantage operations without importing proprietary expressions.
6. Stress each candidate against progression, economy, mystery, institutions, concealment, death, and endgame law authorship.
7. Reject any candidate that makes mortal life a fake tutorial, invalidates other cultivators, or guarantees survival.
8. Run blinded originality and seasoned-xianxia-reader panels.
9. Present the surviving two or three candidates to the user with explicit tradeoffs.
10. Only the user ratifies the protagonist’s true advantage.

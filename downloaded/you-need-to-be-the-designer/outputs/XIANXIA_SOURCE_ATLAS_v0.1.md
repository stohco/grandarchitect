# Xianxia Source Atlas v0.1

**Project:** Grand Architect Xianxia Sandbox  
**Status:** reviewed research backbone; not canon  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Scope:** sources that can help design an original, single-player xianxia universe from mortal life to world-law authorship

---

## 0. What this atlas is for

This is not a popularity ranking and it is not a recipe for combining famous novels. It is a research-routing document. Its job is to tell the research swarm:

1. which sources can answer which design questions;
2. which claims are verified, inferred, or merely promising;
3. which expressions are source-specific and therefore excluded from the project;
4. what must be compared before an idea may become an original design candidate; and
5. where our corpus is culturally, historically, mechanically, or legally incomplete.

The project is single-player. Scores therefore reward depth, continuity, causal legibility, and reactivity; they do not reward networking or co-op suitability.

### The central definition

**Implementably descriptive** means that a source yields concrete, internally related facts about several of the following:

- body and cultivation phenomenology;
- perception and altered consciousness;
- materials, craft, medicine, and failure;
- households, work, travel, law, education, and ritual;
- resource provenance, ecology, logistics, and scarcity;
- institutions and their material reasons for existing;
- readable technique behavior, counters, costs, and traces;
- history that visibly changes later places and people;
- spatial, temporal, social, and cosmological scale; and
- consequences that could become decisions for the player.

Length and ornate prose alone do not count.

## 1. Evidence, access, and confidence

Every source uses independent fields:

| Field | Allowed values |
|---|---|
| `identity_verified` | yes / no / disputed |
| `access_and_rights_status` | cleared-open / cleared-licensed / metadata-only / unresolved / blocked |
| `close_read_status` | not-started / sampled / complete-for-scope / independently-reviewed |
| `design_lens_status` | discovery / provisional / verified-for-scope / rejected |
| `evidence_grade` | A / B / C / D per claim, never per whole work |
| `score_confidence` | low / moderate / high with reviewer rationale |

The corpus tables below display only **current design-lens status**. Bibliographic verification or an official synopsis never verifies a system claim, a close reading, or a score. These fields describe research state, not literary quality.

### Evidence grades for extracted claims

| Grade | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **A** | Exact primary, critical, public-domain, or licensed-edition close reading, with location and reviewer. |
| **B** | Peer-reviewed scholarship, qualified translator analysis, or a strong academic reference. |
| **C** | Licensed publisher/platform metadata or an official synopsis. |
| **D** | Community discussion or reputation used only to discover a candidate. |

No canon proposal may rely solely on Grade C or D evidence. A modern copyrighted work can inform a design question, but cannot supply the project’s proprietary answer.

## 2. Scoring rubric

Each dimension is scored from 1–5. A score is a research-priority hypothesis until the relevant close reading is complete.

| Code | Dimension | A score of 5 means |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | Mortal texture | Convincing households, labor, illness, travel, scarcity, law, and ordinary social life. |
| **C** | Cultivation phenomenology | Practice transforms body, senses, mind, identity, lifespan, habits, and social conduct. |
| **O** | Ontology coherence | Power, death, spirits, realms, causation, and cosmic law derive from intelligible principles. |
| **I** | Institutions | Sects, clans, courts, temples, schools, and bureaucracies have distinct functions and pressures. |
| **E** | Ecology and economy | Resources have provenance, niches, logistics, regeneration, scarcity, markets, and consequences. |
| **T** | Technical arts | Alchemy, medicine, artifacts, formations, talismans, and craft behave as developed systems. |
| **A** | Action and combat | Powers yield readable choices, counters, terrain interaction, commitment, and embodied risk. |
| **P** | Politics and consequence | Reputation, legitimacy, doctrine, loyalty, law, social cost, and any distinct metaphysical consequence systems remain causally legible rather than collapsing into one meter. |
| **L** | Long causality | Events remain consequential across generations, lives, realms, and historical periods. |
| **X** | Transferability | Cross-comparison can yield non-derivative design patterns useful to the game. |

Score anchors: **1** absent or unusable; **2** occasional/shallow; **3** meaningful but incomplete; **4** strong and recurrent; **5** unusually deep, connected, and implementably specific. Every cell stores cited justification, confidence, and reviewer. Scores are never averaged across reviewers without retaining disagreement. A high provisional score is a priority question, not a conclusion.

## 5. Originality boundaries by research lens

The researchers must record both `abstractable_question` and `must_not_copy` for every modern source.

| Source family | We may investigate | We exclude |
|---|---|---|
| Classical philosophy and cultivation texts | Competing models of self, transformation, relation, nature, attention, and cosmology. | Claims that one text defines all Daoism; decontextualized scripture as literal game physics; unsafe real-world alchemical instruction. |
| Journey to the West | Overlapping jurisdictions, genealogy, office, transformation, administrative comedy. | Pilgrim party, episodes, signature weapons, named celestial cast, plot sequence. |
| Mortal’s Journey | Caution, limited access, provenance, preparation, resource scarcity, mundane origin. | Protagonist, bottle, realm sequence, treasures, sects, recipes, route, scenes. |
| Er Gen works | Long causal planting, memory across lives, reinterpretation, domains as lived comprehension, cosmological scale reveal. | Connected-cosmos topology, named worlds, realm ladders, characters, artifacts, signature motifs, catchphrases, revelation order. |
| Beyond the Timescape | Cultivation as bodily and ecological risk; medicine, labor, burial, and architecture under a hostile law-climate. | Its divine pollution, mutations, gods, geography, factions, or survival plot. |
| Sword of Coming | Moral argument embodied by locality, landscape, education, institutions, and long spatial enlargement. | Named schools, towns, grottoes, characters, dialogues, realm structures, and plot. |
| Mao Ni works: Way of Choices and The Path Toward Heaven | Institutions, education, medicine, state/religious authority, fate debates, and advanced-cultivator perspective. | Named academies, objects, religions, polities, characters, techniques, and political arcs. |
| Reverend Insanity | Upkeep, ecological niches, counterfactual plans, strategic institutions, costly information. | Gu ontology, apertures, ranks, recipes, regions, reincarnation device, protagonist, nihilism as project default. |
| Industrial/modern cultivation | Public education, standards, mass logistics, automation, communication, and ideology. | Setting, institutions, species, technologies, conflicts, or terminology of the source. |
| Dwarf Fortress / Caves of Qud | History before play; causality becoming places, objects, testimony, and disputes. | Their cultures, characters, lore voice, factions, mutations, or maximal detail without player relevance. |
| No Mortal Space | The design problem of making each power scale introduce different verbs. | Deity terminology, progression labels, content, assets, spatial sack, outpost/captured-god structures, co-op sect design, UI, or advertised feature composition. |

### Founding originality rule

> A modern work may contribute a question, never the project’s proprietary answer.

A feature inspired by modern fiction uses the following as ideation heuristics, never as a legal or originality safe harbor:

1. at least one historical or scholarly control;
2. comparison across at least two materially different modern works;
3. an independent player-experience or simulation need;
4. documented transformation across ontology, incentives, actors, scale, consequences, presentation, and player verbs wherever materially relevant;
5. a negative-constraint record listing what must not be reproduced; and
6. an originality red-team review before canon promotion.

No source count or changed-axis count proves originality. Every proposal still requires holistic topology/substantial-similarity review, signature-element exclusion, independent derivation, machine-readable `must_not_copy` relationships, and blinded genre-expert review. Renaming a distinctive ladder, artifact, faction, cosmological map, or plot is not transformation.

### Protagonist-asymmetry comparison track

Research “golden finger” patterns only as design risks and abstract operations, never as features to import. Each candidate is classified on two axes:

- **Locus:** embodied, informational, relational, institutional, carried, or environmental.
- **Privileged operation:** discern, learn, integrate, preserve, authorize, access, or adapt.

Every `AdvantageContract` must record lawful origin, uniqueness, discovery stages, operation, realm ceiling, inputs, costs, opportunity costs, counters, detection, replication/theft, disclosure risk, institutional response, failure/degradation, transfer/death, mundane usefulness, and endgame role. No category grants automatic success or player-only physics.

## 6. Coverage gaps that block a coherent universe

1. **Mortal social history:** agriculture, household organization, land tenure, roads, famine, water, crafts, markets, medicine, law, and bureaucracy require historical sources outside xianxia.
2. **Local religion and ritual labor:** temples, registers, festivals, ancestor rites, communal cults, priests, spirit mediums, exorcists, burial, and petitions need separate research.
3. **Buddhist, Confucian, Daoist, medical, and folk distinctions:** karma, rebirth, merit, filial duty, ritual propriety, mandate, return, qi, and fate cannot become one “Eastern spirituality” meter.
4. **Women’s and household perspectives:** the core corpus is too male-author and male-protagonist heavy. Lawful female-authored and female-centered sources must be added.
5. **Minority and frontier representation:** “tribal,” “barbarian,” “demonic,” and geographic periphery require historical and cultural review rather than inherited genre shorthand.
6. **Alchemy material history and safety:** minerals, furnaces, agriculture, medicine, toxicity, waste, and labor require scholarship; the game must not present hazardous historical recipes as advice.
7. **Economics under supernatural capability:** storage, flight, teleportation, longevity, spirit labor, fabrication, prediction, and world travel need explicit scarcity and institutional models.
8. **Immortal psychology:** grief, memory capacity, identity drift, changing language, descendants, boredom, long projects, voluntary forgetting, and attachment need their own research track.
9. **Readable 3D action:** prose is insufficient for telegraphing, animation commitment, spatial tactics, camera, collision, counterplay, and combat at many body scales.
10. **Access:** World of Cultivation and Ascending, Do Not Disturb remain discovery candidates until licensed access or an authorized Chinese edition plus qualified bilingual review is available.
11. **Early web-xianxia history:** candidates such as *Zhu Xian* need legal-edition and unique-contribution review.
12. **Single-player information design:** causal depth must be exposed through senses, rumors, records, testimony, maps, behavior, and consequences without making the player read a lore database.

Each gap becomes a Production risk with owner, minimum source diversity, required artifact, acceptance test, and escalation path. v0.2 requires a work order for all twelve—not only the first and fourth. Readable 3D action receives a separate hands-on corpus covering controls, timing, commitment, cancel windows, camera, collision, AI, terrain, feedback, and high-scale readability. Public marketing for an unreleased game remains metadata about a promise, not evidence for unobserved mechanics.

## 7. Close-reading sequence

### Phase A — conceptual controls

1. Daodejing, Zhuangzi, and Neiye in parallel.
2. Yijing/Xici and Huainanzi.
3. Cantong qi and Baopuzi.
4. Mountains and Seas with grotto-heaven and sacred-geography scholarship.
5. Journey to the West.

**Release:** terminology register separating historical concept, later development, translation choice, genre convention, source-specific expression, and project invention.

### Phase B — mortal ground and institutions

1. Selected Shushan arcs with scholarly guidance.
2. Mortal’s Journey: opening through one complete cultivation ecology and the first scale transition.
3. Way of Choices: academy–religion–state relations.
4. Sword of Coming through a qualified bilingual researcher.
5. World of Cultivation only after legal access is resolved.

**Release:** mortal-life model, institutional function catalog, resource-provenance catalog, and first progression comparison.

### Phase C — continuity and cosmological escalation

1. Renegade Immortal.
2. Pursuit of the Truth.
3. I Shall Seal the Heavens.
4. A Will Eternal as tonal and social counterweight.
5. Beyond the Timescape.

Record recurrence structures only: which facts persist, which are reinterpreted, how scale is revealed, and what a new reader can understand without prior canon.

**Release:** continuity-pattern atlas and cosmology-revelation failure modes.

### Phase D — counter-models

1. Reverend Insanity for adversarial resources and planning.
2. Grandmaster of Demonic Cultivation and Heaven Official’s Blessing for reputation, clans, celestial office, worship, ghosts, and long memory.
3. Cultivation Chat Group for information networks.
4. Forty Millenniums for industrial and state-scale cultivation.
5. Lord of Mysteries for knowledge risk and institutional progression.

**Release:** alternative social orders and non-default ethical models.

### Phase E — procedural translation

1. Caves of Qud: authored structure plus causal procedural history.
2. Dwarf Fortress: prehistory simulation, persistence, and the cost of legibility.
3. No Mortal Space: compare public power-scale promises against our realm-proof requirements.

**Release:** history-to-world-state grammar, information surfaces, and anti-noise evaluation set.

### Consistent comparison slices for long serials

- mortal opening;
- first teacher or institutional entry;
- first complete breakthrough;
- first resource/crafting loop;
- first major faction conflict;
- first death, ghost, reincarnation, or identity-continuity case;
- first scale transition;
- one late cosmological arc; and
- ending or postscript if complete.

This creates comparable evidence. Reading random “best arcs” does not.

### Xianxia terminology and niche-translation record

Research may preserve source language; final player-facing canon must communicate in appropriate, evocative English. Each translated concept records:

```text
Original Hanzi:
Tonal pinyin:
Literal gloss:
Attested and context-specific meanings:
Historical/religious scope:
Modern genre usage:
Project meaning:
Chosen player-facing term:
Term category: established xianxia loanword | proper name | translated technical term | context-retained romanization
Rejected alternatives and why:
Risk of false equivalence or lost distinction:
Bilingual/cultural reviewer:
Romanization-retention rationale, if uncommon:
```

Realm names, techniques, items, offices, materials, places, species, institutions, interface labels, and generated prose use the approved seasoned-reader lexicon. Established English xianxia terms remain; niche or opaque romanizations are translated into appropriate English. An uncommon retained romanization must be contextually justified and explained.

The safe research route is:

```text
located source claim
→ historically or fictionally situated concept
→ cross-source pattern
→ independent gameplay need
→ original design candidate
→ world-law proposal
→ mechanic and content grammar
→ gauntlet test
→ explicit canon decision
```

There is no direct `source-specific expression → original canon` edge.

## 10. Gate to Source Atlas v0.2

v0.2 may be released when:

- all 30 candidates have verified identity, edition/access, and rights metadata;
- every shortlisted score has at least one cited justification per dimension scored 4 or 5;
- the terminology register covers the first 20 foundational terms;
- all twelve coverage gaps have owners, evidence-diversity requirements, work products, and exit tests; mortal social history and women’s perspectives each have at least five qualified sources;
- at least three long serials have been sampled using the common comparison slices;
- provisional sources remain excluded from design synthesis until access is lawful;
- cultural review and originality review have independently signed off; and
- every proposed insight can be traced into the Evidence Graph without becoming canon.

Until then, these rankings remain a routing instrument, not a declaration of truth.

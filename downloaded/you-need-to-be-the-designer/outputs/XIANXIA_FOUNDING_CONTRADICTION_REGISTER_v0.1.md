# Founding Contradiction & Originality Register v0.1

**Status:** pre-canon design challenges  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Purpose:** prevent attractive ideas from becoming incompatible world laws, copied structures, cultural caricature, or shallow power escalation

---

## 0. How to use this register

These are not forty-two decisions that one person should answer in a burst. Each is a persistent graph object with:

```text
question
competing hypotheses
evidence and counterevidence
affected canon nodes
affected player verbs and systems
prototype or thought experiment
pass/fail conditions
risk owner
review history
explicit decision or waiver
reopening triggers
```

The list is founding, not final. Every ontology expansion, canon proposal, experiment, or defect may create more contradictions.

## 1. Cosmology and metaphysics

| # | Decision required | Why it matters | Required proof |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is the Way objective truth, interpretation, or both? | Doctrine diversity is cosmetic if only one belief can interact with reality. Unlimited relativism makes consequences arbitrary. | Three doctrines must make different, partially valid predictions about one phenomenon while remaining constrained by shared evidence. |
| 2 | Are world laws immutable, emergent, inherited, negotiated, or authored? | This determines cosmological continuity and the final player ceiling. | Alter one law and trace consequences through physics, bodies, ecology, craft, society, technique, history, language, and saves. Unexplained exceptions fail. |
| 3 | Why do separate worlds, heavens, domains, or layers exist? | Otherwise they are level-gated maps. | One model must explain formation, separation, boundaries, travel, law variation, resource flows, information barriers, and ancient contact. |
| 4 | Is existence finite, unbounded, cyclic, branching, or regenerating? | Infinite quantity can destroy scarcity, uniqueness, and discovery. | Long-play comparisons must preserve memorable geography and strategic distinction without pretending an unbounded universe is fully simulated. |
| 5 | What is qi, where does it come from, and what limits it? | This affects ecology, economy, cultivation, combat, craft, and infinite-energy exploits. | Test production, depletion, recovery, storage, leakage, conversion, hoarding, environmental flow, and closed-loop engines. |
| 6 | Is the cosmos anthropocentric? | Human-shaped metaphysics, bodies, societies, and ladders make the universe feel fabricated. | Generate and reason through nonhuman cultivation that does not reproduce human anatomy, sects, needs, language, or progression. |

## 2. Death, identity, fate, and moral causality

| # | Decision required | Why it matters | Required proof |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 7 | What survives death: body, memory, soul, name, relationships, debts, ownership, or pattern? | These components need not persist together, and every ghost or return depends on the answer. | Run inheritance, haunting, reincarnation, resurrection, soul damage, possession, copying, and final death through one identity model. |

| 10 | Is fate prediction, constraint, probability pressure, story generated by observers, or compulsion? | Prophecy can enrich planning or erase agency. | Test avoidable, conditional, self-fulfilling, manipulated, partial, obsolete, and false prophecies. |
| 11 | Can revival duplicate a person? | Duplication breaks relationships, succession, guilt, law, property, and stakes. | Compare corpse animation, soul return, memory copy, body clone, reincarnation, possession, and time reversal; state who inherits each identity relation. |

## 9. Ten transformations from inspiration into original design questions

These examples demonstrate the safe research operation: observation → original pressure → explicit exclusion.

1. **Visible power scaling** becomes a realm proof matrix: every breakthrough changes perception, locomotion, interaction scale, responsibility, vulnerability, relation to labor, and at least one constraint. It does not import No Mortal Space’s ranks, items, structures, UI, assets, or co-op.
2. **Connected multiverse continuity** becomes a law-ancestry graph: independent worlds interpret ancient pressures differently, and later knowledge recontextualizes without erasing local truth. It does not import Er Gen’s topology, characters, realms, artifacts, or reveal order.
3. **Cautious mortal advancement** becomes uncertainty about teacher reliability, manual provenance, side effects, adulteration, aptitude disclosure, and safe experimentation. It does not import Mortal’s Journey’s protagonist, treasure, sects, recipes, or route.
4. **Nested cosmic scale** becomes a rule that every horizon answers a question below it and introduces a more fundamental dependency. Travel requires material and conceptual compatibility. It does not import Desolate Era’s worlds, characters, Daos, treasures, or progression.
5. **Cultivation under contamination** becomes world-specific law climates affecting bodies, medicine, labor, architecture, burial, ecology, and travel. It does not import Beyond the Timescape’s gods, pollution ontology, mutations, factions, or setting.
6. **Institutions as philosophy** becomes competing evidence interpretations embodied in education, calendars, offices, training, architecture, diplomacy, and legitimacy. It does not import Mao Ni’s schools, objects, characters, or political arcs.
7. **Power as ecology and logistics** becomes original symbioses—mineral colonies, mnemonic trees, oath-fed spirits, crafted organs, or weather organisms—with habitats, substitutes, reproduction, ethical disputes, and supply chains. It does not import Gu, apertures, ranks, recipes, creatures, or protagonist.
8. **Hazardous knowledge** becomes identity-bearing insight, safe interpretive contexts, perceptual bias, anchors, secrecy incentives, and social incompatibility. It does not import Lord of Mysteries’ Pathways, Sequences, potions, Tarot, churches, setting, or madness rules.
9. **Alchemy and self–world correspondence** becomes doctrines that specify what the self is, what is refined, how place participates, what progress demonstrates, and what kind of person practice tends to produce. It does not reproduce scriptures, diagrams, sacred names, or claims of religious authenticity.
10. **Procedural history** becomes the canonical grammar `cause → actor → intent → method → cost → state change → witnesses → interpretations → future pressure`. It does not import proprietary factions, event text, UI, quest templates, or lore voice.

## 10. Cultural-flattening gauntlet

A proposal fails when it:

- treats qi only as blue mana;
- maps yin and yang onto good and evil;
- merges Daoist, Buddhist, Confucian, folk-religious, medical, alchemical, wuxia, and web-fiction concepts;
- presents one timeless and monolithic “ancient China”;
- gives every region the same roofs, clothing, food, labor, kinship, funeral, government, sect, and philosophy;
- uses sects only as MMO guilds;
- uses talismans only as spell scrolls;
- reduces geomancy to one universal magic grid;
- treats the Way, karma, mandate, reincarnation, immortality, fate, and `all-under-heaven` as synonyms;
- makes mortals only collateral, servants, or admirers;
- makes nonhuman people palette swaps or single-trait species stereotypes;
- uses architecture ornamentally without climate, material, labor, ritual, defense, or status;
- builds names from pseudo-Chinese syllable soup;
- treats “the strong consume the weak” as the only moral philosophy; or
- represents an invention as a definitive account of a living religion.

Required promotion evidence:

1. terminology card distinguishing source language, historical context, religious context, fiction convention, translation choice, and project invention;
2. bilingual and qualified cultural/scholarly review where relevant;
3. regional differentiation audit across ecology, food, work, kinship, architecture, death, education, governance, ritual, and doctrine;
4. mortal-dignity audit;
5. internal-disagreement audit showing that cultures are not monoliths; and
6. established-xianxia and niche-translation terminology audit.

### Xianxia terminology and translation test

Research retains original Hanzi, tonal pinyin, literal glosses, context meanings, translation alternatives, and reviewer notes. Player-facing text keeps established English-language xianxia vocabulary—including qi, Qi Condensation, Dao, yin–yang, spiritual roots, meridians, cultivation, and tribulation. Niche or opaque romanizations that a seasoned English-speaking reader would not understand are translated into appropriate English. An uncommon romanization requires a deliberate contextual rationale and, where relevant, bilingual/cultural review.

Fail on:

- unexplained niche romanization;
- inconsistent translations for one concept;
- one English word collapsing several materially different concepts;
- word-for-word English that misstates the project meaning;
- pseudo-Chinese generated names;
- different romanizations being treated as different things;
- a translated skill or item name promising behavior its semantic contract does not perform; or
- overtranslating established xianxia vocabulary into awkward substitutes; or
- leaving an obscure term untranslated merely because it appeared as pinyin in a source.

## 11. Derivative-copying red team

Similarity is structural, not merely lexical.

### Prohibited resemblance

- unique realm ladders or cosmological topology;
- signature artifact functions, technique constraints, resources, or advancement rituals;
- recognizable character and faction relationship graphs;
- famous scene choreography, plot beats, or revelation order;
- distinctive institution combinations;
- catchphrases, prose cadence, visual compositions, or lore voice;
- source characters with names and details changed; and
- mosaics assembled from recognizable proprietary pieces of several works.

### Mandatory tests

1. **Reference-free pitch:** explain the proposal without naming inspirations.
2. **Nearest-source test:** genre experts should not consistently identify one proprietary source.
3. **Cross-source abstraction:** use genuinely independent observations plus an original gameplay or world-coherence need; no number of sources is a safe harbor.
4. **Negative-constraint record:** list forbidden borrowed elements before ideation.
5. **Topology test:** compare entity and relationship structure, not vocabulary.
6. **Scene-reconstruction test:** the system must not predictably reproduce a famous scene.
7. **Character-substitution test:** a source protagonist must not fit with minor changes.
8. **Aesthetic-removal test:** without art and terminology, the feature still arises from this project’s laws.
9. **Independent-derivation log:** record the contradiction and evidence that selected the feature.
10. **Corpus-leakage test:** runtime generation cannot retrieve or imitate passages, terminology, or authorial voice.
11. **Blind originality panel:** include expert genre readers and unfamiliar players.
12. **Transformation-distance test:** compare ontology, incentives, scale, actors, consequences, presentation, and player verbs holistically. Changing three axes never proves originality by itself.

These are ideation and red-team tools, not numerical originality formulas. Every proposal still requires holistic topology/substantial-similarity review, independent derivation, signature-element exclusion, and blinded genre-expert adjudication.

## 12. Canon-promotion rule

A proposition may be recommended for canon only when:

1. it resolves a registered contradiction or fulfills a founding player promise;
2. observations, abstractions, independent rationale, and exclusions are traceable;
3. it can be explained without reference names;
4. it passes cultural-flattening, English-terminology, and derivative-topology review;
5. it defines effects on at least three dependent systems;
6. any described affordance has a literal semantic action contract;
7. it survives adversarial scenario, persistence, scaling, and exploitation tests;
8. it has a stable identity, owner, version, and world-time scope;
9. incompatible propositions are decided explicitly rather than blended;
10. generated prose and visuals remain representations of canonical state; and
11. a human with authority performs final promotion.

> Inspiration may contribute a question, pressure, or pattern—never its proprietary answer.

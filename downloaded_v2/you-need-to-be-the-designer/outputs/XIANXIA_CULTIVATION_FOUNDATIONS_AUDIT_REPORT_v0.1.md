## 1. Severity and review model

- **S0:** foundational contradiction, unsafe/IP-invalid direction, nontermination/data-corruption class, or architecture that cannot satisfy the project.
- **S1:** release blocker: material cultural/source error, gameplay-destroying exploit, causal/deterministic gap, false guarantee, unbounded process, or reference-hardware infeasibility.
- **S2/S3:** improvement or editorial work that does not block this research release.

The primary architect retained synthesis and acceptance authority. Three independent adversarial roles reviewed in parallel:

1. xianxia/source/cultural ontology;
2. gameplay, controls and exploit behavior; and
3. simulation, determinism, persistence, catastrophe and reference-hardware feasibility.

Reviewers were instructed to reject or bound the work, not to endorse its ambition.

## 3. Supplied Er Gen/Gemini proposal audit

### 3.1 Source and cultural blockers

Two S1 findings were repaired and independently closed:

1. The replacement vocabulary still risked turning Dao into an access-control or jurisdiction system. The contract is now explicitly nondiegetic; generic authority/jurisdiction language became relation-specific attainment, claim scope and support; the future package distinguishes world patterns, *fa*/methods, Daos/paths, domains, vows, formations and actual offices.
2. Several broadly correct Er Gen conclusions lacked claim-level citations. The audit added/narrowed RI, PoT, AWWP and BTT evidence; separated speaker-relative comparison from narrator-certified taxonomy; distinguished Summer Immortal from Lower Immortal; and localized the divine-sense example to ISSTH.

Final source/cultural disposition: **PASS; zero remaining S0/S1.**

### 3.2 Gameplay and exploit blockers

Six S1 findings were repaired:

| Finding | Repair |
|---|---|
| semantic fields were not a composition algebra | added bounded `TECH-DAG-v0.1`, shared rolling contribution/cost ledger, typed reads/writes/reservations, ordering/concurrency/exclusion, partial milestones, fail-closed recursion and permutation/save/NPC fixtures |
| intent/safety could guarantee nonlethal outcomes or reveal hidden state | compiled intent as fallible aim/magnitude/stop preferences over `AuthorizedInformationView`; UI feasibility is believed/uncertain and may be wrong |
| doctrine policies could create zero-latency perfect autoplay | made policy evaluation a semantic action with reaction latency, priority, hysteresis, cooldown, saturation, resource arbitration, closure cap and player/NPC parity |
| four control layers could grow without bound or depend on frame rate/device dexterity | added `CONTROL-SURFACE-v0.1`: finite prepared/radial sets, deterministic perceived targeting, scheduler-time milestones, buffer/cancel rules, device-independent semantic geometry and accessibility tests |
| auto-loot could become an ownership/trap/life oracle or duplicate custody | changed truth labels to evidence-based appearance/unknown states and added idempotent `COLLECT-BATCH-v0.1` milestone reservations, movement, revalidation, partial receipt and save tests |
| terrain carving validated topology only once | added `TERRAIN-TXN-v0.1` with per-milestone moving-occupancy/topology/front recertification, interleaved support/drain/vent, conserved spoil/remaps, overlapping edit arbitration and catastrophe escalation |

The repair re-audit then found five second-order S1 interactions and closed them:

- doctrine conflict selection now honors actor-authored obligation/priority before deadlines, feasibility, known threat and final exact identifier ties;
- every recursive technique class now supplies a conservation-preserving terminal reducer, while closure-bound exhaustion is a specification defect rather than an arbitrary gameplay outcome;
- collection and terrain leases prevent duplicate commit but confer no possession, force, claim or collision priority;
- hard-pause remapping cannot arm an unprepared technique/artifact/doctrine/anchor without its ordinary semantic preparation; and
- universal convenience-compiler parity now covers target proposals, accessibility geometry, auto-collection, terrain blueprints and high-realm macro delegation as well as combat techniques.

Final supplied-proposal gameplay disposition: **PASS; zero remaining S0/S1.**

**Status:** pre-canon semantic constitution  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Scope:** everything the eventual universe bible must know, generate, simulate, present, and test

---

## 0. Purpose

This document defines the shape of the eventual universe encyclopedia. It does not yet lock realm names, maps, factions, history, species, or cosmology.

The encyclopedia is not a lore archive. It is the human-readable surface of canonical world state and behavior contracts. If a page says something exists or can happen, it must identify what makes it true, how it changes, who can perceive it, what the player can do with it, and which gauntlet proves it.

Historical grounding remains carefully labeled. Daoist traditions are heterogeneous; later cultivation models are specific developments rather than universal facts of all Chinese religion ([Stanford: Religious Daoism](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2024/entries/daoism-religion/)). The five phases are dynamic relationships and processes rather than five immutable fantasy substances ([Stanford: Chinese metaphysics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/chinese-metaphysics/)). Karma must remain distinct from reputation, celestial statute, fate, luck, and generic causality. Sacred-geography research such as [grotto-heaven studies](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/72h4g7h7) offers a historically situated comparative model, not content to copy.

### Three truth layers

1. **World truth:** what canonical state currently entails.
2. **Actor belief:** what a person, sect, spirit, institution, or culture believes.
3. **Presentation:** what a sense, tooltip, rumor, map, manual, vision, record, or narrator communicates.

These may disagree intentionally. They may not be silently conflated.

## 2. Global invariants

1. No orphan noun, effect, resource, institution, description, or event.
2. No effect without substrate/authority, path, conditions, costs, traces, and counters.
3. Rare phenomena require rare causal histories, not merely low random rolls.
4. Higher realms add qualitative verbs, perception, responsibility, coupling, and constraints—not only statistics.
5. NPCs and the protagonist use the same laws and resolution rules. A unique protagonist opportunity must be explicitly modeled rather than implemented as a hidden exception.
6. Distance, time, logistics, information, and jurisdiction remain meaningful at every scale.
7. Loaded and abstracted simulation obey the same canonical rules and conserved facts.
8. Exceptions are explicit entities with cause, scope, authority, and cost.
9. Procedural prose describes state; it does not create state.
10. Descriptions are semantic contracts and cannot exceed executable behavior.
11. Source-specific modern names, ladders, characters, artifacts, plots, institution graphs, or cosmologies do not enter canon by renaming.
12. Player-facing terminology follows established English-language xianxia usage while translating niche romanizations for seasoned English-speaking readers.
13. The absolute Dao is not an inventory object, owned resource, exhaustible boss, or final stat.
14. Every page exposes player verbs, failure, discoverability, and at least two gauntlet obligations.
15. The protagonist may have a unique lawful asymmetry but never player-only physics, free provenance, automatic comprehension, guaranteed survival, or permission to exceed the realm capability envelope without sufficient external cause.
16. Original invention preserves the xianxia spine: cultivation toward immortality/transcendence through qi, body, soul/spirit, Dao comprehension, lineage, institutions, alchemical/cosmological transformation, and self–world consequence. Xuanhuan-style genre mixing is not allowed to displace that spine accidentally.

# Part I — Metaphysical substrate

## 3. Cosmology and world topology

**Must know:** domain identity, parents/neighbors, origin, age, scale, boundary type, law profile, clock, entry/exit, inhabitants, resource cycles, jurisdictions, anchors, failure/collapse.

**Player verbs:** discover, map, enter, leave, anchor, open, seal, stabilize, damage, repair, found, abandon.

**Generation:** cosmological pressure → stable law-knot → boundary → sustaining cycle → adapted life → contested access → historical trace.

**Gauntlet:** Why does this domain exist? What fails when its anchor breaks? How do mortal and ascendant senses encounter the same boundary?

## 4. Fundamental ontology and world laws

**Player verbs:** observe, test, infer, exploit, harmonize, resist, violate, draft, amend, revoke, teach.

**Generation:** repeated phenomenon → inferred regularity → named law → users/institutions → edge case → refinement, counter-law, or recognized exception.

**Gauntlet:** Can a universal claim be falsified? What occurs when jurisdictions overlap? Who can notice a violation?

## 5. Matter, qi, polarity, and phase

**Player verbs:** sense, gather, circulate, condense, refine, vent, blend, separate, cleanse, contaminate, seed.

**Generation:** environmental process → signature → material/organism adaptation → usable resource → technique/institution → extraction consequence.

**Gauntlet:** What sustains an “infinite” spring? What happens when incompatible rhythms mix? What residue proves refinement occurred?

## 6. Time, space, scale, and continuity

**Must know:** coordinates, topology, distance, scale band, local clock, calendar mapping, dilation, recurrence, anchor, travel duration, synchronization, persistence.

**Player verbs:** wait, travel, pursue, intercept, accelerate, slow, anchor, synchronize, date, reconstruct.

**Generation:** law and mass/history → topology → path → bottleneck → regular users → alteration/decay.

**Gauntlet:** What changes during 200 years away? How do different clock rates reconcile? Why does a caravan still use a pass?

## 7. Causal, religious, political, and probabilistic modules

Ordinary causality is mandatory. Fate pressure, karma, mandate, celestial statute, and luck are separate optional ontology modules until canon decides which exist as world truth, which are local interpretations, and which do not exist. They are never one universal destiny system.

**Must know for each enabled module:** initiating event, agent, intention and knowledge where relevant, affected relations, causal descendants, jurisdiction, religious/ethical interpretation, social judgment, statute, fate pressure, probability change, evidence, counter, and discharge. Fields do not transfer automatically between modules.

**Player verbs:** vow, incur, repay, atone, inherit, transfer, divine, conceal, sever, fulfill, exploit.

**Generation:** action + relation + jurisdiction → ordinary consequences and trace; each enabled cultural or metaphysical module separately interprets or transforms that trace under its own rules.

**Gauntlet:** How do accident, coercion, and deliberation differ? Can debt transfer without consent? Can good reputation coexist with hostile fate?

## 8. Spirits, ancestors, celestial offices, and nether jurisdictions

These are candidate super-types, not a mandatory bureaucratic cosmology. A world or culture may support none, some, or incompatible local models.

**Must know:** origin, identity continuity, territory, office, registry, worship/support, duties, authority, enforcement, petition, rival, appeal, installation, removal, death state.

**Player verbs:** petition, honor, register, summon, negotiate, litigate, expose, install, depose, accept/refuse office.

**Generation:** choose an ontically supported origin—dead-person continuity, transformed being, place/ecological process, appointed officeholder, constructed agent, prehuman being, communal recognition, or disputed belief → identity and needs → territory/relationship → recognition or conflict → history. Communal need is one possible origin, not the universal one.

**Gauntlet:** What survives temple destruction? Which authority hears an unregistered ghost? What happens when two offices claim one river?

## 8A. Lived religion, ritual labor, and household practice

**Must know:** culture/period, truth layer, participants, specialists, household/community role, place, calendar, offerings/material labor, petition, efficacy claims, internal disagreement, cost, care obligations, authority, suppression, and adaptation.

**Player verbs:** attend, petition, host, maintain, sponsor, learn, refuse, reform, interpret, mourn, reconcile.

## 8B. Funerary practice, remains, mourning, and ancestors

## 37. Terminology, translation, and generated naming

Final canon and UI preserve established English xianxia vocabulary—qi, Qi Condensation, Dao, yin–yang, spiritual roots, meridians, cultivation, tribulation—while translating niche or opaque romanizations. Research preserves source language so it does not leak accidentally into presentation.

Every term records the following fields when applicable. Hanzi and pinyin are required only when documented Chinese-language provenance exists; project-native English and invented-language names must never fabricate Chinese ancestry.

```text
concept/entity ID and domain
original Hanzi
tonal pinyin
literal gloss
attested/context meanings
historical/religious scope
modern genre use
source editions and locations
chosen player-facing term
term category: established xianxia loanword | project-native English | translated technical term | Chinese proper name | invented-language name | context-retained romanization
short UI and long explanatory forms
rejected translations and reasons
ambiguities, false equivalences, and lost distinctions
confidence
bilingual and cultural reviewers
romanization-retention rationale, if uncommon
originality review
version and replacement history
source-language provenance, if any
invented language family, morphology, endonym/exonym, borrowing, and naming history, if any
```

**Generation by category:**

- realm: transformation + embodied consequence + culture-specific metaphor;
- technique: mechanically true action + principle + signature image;
- item/material: function or physical property + provenance;
- office: jurisdiction + duty + rank;
- place: geography + history + local language rules;
- species: ecology/morphology + situated observer name, with multiple cultural names allowed;
- institution: doctrine/function + form + historical identifier;
- person: language-specific surname/given-name grammar, kin/generation rules, nickname/title history;
- earned title: witnessed deed or recognized office, never random superlative stacking.

**Gauntlet:** Would a seasoned English-speaking xianxia reader understand it? Was a familiar genre term awkwardly overtranslated? Is an uncommon retained romanization necessary and explained? Do different cultures name one thing coherently? Did a grand title arise from history rather than a word bag?

No page is complete while a meaningful dependency remains untyped prose.

## 40. Generation dependency order

Initial creation proceeds roughly:

```text
absolute project invariants
→ cosmology and local laws
→ time, space, causality, identity
→ geology, flows, climate, and water
→ ecology and resources
→ species, bodies, and perception
→ cultures, languages, and domestic life
→ settlements, labor, and economies
→ institutions and jurisdictions
→ characters and relationships
→ simulated history and memory
→ current pressures and plans
→ opportunities, rumors, maps, descriptions, and quests
```

After initialization, feedback is bidirectional. War alters ecology; resources alter institutions; cultivation alters bodies; institutions shape land; stories shape reputation; law-authorship changes all dependent systems.

Bidirectional does not mean arbitrary iteration. The Production Graph partitions dependencies into strongly connected components, schedules phases in a deterministic order, assigns stable tie-breaks, bounds iterations, declares convergence/error criteria, and identifies valid persistent cycles such as seasons or market oscillations. Nonconvergent components produce a visible conflict/failure state; they do not loop forever or depend on traversal order.

### Completion rule

A page is specified only when:

1. required fields are complete;
2. dependencies resolve;
3. lifecycle and transitions exist;
4. named player and NPC verbs exist;
5. counters, failures, and traces are explicit;
6. procedural generation can derive it from causes;
7. presentation communicates it at the right confidence;
8. its risk-partitioned gauntlet obligations pass across lifecycle, failure, interaction, persistence, consequence, and presentation; a raw test count is not sufficient;
9. literal descriptions are entailed by behavior;
10. English terminology, cultural context, and originality are reviewed; and
11. an authorized decision admits it to a canon release.

That standard turns the encyclopedia into the semantic constitution of the universe rather than a collection of evocative paragraphs.

# Apex Cosmology and Xianxia Continuity — Pre-Canon Candidate Review Draft v0.1

**Status:** original design research; pre-canon; no realm ladder, cosmology, soul model, protagonist advantage, or game code authorized  
**Purpose:** test whether several microcosm → macrocosm → apex structures can preserve xianxia cultivation, mortal continuity, ontological rigor, player/NPC parity, and original expression at extreme scale  
**Consumes:** Cultivation Foundations Release Index, Terminology Foundation, Identity/Death Models, Qi Condensation Envelope, Time/Catastrophe Architecture, Er Gen/Gemini Mechanics Critique, Next-Phase Source Workplan, and Gameplay Gauntlet Workplan  
**Project boundary:** every title and stage label below is a workshop description, not final lore or player-facing terminology

---

## 0. Review question

The project needs an apex that feels like the culmination of cultivation rather than a change of genre into superhero statistics, strategy-game administration, abstract database access, or arbitrary omnipotence.

This review asks:

> Can mortal embodied practice grow into cosmological agency while body, qi, heart-mind, spirit, personhood, Dao/path, world patterns, relationships, institutions, and consequences remain meaningfully distinct?

The candidates are deliberately incompatible emphases. They are not four regions of one secretly selected cosmology. The user may choose one, request hybrids only after a composition audit, defer all, or reject all.

## 1. Terms used only for analysis

### 1.1 Microcosm, macrocosm, and apex

- **Microcosm** means the cultivator's situated, embodied field of practice: body, qi relations, attention, heart-mind, learned operations, environment, teachers, materials, and consequences. It does not assert that “ancient Chinese people believed the body literally contained a universe.”
- **Macrocosm** means progressively larger relations in which practice can participate: household, ecology, institution, terrain, region, world, routes between worlds, and long causal history. It does not mean a larger health bar.
- **Apex** means the maximum player-facing cultivation condition under study. It is not a final realm name, numbered “step,” translator equivalence, or statement that nothing can exist beyond it.
- **Apex immortal** is an analytical placeholder for a cultivator who has reached that candidate's highest tested condition. **Immortal** here does not guarantee absolute deathlessness, invulnerability, permanent identity, moral superiority, or exemption from causality.

The research must locate the histories and modern genre uses of microcosm–macrocosm comparison and immortality/transcendence before any cultural claim is made. These English analytical terms create no historical lineage by themselves.

### 1.2 No automatic ladder

Each candidate is expressed as functional bands:

```text
mortal situated life
→ embodied establishment
→ relational enlargement
→ world-participating practice
→ apex condition
```

Those bands are comparison scaffolds. They do not select realm names, stage count, breakthrough order, lifespan, tribulations, required institutions, or final Chinese terminology.

## 2. Ontology separation contract

No candidate advances until every row below has its own source status, project definition, operations, evidence, failure modes, and relation to the others.

| Domain | Minimum distinction | Forbidden collapse |
|---|---|---|
| **body / embodiment** | material and other ratified forms through which an actor exists and acts; capacities, needs, injuries, senses, age, environment and accessibility remain specific | body is not merely an avatar, qi container, disposable shell, or automatic proof of identity |
| **qi** | a project-selected medium or family of relations with explicit sources, transport, transformation, information limits, conservation and failure | qi is not generic mana, joules, breath, life, morality, fate, spirit, or permission |
| **heart-mind** | *xin*-related attention, affect, desire, judgment and intention only as separately researched and selected | heart-mind is not a detachable soul, spirit token, sanity bar, moral alignment, or mind-control defense stat |
| **spirit** | *shen* or any project spirit concept only in the exact selected sense: presence, numinous efficacy, conscious spirit, deity/spirit being, or another bounded referent | spirit does not automatically establish consciousness, personhood, numerical identity, memory or survival after death |
| **consciousness and agency** | capacities for experience, choice and responsibility under a selected model | consciousness is not ownership, social recognition, legal standing or proof of one metaphysical substance |
| **personhood and identity continuity** | numerical identity, bodily/psychological/causal/cultivation continuity, social recognition, legal standing, oath liability and viewpoint succession are separate judgments | a copy, restored pattern, body, memory, name, spirit or save file does not become “the same person” by label |
| **Dao / a dao / path** | Dao and particular paths retain their researched semantic and project-specific roles as way, practice, orientation, possibility, proof or another selected sense | Dao is not statute, engine privilege, spell school, mana element, moral goodness, fate or world-editor authority |
| **world patterns** | observed or established causal regularities and compatible media at local or cosmic scales | world patterns are not automatically Dao, Heaven, karma, laws in a statutory sense, or immutable code |
| **institutions and offices** | actual roles, lineages, sects, courts, temples, vows, territories and custody relations with witnesses, succession and enforcement | social or divine office does not prove ontic supremacy, Dao truth, identity or universal jurisdiction |
| **optional modules** | Heaven, karma, fate, destiny, mandate, tribulation, rebirth and cosmic office remain separately researched candidates | none may become reputation, morality meter, RNG, difficulty scaling, automatic punishment or protagonist rescue |

### 2.1 Cross-domain rule

A breakthrough may relate several domains, but no relation establishes identity. For example, a bodily transformation may alter qi routing and attention without producing a new spirit; a spirit continuity may persist without proving the same person; Dao insight may change practice without editing world truth; an institution may recognize an attainment without creating it.

## 3. What makes an apex remain xianxia

Every candidate must retain all of the following:

1. **Cultivation remains practiced transformation.** Teaching, self-correction, resources, body, environment, time, insight, failure and consequence matter at every scale.
2. **Breakthroughs change relations and verbs.** They do not merely multiply damage, lifespan or menu permissions.
3. **Dao/path remains lived and contestable.** A path is demonstrated through action, consequence and continued practice, not selected as a character class or declared true by UI.
4. **The mortal world remains causally real.** Food, care, disability, labor, ritual, law, kinship, memory, places and institutions do not become meaningless scenery.
5. **Lineage and transmission remain possible without becoming mandatory uniformity.** Teachers, students, rivals, texts, embodied demonstration, institutions and independent discovery can preserve or transform a practice.
6. **Immortality remains a question, not a free premise.** Longevity, transformed embodiment, persistence, resurrection, reincarnation, successorhood and remembrance stay distinct.
7. **Combat and noncombat agency grow together.** Preservation, travel, repair, teaching, mediation, concealment, construction, ecology, testimony and refusal remain as real as destruction.
8. **Apex action has sources, preparation, interfaces, counters, uncertainty and aftermath.** No one gains root access to reality.
9. **NPC parity survives.** An isomorphic non-player cultivator receives the same ontology, operations, costs, risks and possible apex.
10. **Cultural fluency is carried by researched concepts and coherent practice, not decorative Chinese syllables or copied ladders.**

## 4. Common apex action contract

Before any apex feat is admitted, it must declare:

```text
actor and embodiment state
available evidence and uncertainty
selected operation and target relation
source and contribution ledger
claim scope and support
geometry, routes, anchors and compatible media
preparation, reaction and commitment milestones
duration, concurrency and attention
counteractors and interruption surfaces
body, qi, heart-mind and spirit consequences where applicable
personhood/identity judgments touched, if any
institutional, legal, ritual and ecological consequences
optional Heaven/karma/fate/tribulation modules, if separately ratified
failure transitions and irreversibility
persistent aftermath and future-refinement obligations
```

Scale can enlarge these fields. It cannot remove them.

## 5. Candidate A — Reciprocal Microcosm

**Workshop thesis:** cultivation deepens the ability to maintain coherent exchange among embodiment, environment and multiple incompatible conditions. The apex fantasy is not “a universe stored in the body”; it is becoming capable of hosting and mediating a bounded living concord without erasing its participants.

### 5.1 Functional ascent

| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
|---|---|---|
| mortal situated life | learns bodily limits, care, craft, ecology and reciprocal dependence | observe, regulate, nourish, shelter, cooperate, recover |
| embodied establishment | establishes a stable cultivated circulation and discriminates internal/external changes without treating the body as a closed machine | route, buffer, exchange, diagnose, vent, retain |
| relational enlargement | extends supported exchange into tools, formations, sites, companions and local ecologies | couple, translate medium, quarantine, balance throughput, share burden |
| world participation | mediates incompatible climates, qi ecologies, spatial conditions or world patterns across a bounded territory | establish interface, sustain refuge, reconcile boundary, restore cycle |
| apex condition | can found and maintain a finite concordant habitat in which several otherwise incompatible causal ecologies coexist while retaining separate histories and agency | host, translate, partition, reconnect, seed succession, relinquish stewardship |

### 5.2 Domain behavior

- **Body:** remains the first learned ecology and a continuing source of vulnerability and knowledge; later embodiments do not become disposable.
- **Qi:** transports and transforms contribution across interfaces; it is not the habitat's only substance or source.
- **Heart-mind:** manages attention, intention, tolerance of contradiction and prioritization; it does not command inhabitants.
- **Spirit:** may support presence or communication only if a selected spirit model permits it.
- **Personhood:** every inhabitant remains a person under the selected ontology; being hosted creates no ownership or identity absorption.
- **Dao/path:** demonstrated through sustaining reciprocal conditions and accepting constraints; not universal ecological morality.

### 5.3 Apex immortal can

- mediate a finite boundary between otherwise incompatible environments;
- sustain a refuge or nursery through declared sources, anchors and maintenance;
- translate some forces, qi expressions or local patterns across that boundary;
- preserve histories, remains and causal traces through a world transition when physically and ontically supported;
- teach others to operate parts of the concord independently; and
- withdraw, leaving a viable successor system if enough structure and stewardship exist.

### 5.4 Apex immortal cannot

- create unlimited matter, qi, life or persons from nothing;
- make hosted inhabitants extensions, inventory or dream-figures of the host;
- become omniscient or omnipotent “inside” the habitat;
- declare incompatible patterns compatible without transformation, loss or consequence;
- guarantee ecological harmony, moral agreement or permanent stability;
- restore numerical identity merely by preserving a pattern; or
- hide external costs inside a private-world abstraction.

### 5.5 Gameplay promise and risk

**Promise:** habitat building, evacuation, mediation, ecological restoration, defensive combat and world-scale stewardship become one coherent family of increasingly expressive operations.

**Risk:** it can collapse into a familiar inner-world inventory trope, a benevolent empire simulator, or omnipotence inside a pocket dimension. Any implementation that makes inhabitants nonpersons or turns hosting into owner permissions fails immediately.

## 6. Candidate B — Paths Across Horizons

**Workshop thesis:** cultivation begins with learning routes through the body and matures into establishing traversable, maintained relations across otherwise separated places and conditions. The apex fantasy is opening a genuinely new cosmic path, not teleporting by menu or owning every destination.

### 6.1 Functional ascent

| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
|---|---|---|
| mortal situated life | learns paths of labor, trade, kinship, pilgrimage, danger and return | navigate, remember route, guide, mark, negotiate passage |
| embodied establishment | establishes reliable qi and attention routes with known bottlenecks and recovery | circulate, redirect, bypass, seal route, reopen route |
| relational enlargement | reads and alters supported routes among people, techniques, terrain, institutions and local spaces | trace, bridge, divert, gate, escort, interdict with actual standing/support |
| world participation | creates or repairs routes between distant regions or world conditions with anchors, schedules and transit ecology | establish corridor, translate transit state, maintain threshold, rescue stranded travelers |
| apex condition | can originate a finite durable path between previously disconnected cosmic ecologies and make it independently traversable under explicit conditions | open horizon, stabilize passage, publish conditions, distribute anchors, close responsibly |

### 6.2 Domain behavior

- **Body:** teaches route, obstruction, rhythm, fatigue and adaptation; bodily routes are not maps of all reality.
- **Qi:** supports traversal and route transformation but cannot carry arbitrary identity, ownership or truth.
- **Heart-mind:** holds orientation, divided attention and intention under extreme distance; it is not a universal targeting system.
- **Spirit:** may extend presence or signaling only through supported routes and the selected spirit ontology.
- **Personhood:** transit, projection and remote embodiment each receive independent identity judgments.
- **Dao/path:** “path” is operative as practiced possibility and passage, while Dao remains broader or different if canon selects it so; the English overlap does not prove identity.

### 6.3 Apex immortal can

- create a traversable relation where none previously existed, given sufficient knowledge, support and compatible endpoints;
- make route conditions inspectable rather than secretly privileging the protagonist;
- move or coordinate large cohorts without issuing one command per traveler;
- redirect attacks, evacuations, trade, messages or ecological flows through maintained pathways;
- contest another path by anchors, timing, medium, destination relations and support; and
- leave routes that outlast the founder if maintenance and succession are real.

### 6.4 Apex immortal cannot

- arrive where no route, relation, anchor or compatible transition exists;
- treat distance as deleted rather than transformed;
- know what lies at an unobserved destination;
- own people, places or goods merely because their route carried them;
- make passage safe, instantaneous, costless or one-way by UI declaration;
- merge endpoint laws, Daos, histories or identities; or
- escape all confinement, causality or consequence by calling everything a path.

### 6.5 Gameplay promise and risk

**Promise:** bodily cultivation, movement combat, exploration, trade, rescue, migration, formation networks and cosmic travel share one deep grammar.

**Risk:** it can become unrestricted fast travel, portal spam, administrative logistics, or a renamed spatial superpower. If route work no longer requires embodied understanding, endpoint relations, maintenance or uncertainty, the candidate fails.

## 7. Candidate C — Transformative Succession

**Workshop thesis:** cultivation deepens the ability to carry viable relation and meaning through irreversible transformation. The apex fantasy is shepherding a dying world into a genuinely new successor ecology without pretending that loss, death or personal identity has been undone.

### 7.1 Functional ascent

| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
|---|---|---|
| mortal situated life | lives through seasons, aging, injury, mourning, inheritance, repair and institutional memory | preserve, compost, repair, teach, mourn, bequeath |
| embodied establishment | learns controlled refinement, shedding, healing and changed equilibrium without reset | transform residue, consolidate scar, redirect decay, recover differently |
| relational enlargement | guides households, lineages, ecologies, techniques and settlements through disruptive change | triage, conserve seed, archive trace, transfer stewardship, adapt practice |
| world participation | manages finite world-scale cycles of depletion, catastrophe, dormancy, migration and renewal | preserve viable relations, release failed structures, establish successor conditions |
| apex condition | can guide a terminal world process into one or more successor ecologies while conserving declared matter, contribution, evidence and selected continuities | shepherd ending, partition inheritance, seed successor, witness loss, relinquish predecessor |

### 7.2 Domain behavior

- **Body:** aging, scars, death and transformation remain informative; refinement is not automatic rejuvenation.
- **Qi:** can be conserved, transformed, dispersed or rendered unusable according to the selected model; it does not guarantee life.
- **Heart-mind:** bears grief, judgment and changing commitment; emotional pain is not a resource or required moral purity.
- **Spirit:** persistence, dissolution or transformation remains conditional on the selected spirit model.
- **Personhood:** successor ecology, descendant, copy, reincarnation, restored memory and viewpoint continuation never prove numerical identity.
- **Dao/path:** a path is shown by how it meets change and what it can carry forward, not by immunity to endings.

### 7.3 Apex immortal can

- perceive and influence some long processes of decay, exhaustion, migration and renewal;
- preserve bounded archives, seeds, techniques, ecologies, remains and testimony through catastrophe;
- allocate contribution among incompatible successor possibilities;
- refuse false restoration and expose what cannot be conserved;
- help institutions and peoples choose different continuities; and
- continue acting through a selected survival/identity model if its conditions genuinely hold.

### 7.4 Apex immortal cannot

- rewind the world, restore every victim or make irreversible events unhappen;
- declare a successor to be the same world or person;
- preserve all information, species, cultures, obligations and possibilities simultaneously;
- convert grief, sacrifice or death into guaranteed power;
- make decay morally deserved or renewal morally superior;
- exempt the protagonist from the same succession rules; or
- call a branching copy “reincarnation” without a selected ontology.

### 7.5 Gameplay promise and risk

**Promise:** recovery, inheritance, ecology, catastrophe, death, archives and cosmic-scale cultivation remain part of one life rather than reset mechanics.

**Risk:** it can aestheticize mass death, make catastrophe a progression resource, enforce sacrificial morality, or disguise save/reload as cosmology. Any design that requires disposable civilizations, compulsory grief, or identity hand-waving fails.

## 8. Candidate D — A Path That Outlives Its Founder

**Workshop thesis:** cultivation culminates not in owning reality but in establishing a new, independently viable way of transformation that other persons can test, alter, refuse and carry beyond the founder. The apex fantasy is creating possibility rather than universal command.

### 8.1 Functional ascent

| Band | Candidate transformation | New operation families |
|---|---|---|
| mortal situated life | learns crafts, ethics, bodily practice, testimony, disagreement and teaching | imitate, question, demonstrate, revise, transmit |
| embodied establishment | proves a bounded method through repeatable personal transformation and failure | practice, falsify, diagnose, correct, adapt |
| relational enlargement | distinguishes transferable structure from founder-specific body, culture, resources and charisma | teach variants, compare evidence, establish lineage, permit dissent |
| world participation | creates institutions, ecologies, materials and interfaces through which materially different beings can explore the path | publish conditions, distribute practice, protect plurality, expose failure |
| apex condition | establishes an original path whose core transformation remains possible after the founder's withdrawal or death, without making every practitioner identical | found possibility, release monopoly, support divergence, receive contradiction |

### 8.2 Domain behavior

- **Body:** every practitioner must embody the path differently; no universal human male body becomes the template for all species or persons.
- **Qi:** a path may use qi but cannot define all qi or deny other transformations by declaration.
- **Heart-mind:** intention, affect and judgment influence practice without becoming mandatory personality or moral alignment.
- **Spirit:** a path may cultivate a spirit relation only if separately established and cannot use it as automatic personhood proof.
- **Personhood:** students, created consciousnesses and successors remain independent persons; lineage does not absorb identity.
- **Dao/path:** this candidate makes a particular practiced path central while refusing the claim that one founded path exhausts Dao or all possible ways.

### 8.3 Apex immortal can

- establish a transformation that others can reproduce under disclosed conditions;
- create methods, sites, institutions, artifacts or ecologies that support different embodiments of the path;
- recognize valid divergence rather than only exact imitation;
- contest rival claims through consequences, evidence and action;
- relinquish control without the path vanishing immediately; and
- remain influential through teaching and causal history without that influence proving personal survival.

### 8.4 Apex immortal cannot

- make belief true, compel disciples, own a lineage or monopolize Dao;
- grant attainment through title, certification, worship or proximity alone;
- ensure every body, culture, species or world can practice the same method;
- define dissent as qi deviation, inner demon, sin or ontic corruption by default;
- make institutional success proof of cosmological truth;
- copy a famous fictional path, founder arc, ladder or signature method; or
- turn all endgame play into sect administration.

### 8.5 Gameplay promise and risk

**Promise:** teaching, experimentation, institutions, companions, procedural variation, rivalry and legacy become genuine apex play while preserving individual agency.

**Risk:** it can become a religion simulator, moral conquest, prestige tree, or passive empire management. If followers are resources, disagreement is always failure, or institutional scale replaces personal cultivation, the candidate fails.

## 10. Universal apex permissions and prohibitions

These are candidate-review constraints, not lore.

### 10.1 An admitted apex may be able to

- act across world-scale distances or durations through explicit routes, sources, anchors, embodiments or institutions;
- alter bounded world conditions through supported operations and persistent contribution;
- create habitats, routes, successor conditions or independently viable practices, depending on the selected candidate;
- survive bodily destruction only if a separately selected identity/continuity model supports that exact transition;
- perceive and coordinate far more than a lower cultivator while retaining uncertainty, attention limits, concealment and counter-detection;
- preserve, transform, redirect or contain catastrophic processes without guaranteeing success; and
- leave causal structures that outlast their immediate presence.

### 10.2 No admitted apex may

- edit engine variables, database state, narrative flags or “reality code” as its ontology;
- become omnipotent, omniscient, omnipresent or infinitely concurrent;
- create contribution, matter, qi, persons, identity, ownership, legitimacy or moral truth by declaration;
- treat a world, species, population, disciple, copy, spirit or created consciousness as inventory;
- make Dao equivalent to statutory law, universal permission or spell-school ownership;
- make qi equivalent to a universal energy bar or sufficient proof of every capability;
- make *xin*, *shen*, consciousness, soul, personhood, memory and identity interchangeable;
- erase causal history, committed costs, witnesses, scars or externalized harm;
- guarantee resurrection, reincarnation, restoration, refuge or successorhood because the actor is the protagonist;
- automatically defeat lower beings, ignore uncovered interfaces, or trigger universal worship/kowtow;
- convert karma, fate, Heaven, mandate, tribulation or office into a moralized difficulty controller; or
- bypass the same semantic resolver, catastrophe contract and NPC-parity rules that govern everyone else.

## 11. Cultural and source research gates

No candidate enters user selection until the Next-Phase Source Workplan passes for every claim it touches.

### 11.1 Required claim-card families

1. qi, Dao, *xin*, *shen*, *jing*, *xing* and *ming*;
2. immortality, transcendence, longevity, transformed embodiment, ascent and celestial/divine office as distinct source concepts;
3. microcosm–macrocosm language in the exact historical, religious, medical, literary and scholarly contexts used;
4. body/landscape relation, inner alchemy, lineage, teaching, vows, domains, world patterns, creation/formation and cosmic cycles;
5. Buddhist, Daoist, Confucian, local-religious, medical, literary and modern-genre identity/death materials kept in separate tracks;
6. modern licensed xianxia depictions of inner worlds, domains, world creation, route transcendence, reincarnation, apex ranks and founded Daos; and
7. translator notes and English terminology without treating translation aids as authorial identity.

### 11.2 Cultural scope gates

- No source may authorize “Chinese belief,” “Daoist cosmology,” “Buddhist reincarnation,” “the Chinese soul,” or “traditional cultivation” as a singular universal.
- Historical/religious materials, modern lived practice, modern genre convention and original design remain visibly separate.
- The body is not assumed male, able-bodied, human, reproductively normative or anatomically universal.
- *Shen* is never translated into an identity-bearing soul without passage-level and ontology-level support.
- Microcosm–macrocosm correspondence is not converted into literal anatomy or medical advice.
- Immortality is not silently mapped to Western absolute deathlessness or moral divinity.
- Ritual, hierarchy, lineage, office and deference remain culturally situated relations, not automatic mechanics.
- Minority, frontier, diaspora and non-Han materials receive their own qualified scope and are not decorative variation on a Han default.

### 11.3 Genre corpus gates

- Any claim of a sampled genre convention meets the multilingual/licensed thresholds in the Source Workplan.
- At least three materially different apex implementations and two counterexamples are coded before a project abstraction advances.
- One author, translator, platform, fan chart or shared-universe comparison can never establish the project's apex.
- Speaker-relative claims, poetic descriptions, feats, titles, ranks and translator mappings remain distinct.
- Absence of a limit in one chapter is not evidence of omnipotence.

### 11.4 Reviewer gate

Source-language/translation, history of Chinese religions/philosophies, modern xianxia, identity/death, disability/body, cultural sensitivity, originality, gameplay and continuity reviewers must independently close their own domains. The synthesis author cannot close a cultural or originality S1 against their own draft.

## 12. Originality gate

Every candidate must pass the Source Workplan's full originality red-team. At minimum:

- workshop titles are replaced only after name-level corpus and linguistic review;
- no named ladder, numbered “steps,” bridge sequence, immortal rank chain, signature artifact, famous technique, shared-world cosmology or protagonist-specific route is imported;
- removing the strongest individual inspiration leaves the candidate's core operation, mortal root, progression, failure and apex intact;
- changing proper nouns cannot be used to disguise copied structure;
- the combination of operation, cost, progression, imagery, institution and climax does not identify one source to blinded reviewers;
- no candidate is a collage of recognizable Er Gen or other popular-work parts;
- visual identity, spatial motifs, breakthrough staging and catastrophe imagery receive the same review as prose/mechanics; and
- procedural generation uses only cleared primitives and cannot reconstruct source fingerprints through recombination.

These candidates are functional abstractions. Their eventual cultures, histories, terms, iconography and metaphors must be authored from the selected original universe rather than pasted onto this review.

## 13. Candidate-specific paper fixtures

Each candidate must pass all fixtures at mortal, early Qi Condensation, later local, world-participating and apex bands.

### 13.1 Common fixtures

- **Mortal root:** show three ordinary-life decisions whose learned relations later matter at apex; no retroactive destiny marker.
- **First cultivation:** produce a genuinely new verb without solving mortal food, care, disability, debt, law or relationship play.
- **Peer conflict:** win, lose, disengage, negotiate and protect through different supported relations.
- **Cross-band conflict:** a lower actor changes outcome through preparation, an uncovered interface, institution, environment, evidence or dependent relation.
- **Teaching:** transmit something useful without copying attainment or personality.
- **Catastrophe:** preserve or change consequences without erasing the event.
- **Identity:** test body loss, copy, restored pattern, possession, reincarnation claim and viewpoint successor under every surviving identity model.
- **Institution:** test recognition, dissent, succession, coercion and corruption without making status equal truth.
- **No-protagonist:** an isomorphic NPC reaches and uses the same apex.
- **Absence:** remove the apex actor; ordinary people, lower cultivators, ecologies and institutions still produce coherent history.

### 13.2 Candidate discriminators

- **Reciprocal Microcosm:** inhabitants successfully oppose the host; a habitat survives founder withdrawal; an incompatible medium cannot be wished away.
- **Paths Across Horizons:** an unknown endpoint defeats perfect routing; a route carries no ownership; broken support strands the apex actor.
- **Transformative Succession:** some valued continuity must be lost; a successor is not mislabeled identical; catastrophe cannot be profitably farmed.
- **Path Outliving Founder:** students produce legitimate divergence; an institution succeeds while one doctrine claim fails; founder death does not answer identity through legacy.

## 14. Harsh failure criteria

Any one of the following is S1 unless it causes foundational identity/causal corruption, in which case it is S0:

1. The apex is describable as root access, world editing, admin privilege, reality code, omnipotence or “whatever the plot needs.”
2. Progression reduces to one scalar power, lifespan, energy, comprehension or jurisdiction score.
3. Body, qi, heart-mind, spirit, consciousness, personhood, identity and Dao/path become one substance or interchangeable bars.
4. A copied body, memory, spirit, pattern, world or lineage is declared numerically identical without the selected identity model.
5. Mortal care, work, disability, ritual, law, ecology, relationships or institutions become irrelevant at apex.
6. Apex immortality requires male, human, able-bodied or reproductively normative embodiment.
7. A candidate makes catastrophe, sacrifice, followers, worship, grief or lower lives into automatic cultivation fuel.
8. A candidate requires universal kowtow, obedience, insanity, surrender, moral deference or protagonist rescue in response to power.
9. Dao becomes statute or owned spell school; qi becomes mana; karma becomes reputation; Heaven becomes difficulty scaling; tribulation becomes loot delivery.
10. Apex perception becomes an ownership, intent, morality, identity, inventory, safe-target or future-truth oracle.
11. The apex can create persons and then treat them as extensions, simulations or property.
12. The design has no costs, counters, preparation, failure, uncertainty, persistent aftermath or NPC parity.
13. A lower actor cannot matter through any indirect, prepared, relational, environmental, institutional or informational interface.
14. Two or more candidates converge to the same play with different nouns.
15. The candidate's xianxia identity depends on unexplained romanization, decorative architecture, copied realm labels or generic VFX rather than cultivation practice.
16. A cultural claim lacks a scoped card or merges distinct traditions into an “ancient Eastern” synthesis.
17. A blinded originality review repeatedly identifies one protected source from the candidate's combined structure or expression.
18. The candidate requires an unselected soul, Heaven, karma, fate, tribulation, reincarnation or world-law module to function.
19. Player status supplies survival, opportunity, truth, resources or permissions unavailable to an isomorphic NPC.
20. Presentation scale requires the renderer to own canon or whole-universe high-detail simulation.

After the gates pass, the user should receive concrete scenario comparisons addressing:

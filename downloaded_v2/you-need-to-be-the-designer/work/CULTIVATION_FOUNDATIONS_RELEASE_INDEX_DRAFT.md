## 1. Outcome

This release makes the project's first playable span—from ordinary mortal life through the threshold of Qi Condensation—specific enough to test without prematurely choosing the universe's realm ladder, final cosmology, exact protagonist advantage, soul truth, or first region.

It also establishes how a single-player living universe can pause, advance time, preserve causality, resolve distant events, and admit catastrophic possibilities without either simulating every atom or granting the protagonist hidden immunity.

When the candidate gate closes, the release becomes a **reviewed design substrate**, not canon. User-ratified choices will later become immutable canon revisions through the Research Graph process.

## 3. Decisions safe to carry forward

These are release constraints or strong recommendations. They are not permission to invent missing canon around them.

1. Use **Qi Condensation**, not “Vital Breath Condensation.” Retain seasoned English-language xianxia terms such as qi and Dao. When spiritual roots, meridians or tribulations are selected for a setting/source, use the familiar English terms rather than gratuitous romanization. This vocabulary policy does not itself select their ontology. Translate or gloss niche romanization; preserve Hanzi and tonal pinyin in research records where relevant.
2. The player begins genuinely mortal. Mortal food, shelter, work, disability, care, law, learning, ritual, leisure, relationships and failure remain worthwhile gameplay rather than a disposable tutorial.
3. Cultivation is a multidimensional transformation. Candidate domains include body, qi, heart-mind, spirit, Dao orientation, vital continuity, practice and provenance, environment, institution, and failure/debt. Personhood and identity-continuity judgments remain separate ontology questions unless later canon explicitly relates them; no English label or slash establishes identity, and none of these domains may collapse into one level or combat-power number.
4. Ontic capability, certification and audience recognition are different. A person may change before anyone can certify, understand or socially recognize the change.
5. Qi Condensation adds new verbs but remains early cultivation. Its provisional exceptional personal peak fixture is below 220 kJ for the declared short coactivation event; ordinary output is lower, coupled limits apply, and no one scalar energy figure proves every capability.
6. A Qi Condensation protagonist cannot personally split a huge mountain. Any early mountain-scale consequence must identify external contribution, preparation, structure, environment, instability and honest attribution.
7. A realm gap never substitutes for resolving the attacked facet, perception, timing, source, claim scope/support, geometry, defense, cost and aftermath.
8. Every mechanically active effect must compile to a semantic contract. Names, imagery, symbolism, ritual language, beliefs, lies and unreliable descriptions need not be literally ontic. Presentation alone cannot create a new action verb, and differently wired actions cannot be declared equivalent by superficial labels.
9. The protagonist may be exceptional only through declared, testable operations with explicit locus, transfer/copy/loss/inheritance/death behavior under the same physics as everyone else. Genuinely nontransferable uniqueness may follow the selected ontology; it may never be an undeclared `isPlayer` privilege. Residual Error Sense, Held Pattern and Reciprocal Cadence survive only as research directions.
10. Hard pause freezes canon. Menus and the reading portion of non-hostile dialogue may pause; committed speech, transfers, rituals and other world acts consume semantic time and can expose interruption points.
11. Time advance never becomes an oracle. It may interrupt only through information the protagonist or an authorized monitor could receive under established sensing/communication rules.
12. Catastrophes remain possible and consequential. Inhabited mortal worlds persist through layered ecological, territorial, institutional, formation, deterrence and carrying-capacity causes—not because dangerous beings are forbidden from existing.
13. Opening admission and later catastrophe admission are distinct. In the recommended opening policy, every candidate viewpoint facing committed or uncommitted K0–K7 threats needs one finite witness that survives the opening interval, enters a disclosed identity-model continuation, or permits a consequence-changing action followed by an explicitly disclosed playable successor; only a disclosed `Doomed Prologue` waives this. Later, only `Epic Protagonist` mode filters not-yet-committed K4+ candidates and requires survival or viewpoint continuation; it does not admit the opening's successor-only alternative. `Lawful Chronicle` guarantees only the opening gate, while `Unbounded Cosmos` waives even that special filter after basic character-creation safety. Every filter constrains content admission, never physics after commitment.
14. World truth is event/state simulation. Rendering is an independent view. Distant history may use cohorts, summaries and analytical propagation only when future refinement preserves every protected observation and consequence.
15. Automatic loot attraction is desirable after it resolves detection, evidence-based classification, collection policy, path/control, transport and storage under established world rules. Terrain destruction uses hybrid sparse volumes and persistent operations, not a planet-wide high-resolution voxel array.

## 4. The gauntlet loop

Every proposed realm, technique, item, NPC policy, catastrophe or procedural generator enters the same loop:

```text
source/proposal
→ typed claim and originality boundary
→ semantic action/state contract
→ ordinary positive fixture
→ edge and failure fixtures
→ adversarial/exploit fixtures
→ NPC-isomorphism test
→ persistence and future-refinement test
→ reference-hardware budget
→ independent cultural + gameplay + technical review
→ repair or reject
→ user decision for world-defining canon
```

The loop is intentionally not capped to a static checklist. New encyclopedia domains and new mechanics automatically create new test obligations through the Open-Ended Universe Gauntlet.

## 5. What remains open

- final cosmology and ontology of Heaven, world laws, Dao, immortality and the larger multiverse;
- original realm ladder above the mortal threshold and the exact internal structure of Qi Condensation;
- first region, cultures, languages, geography, species, polities, sects, lineages and history;
- soul/death/reincarnation candidate;
- protagonist advantage candidate, locus and contract;
- divine sense, concealment and evidence specifics;
- technique grammar, intent controls and cross-pattern/path/domain contests;
- artifacts, alchemy, formations, talismans, crafting, medicine and economies;
- terrain, construction, ecology, travel, vehicles, pocket spaces and cosmic topology;
- AI belief, relationships, institutions, war, surrender, succession and historical memory;
- measured renderer/simulation budgets on the GTX 1070 / i5-3570K / 8 GB reference machine; and
- the full PRD and implementation architecture.

None of those gaps is permission for a generator to improvise canon. Unknown remains typed unknown until researched and decided.

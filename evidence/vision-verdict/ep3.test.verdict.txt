**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE REPORT: STAGE A**

**Actual Content**
*   **Setting:** An outdoor environment consisting of a flat green plane and a grey/tan paved area.
*   **Objects:** Several primitive geometric structures. These include large grey rectangular prisms, flat horizontal planes supported by thin vertical cylinders (poles), a few low-poly conical green shapes (trees), and a massive light-blue pyramid in the background.
*   **Scale:** Inconsistent. The grey block on the right is monolithic compared to the thin poles and small conical trees.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. Objects are spaced far apart with vast amounts of empty, featureless terrain.

**Player Perception**
A player would perceive this as a "greybox" or a developer's test map. It looks like a layout phase where spatial volumes are being tested before any actual assets are created.

**Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Primitive/Low-poly.
*   **Quality:** Minimal. There is no evidence of texturing, material mapping, or complex geometry. Lighting is flat with no visible shadows, ambient occlusion, or global illumination.

**Level of Completion**
**BLOCKOUT**

**Defects and Distractions**
*   **Lighting:** Total absence of depth; the scene lacks contrast and directional light, making it difficult to gauge distance.
*   **Ground Plane:** The green foreground is a featureless, blurred gradient that provides no tactile or visual information.
*   **Geometry:** Edges are perfectly sharp and mathematical; there is no weathering, beveling, or organic variation.
*   **Empty Zones:** The entire foreground and the vast majority of the sky/background are dead space.

**Inspection Limit**
The image stops rewarding closer inspection immediately. There are no fine details, textures, or hidden elements to discover. Once the basic geometric shapes are identified, there is zero further visual information to extract.

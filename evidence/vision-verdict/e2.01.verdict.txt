## STAGE A (blind)
**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE REPORT: STAGE A**

**1. Visual Content Summary**
*   **Setting:** A featureless, flat plane suspended in a monochromatic blue-grey void.
*   **Objects:** A central cluster of approximately 10–12 tiny, untextured rectangular prisms. Scattered across the main plane are several small, low-poly conical shapes. Large, sharp-edged, flat white polygons frame the perimeter, suggesting unfinished terrain or floating shards.
*   **Scale:** Appears to be a high-altitude or "god-view" perspective. If the central boxes are buildings, the scale is massive and the density is near zero.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. Content is concentrated in a microscopic center point, leaving ~95% of the frame as empty, flat color.

**2. Player Impression**
A player would conclude they are looking at a **technical test** or a **broken camera state**. The visual language suggests a "Greybox" environment where the player has zoomed out far beyond the intended play area, or the world has failed to load textures and environmental assets. The cones are recognizable only as "placeholder trees," and the boxes as "placeholder buildings."

**3. Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Primitive Low-Poly / Untextured.
*   **Production Quality:** Non-existent. There are no materials, shaders (beyond a basic distance fog/gradient), or post-processing effects visible. The lighting is a single point source causing a flat white bloom in the center.

**4. Apparent Level of Completion**
**PRIMITIVE_PLACEHOLDER**
This is not a "Stylized Prototype" because it lacks a cohesive aesthetic. It is a collection of default engine primitives (cubes, cones, planes) placed in a default scene.

**5. Defects, Distractions, and Empty Zones**
*   **Empty Zones:** The entire periphery is a dead zone. The blue-grey background provides no sense of atmosphere or horizon.
*   **Geometry Defects:** The large white shapes at the edges have razor-sharp, aliased edges and no thickness, appearing as 2D planes clipping into the camera view.
*   **Visual Noise:** The central bloom/light source is washed out, obscuring the only "content" in the image.
*   **Lack of Context:** There is no focal point, no UI, and no indication of player presence or objective.

**6. Inspection Reward Limit**
**Immediate.**
The image stops rewarding inspection the moment the viewer identifies the central shapes as untextured blocks. There is no hidden detail, no micro-texture, and no environmental storytelling. Closer inspection only reveals the jagged edges of the low-poly geometry.

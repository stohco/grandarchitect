**REPORT: PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE EXAM (STAGE A)**

**1. Actual Content (Setting, Objects, Scale, Density)**
*   **Setting:** A corner of a room consisting of two flat white walls and a brown, textured floor.
*   **Objects:** A collection of low-poly geometric primitives scattered on the floor. This includes two beige rectangular blocks, three octagonal prisms (cylinders), two brown angular blocks, and one thin, vertical green line/rod.
*   **Scale:** Indeterminate. Without a reference entity, this could be a small diorama or a large warehouse. The camera height suggests a human-eye perspective, making the objects appear knee-to-waist high.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. Approximately 85% of the frame is empty wall or floor space.

**2. Player Perception**
A player would perceive this as a "test map" or a "void." The objects lack sufficient detail to be identified as specific items, though the octagonal prisms might be interpreted as stools or logs, and the blocks as crates. The green rod appears to be a technical marker or a glitch rather than an intentional prop.

**3. Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Primitive 3D. There is no discernible art direction beyond basic geometric representation.
*   **Quality:** Extremely low. Lighting is flat and ambient; there are no contact shadows (Ambient Occlusion) or cast shadows, causing objects to appear as if they are floating. Textures are low-resolution and lack material properties (no roughness, metallic, or normal maps).

**4. Apparent Level of Completion**
*   **PRIMITIVE_PLACEHOLDER**
This is the earliest stage of asset placement. It lacks the architectural intent required for a "Blockout."

**5. Defects, Distractions, and Empty Zones**
*   **Moiré/Aliasing:** The floor texture exhibits significant shimmering and pattern interference (aliasing) toward the back of the room.
*   **The "Void" Ceiling:** A black sliver is visible at the top of the frame, indicating the walls do not meet a ceiling or the skybox is leaking.
*   **Lack of Grounding:** The total absence of shadows makes the spatial relationship between objects and the floor impossible to gauge accurately.
*   **Empty Zones:** The walls are featureless white planes. The floor texture is a repetitive, noisy brown tile with no variation or "wear."
*   **The Green Line:** A single-pixel-width green vertical line stands in the center-right, serving as a visual distraction with no clear purpose.

**6. Inspection Reward Limit**
The image stops rewarding inspection instantly. There are no hidden details, micro-textures, or environmental storytelling elements. Closer inspection only reveals the mathematical sharpness of the polygon edges and the low-resolution noise of the floor texture.

**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE REPORT: STAGE A**

**Actual Content**
*   **Setting:** A vast, featureless green plane under a flat, pale blue sky.
*   **Objects:** A central cluster of primitive geometric shapes. This includes grey and tan rectangular prisms (buildings), thin brown planes supported by thin cylinders (canopies/walkways), and green cones (trees). 
*   **Scale:** Extreme disparity. Small-scale "buildings" are dwarfed by two monolithic background structures: a massive light-blue pyramid and a massive dark-grey rectangular block.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. A single island of geometry in an empty void.

**Player Perception**
A player would perceive this as a developer's test level, a "greybox" environment, or a very early technical prototype. There is no narrative or atmospheric communication; it looks like a spatial layout test.

**Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Primitive Low-Poly / Untextured.
*   **Quality:** Minimal. There are no textures, no materials, no complex shaders, and no environmental effects (wind, particles, atmospheric haze). Lighting is flat and basic.

**Level of Completion**
**BLOCKOUT**

**Defects and Distractions**
*   **Empty Zones:** The foreground is a massive, dead zone of flat green color that provides no visual interest or guidance.
*   **Lack of Definition:** No distinction between ground types (e.g., the transition from the green plane to the tan "concrete" area is a sharp, unrealistic edge).
*   **Geometry:** The "canopies" are single-plane thin, lacking any physical thickness.
*   **Composition:** The scale of the background monoliths is disconnected from the midground, creating a jarring sense of proportion.

**Inspection Limit**
The image stops rewarding closer inspection immediately. There is no secondary detail, no weathering, no architectural nuance, and no micro-geometry. Once the basic shapes are identified, there is nothing left to see.

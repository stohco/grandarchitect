# VISION INSPECTION — ep3.mount

## STAGE A (blind)
**REPORT: PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE EXAM**
**STAGE A — BLIND PLAYER READ**

**1. CONTENT SUMMARY**
*   **Setting:** A flat, open expanse containing primitive geometric structures.
*   **Objects:** Several thin-pillared wooden structures (resembling market stalls or lean-tos), three identical conical green shapes (trees), various brown spheres and cylinders (pots or crates), and several massive, featureless grey/blue rectangular monoliths.
*   **Scale:** The stalls suggest a human-scale environment, but the surrounding grey blocks are massive, skyscraper-sized volumes.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. Objects are sparse and separated by large tracts of empty, flat ground.

**2. PLAYER PERCEPTION**
A player would perceive this as a **technical test map** or a **pre-alpha greybox layout**. There is no narrative context or environmental storytelling. It looks like a "Marketplace" or "Town Square" layout being tested for scale before any art assets are applied.

**3. ART STYLE AND PRODUCTION QUALITY**
*   **Style:** Flat-shaded, untextured low-poly. There is no cohesive art direction beyond "functional geometry."
*   **Quality:** Extremely low/Developmental. Lighting is a single directional source with hard shadows. There is no ambient occlusion, no texture mapping, and no post-processing.

**4. LEVEL OF COMPLETION**
**BLOCKOUT**
The scene contains the basic spatial logic of a level (where buildings are, where the "floor" is) but lacks all visual finish, materials, and environmental detail.

**5. DEFECTS, DISTRACTIONS, AND EMPTY ZONES**
*   **Background Geometry:** The large triangular shape in the center-left background is a primitive, single-polygon "mountain" that looks like a placeholder or a geometry error.
*   **Foreground Void:** The bottom 30% of the frame is a featureless, dark green/black plane with no visual information.
*   **Monoliths:** The large grey blocks on the right and left are completely featureless, creating "dead zones" in the composition.
*   **Lighting/Shadows:** Shadows are pitch black and lack any light bounce, making the undersides of structures look like "voids."
*   **Skybox:** The sky is a flat, two-tone gradient with no clouds, stars, or atmospheric depth.

**6. INSPECTION LIMIT**
The image stops rewarding closer inspection almost immediately. Because there are no textures, decals, or fine-mesh details, zooming in only reveals the jagged edges of low-resolution polygons and flat color fills. There is no "micro" level to this environment.

# VISION INSPECTION — ep3.land

## STAGE A (blind)
**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE EXAMINER: STAGE A REPORT**

**1. Visual Content Summary**
*   **Setting:** An abstract, outdoor environment consisting of a flat, dark green ground plane and a two-tone blue sky/void.
*   **Objects:** Several rectangular prisms and flat platforms. Two primary structures sit on the left and right, consisting of tan blocks atop brown-textured bases. A single small tan cube sits in the center mid-ground. A faint, blue-tinted silhouette of a similar structure is visible in the far distance.
*   **Scale:** Indeterminate. Due to the lack of recognizable human-scale objects (doors, windows, foliage), the scene could represent a tabletop model or a series of massive buildings.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. The scene is composed of approximately 10–12 primitive geometric shapes.

**2. Player Impression**
A player would perceive this as a "Greybox" or a technical engine test. It lacks the visual language of a functional game world. There is no clear path, objective, or point of interest. It looks like a "Work in Progress" screen from a developer's first week of 3D environment construction.

**3. Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Primitive Geometric / Early Prototype.
*   **Quality:** Minimalist/Technical. Textures are basic tiling patterns (wood grain and vertical stripes) applied to primitive UV maps. There is no evidence of advanced shaders, ambient occlusion, or complex lighting.
*   **Lighting:** Flat and directional. There are no cast shadows on the ground plane, causing objects to appear as if they are floating or "pasted" onto the scene rather than existing within it.

**4. Apparent Level of Completion**
**PRIMITIVE_PLACEHOLDER**
The image lacks the structural logic required for a "Blockout" (which usually implies a playable layout). This is a collection of assets in a void.

**5. Defects, Distractions, and Empty Zones**
*   **Shadow Absence:** The total lack of contact shadows or drop shadows makes the spatial relationship between objects and the ground impossible to verify.
*   **Texture Stretching:** The wood-grain texture on the right-hand platform is stretched horizontally, showing a lack of proper UV scaling.
*   **Horizon Line:** The transition between the ground and the "fog" is a sharp, aliased line.
*   **Empty Zones:** Approximately 85% of the frame is empty space (the dark foreground and the featureless sky).
*   **Aliasing:** Jagged edges are visible on the silhouettes of the blocks against the sky.

**6. Inspection Reward Limit**
**Immediate.**
The image stops rewarding closer inspection the moment the basic shapes are identified. There are no hidden details, no material depth, and no environmental storytelling. Zooming in only reveals low-resolution texture tiling and pixelated edges.

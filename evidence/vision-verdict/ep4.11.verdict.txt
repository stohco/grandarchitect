## STAGE A (blind)
**REPORT: PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE EXAM (STAGE A)**

**1. Frame Content (Setting, Objects, Scale, Density)**
*   **Setting:** A featureless, void-like environment consisting of a flat, pale-green ground plane and a uniform, light-blue sky gradient.
*   **Objects:** Two objects are present. One is a massive, rounded, faceted grey mass occupying approximately 80% of the frame. The second is a tiny, low-poly grey rock sitting at the base of the larger mass.
*   **Scale:** Ambiguous. If the small rock is intended to be human-scale, the larger mass is a mountain. If the small rock is a pebble, the larger mass is a boulder. The lack of environmental cues (grass, trees, structures) makes scale impossible to verify.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. The scene contains only two meshes and two flat planes.

**2. Player Perception**
A player would perceive this as a **technical test environment** or a "greybox" level. They would identify the large object as a placeholder for a mountain or a large rock formation, and the small object as a scale reference or a prop test. There is no narrative or gameplay context visible.

**3. Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** None. This is raw engine output using diagnostic materials.
*   **Quality:** Non-existent. The large object uses a standard grey-on-grey checkerboard UV-mapping texture, typically used to check for texture stretching or density. The lighting is flat and ambient with no directional shadows for the primary mass.

**4. Apparent Level of Completion**
*   **PRIMITIVE_PLACEHOLDER.**
This is not a "world" or even a "blockout" of a level; it is a single-asset display in a void.

**5. Defects, Distractions, and Empty Zones**
*   **UV Checkerboard:** The most distracting element is the diagnostic texture, which signals "unfinished work" to any viewer.
*   **Visible Faceting:** The large mass has sharp, jagged edges along its silhouette, indicating a low polygon count without smoothing groups or high-fidelity modeling.
*   **Lack of Contact:** The large mass appears to sit on top of the ground plane with zero contact shadowing (Ambient Occlusion), making it look like it is floating or poorly composited.
*   **Empty Zones:** 100% of the background and ground plane are empty. There is no visual interest beyond the central objects.

**6. Inspection Reward Limit**
The image stops rewarding inspection **instantly**. There are no hidden details, micro-textures, or environmental storytelling. Closer inspection only reveals the pixelation of the checkerboard texture and the mathematical straightness of the low-poly edges.

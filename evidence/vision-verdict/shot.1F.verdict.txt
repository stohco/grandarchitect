# VISION INSPECTION — shot.1F

## STAGE A (blind)
**REPORT: PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE EXAM**
**STAGE: A — BLIND PLAYER READ**

**1. Frame Content (Setting, Objects, Scale, Density)**
*   **Setting:** A flat, outdoor environment with a void-like sky.
*   **Objects:** Two identical, open-sided rectangular structures on raised grey plinths. A series of flat, tan-colored planes representing paths or ground. Several green, double-coned primitive shapes. Massive, monolithic tan blocks with a repeating pattern dominate the background.
*   **Scale:** Human-scale architecture in the midground, dwarfed by massive, featureless background geometry.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. The scene consists of approximately 15–20 primitive geometric volumes.

**2. Player Perception**
*   A player would perceive this as a "Greybox" or "Blockout" phase of a level. It looks like a technical test for scale or a very early architectural layout for a park or pavilion area. There is no narrative or gameplay context visible.

**3. Apparent Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Primitive 3D / Low-Poly.
*   **Quality:** Very low. Textures are low-resolution and tile poorly. There is no evidence of a lighting engine, ambient occlusion, or shadows. Edges are perfectly sharp with no beveling. The "trees" are the most basic possible representation of foliage.

**4. Apparent Level of Completion**
*   **BLOCKOUT.** This is a functional arrangement of shapes to test spatial relationships. It contains no final assets, no environmental effects, and no polished materials.

**5. Obvious Defects, Distractions, and Empty Zones**
*   **Texture Tiling:** The background blocks show severe, distracting repetition of a small texture pattern.
*   **Texture Stretching:** The tan "wood" texture on the ground planes is stretched horizontally, losing its intended aspect ratio.
*   **Floating Geometry:** The structures appear to sit on top of the ground without any transition or "grounding" shadows, making them look like they are hovering.
*   **Visible Seams:** Clear lines are visible where the ground planes meet.
*   **Empty Zones:** 80% of the frame is empty space (flat ground or flat sky).
*   **Lack of Depth:** The absence of shadows or fog makes it difficult to judge the distance of the large background blocks.

**6. Inspection Reward Limit**
*   **Immediate.** The image stops rewarding inspection the moment the basic shapes are identified. There are no micro-details, no hidden elements, and no material depth to discover. Closer inspection only reveals the low resolution of the textures and the simplicity of the geometry.

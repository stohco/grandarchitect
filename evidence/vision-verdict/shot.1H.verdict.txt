## STAGE A (blind)
**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE REPORT**

**1. Frame Content**
*   **Setting:** An enclosed interior corner consisting of two white walls, a dark ceiling, and a tan/brown floor.
*   **Objects:** Two low-poly octagonal cylinders (stools), one rectangular orange/peach block (bench/platform), a single cluster of thin green vertical polygons (grass/plant), and one brown rectangular beam protruding from the ceiling.
*   **Scale:** Human-scale room, though proportions are abstract.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. The scene is approximately 95% empty space.

**2. Player Perception**
A player would perceive this as a "test room" or a "void" environment. It resembles a primitive representation of a room—perhaps a house or a shop—but lacks any functional or narrative signifiers. The objects appear to be floating or loosely placed rather than integrated into a lived-in space.

**3. Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Primitive Low-Poly / Grey-box.
*   **Quality:** Minimal. There is no evidence of advanced lighting (global illumination, ambient occlusion), complex shaders, or high-resolution texturing. The geometry is basic and the color palette is flat.

**4. Apparent Level of Completion**
**PRIMITIVE_PLACEHOLDER**
This is below the level of a "Blockout," as it lacks the structural intentionality of a level design layout. It appears to be a technical test for asset placement or basic engine rendering.

**5. Defects, Distractions, and Empty Zones**
*   **Lighting/Texture Errors:** The floor features a harsh diagonal seam or lighting gradient that does not align with the geometry or any visible light source.
*   **Clipping:** The green plant-like object is clipping directly through the floor plane with no base, pot, or transition.
*   **Lack of Contact:** Objects (stools, bench) have no contact shadows or ambient occlusion, making them appear to "float" on the floor surface.
*   **Geometry:** The ceiling beam terminates abruptly into the wall without any structural support or trim.
*   **Empty Zones:** The walls and ceiling are completely featureless, providing no visual information.

**6. Inspection Reward Limit**
Inspection stops rewarding the viewer almost instantly. There are no micro-details, hidden textures, or subtle environmental storytelling elements. Closer inspection only reveals the aliasing on the polygon edges and the low-resolution "muddy" quality of the floor texture.

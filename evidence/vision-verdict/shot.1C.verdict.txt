## STAGE A (blind)
**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE EXAMINER: STAGE A REPORT**

**1. PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION**
*   **Setting:** A flat, outdoor environment consisting of a wide, tan-colored path or road bisecting a green plane.
*   **Objects:** 
    *   Two large, rectangular structures on the left/center composed of primitive boxes with a horizontal wood-grain texture.
    *   A central grey hexagonal base supporting a vertical cylinder and a floating brown wedge (resembling a primitive well or fountain).
    *   Several identical "trees" consisting of a brown rectangular trunk and a dark green cone.
    *   Distant white and light-blue triangles representing mountains.
*   **Scale:** Human-eye level perspective. The structures are roughly 2–3 times the height of the camera.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. Large expanses of the frame contain only flat, unshaded color or single-tiling textures.

**2. PLAYER PERCEPTION**
A player would perceive this as a **very early technical prototype** of a rural or "Wild West" town. The wood textures and dirt road suggest a frontier setting, but the lack of any architectural detail (windows, doors, roofs) makes the environment feel like a non-functional geometry test.

**3. ART STYLE AND PRODUCTION QUALITY**
*   **Style:** Primitive 3D / Low-Fidelity. It utilizes basic geometric primitives (cubes, cones, cylinders) with low-resolution, repeating textures.
*   **Quality:** Extremely low. There is no evidence of a lighting engine; surfaces are flat-lit with no shadows, ambient occlusion, or highlights. Aliasing (jagged edges) is prominent on all diagonal lines.

**4. LEVEL OF COMPLETION**
**PRIMITIVE_PLACEHOLDER**
This is below the level of a "Blockout." While a blockout focuses on spatial flow using grey boxes, this uses "final-intent" textures (wood, grass) on unedited primitive shapes, indicating a stage where basic asset placement is being tested without regard for lighting, composition, or geometry.

**5. DEFECTS, DISTRACTIONS, AND EMPTY ZONES**
*   **Zero Lighting:** The absence of shadows makes every object appear to "float" on top of the ground plane rather than sit within it.
*   **Texture Seams:** A prominent diagonal distortion/seam is visible in the bottom-right quadrant of the road texture.
*   **Skybox:** The sky is a flat, two-tone gradient with a sharp, unnatural transition.
*   **Floating Geometry:** The brown element on the central "well" is not physically connected to the base.
*   **Empty Zones:** Approximately 70% of the frame is empty space (flat ground or flat sky).
*   **Lack of Depth:** The "mountains" are 2D-style triangles that do not convey atmospheric perspective or volume.

**6. INSPECTION REWARD LIMIT**
**Surface Level.**
The image stops rewarding inspection almost immediately. There are no fine details, no hidden geometry, and no material properties to observe. Closer inspection only reveals the low resolution of the tiling textures and the mathematical sharpness of the un-beveled polygon edges.

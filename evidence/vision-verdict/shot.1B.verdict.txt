**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE EXAM: STAGE A**

**1. Frame Content (Setting, Objects, Scale, Density)**
*   **Setting:** A featureless, flat green plane bisected by a single diagonal brown strip (presumably a road). The horizon is obscured by a thick, uniform grey-blue fog.
*   **Objects:** A single, central vertical structure composed of primitive rectangular blocks. It features a tall tan textured slab, grey vertical pillars, and horizontal crossbars. In the far distance, faint silhouettes of blocky shapes (possibly houses or trees) are visible through the fog.
*   **Scale:** Indeterminate. The "road" suggests a human scale, but the lack of environmental context (grass blades, debris, or recognizable architectural standards) makes the central object appear like a small toy on a tabletop or a massive monument in a void.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. Approximately 90% of the frame consists of empty space (flat ground, flat sky, or uniform fog).

**2. Player Perception**
*   A player would perceive this as a **technical test** or a **"Hello World" environment** for a game engine. 
*   The central object is unrecognizable; it lacks the silhouette of a functional building or a living creature. It would likely be interpreted as a "placeholder" or a "test asset" meant to be replaced by a real model.

**3. Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Art Style:** Primitive/Programmer Art. There is no cohesive aesthetic beyond the use of basic geometric primitives and low-resolution tiling textures.
*   **Production Quality:** Minimalist/Technical. Lighting is a single-source directional light with a hard-edged shadow. There is no ambient occlusion, global illumination, or post-processing beyond a basic distance fog.

**4. Apparent Level of Completion**
*   **PRIMITIVE_PLACEHOLDER.**
*   This is not a world; it is a coordinate system with a few meshes dropped in to verify rendering.

**5. Defects, Distractions, and Empty Zones**
*   **Texture Tiling:** The "road" texture shows obvious, repetitive linear banding.
*   **Geometry:** The central object has visible gaps between blocks and lacks any structural logic.
*   **Empty Zones:** The green "ground" is a single hex-code color with zero texture or detail. The sky is a flat gradient.
*   **Fog Clipping:** The distant objects are being aggressively culled by the fog, creating a "wall" effect rather than a natural atmosphere.
*   **Shadows:** The shadow cast by the central object is a sharp, aliased black shape that does not account for the diffused light suggested by the heavy fog.

**6. Reward for Closer Inspection**
*   **Zero.** 
*   Closer inspection only reveals the low resolution of the textures (specifically the "wicker" or "brick" pattern on the tan block) and the lack of connectivity between the primitive shapes. There are no hidden details, environmental storytelling, or micro-level polish.

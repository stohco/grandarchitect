**PERCEPTUAL EVIDENCE REPORT: STAGE A**

**Actual Content**
*   **Setting:** A flat, featureless green plane bisected by a straight, tan-colored path/road extending toward a foggy horizon.
*   **Objects:** A single central structure consisting of a tall, yellow-textured rectangular prism supported by a grey frame with brown capping. In the far distance, obscured by fog, are several white geometric blocks and three conical shapes.
*   **Scale:** Ambiguous. The road suggests a human-scale environment, but the lack of reference entities (characters, vehicles, foliage) makes the actual size of the central structure impossible to determine.
*   **Density:** Extremely low. One primary asset in a void.

**Player Perception**
A player would perceive this as a technical test environment, a "greybox" level, or a very early alpha build. There is no narrative or environmental storytelling present; it reads as a functional test of a specific asset or a pathing route.

**Art Style and Production Quality**
*   **Style:** Low-poly / Primitive.
*   **Quality:** Very low. There is a total absence of dynamic lighting, ambient occlusion, or cast shadows, causing objects to appear as if they are floating on the surface. Textures are basic; the yellow pillar has a grainy noise pattern, while the road and ground are flat colors/gradients.

**Completion Level**
**BLOCKOUT**

**Defects and Distractions**
*   **Lighting:** No shadows. The lack of contact shadows between the structure and the ground destroys the sense of weight and placement.
*   **Environment:** The ground is a perfectly flat, textureless plane.
*   **Atmospherics:** Heavy fog is being used as a "curtain" to hide the lack of a horizon or background assets.
*   **Geometry:** The distant "trees" and "buildings" are primitive cones and cubes.

**Inspection Limit**
The image stops rewarding inspection immediately upon identifying the central structure. There are no micro-details, hidden elements, or material complexities to investigate. Once the basic geometry is noted, there is no further visual information to extract.
